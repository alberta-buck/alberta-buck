* Findings

1. The pseudo-stabilizer was never fed.  =grep -rn setShadowOffset alberta_buck/=
   found no caller: WP-3a's sim-only offset existed but the
   UndertakingAgent / FacilityAgent never booked into it, so every
   shadow cell so far (including session 2's integration smokes with
   UndertakingAgent=1, FacilityAgent=2) fed K the desk's book only.
   =shadow_book.py= now books =ut_absorbed_open - ut_issued_open -
   fac_drawn= (absorbed positive, issued negative) whenever it changes.
2. On the calm none-arm the undertakings at the default band never
   trade in 30 days (s30 / v30: =ut_trades= 0, bvib within +/-2% after
   the day-0 bootstrap transient of 1.0635), so "UndertakingAgent=1"
   alone does not put a position in front of K; =eps=0.005, p=1.0= does
   (8 trades, 57.6k issued / 15.2k absorbed).  At the smoke gains the
   loop's contribution is tiny (|uQ| <= 4e-5, |uQI| <= 8e-5 K units).
3. S and V units on the same books (s30e vs v30e, identical histories
   up to K's fourth decimal): s_S = q / D = -0.0023 (D ~ 10.4M BUCK of
   pool reserve); s_V = (w_off * q / cap_off + w_desk * 0) / (w_off +
   w_desk) = -0.0012 -- the desk, included at weight 1 with an empty
   book, halves the level's fill.  That is the normalization D7
   specifies; whether an idle stabilizer should dilute the aggregate is
   decision 11's weight question (open issue 4).
4. A one-day booking lag, fixed in =671e298=.  Agents run =compute()=
   themselves (=equilibrium_agents.py:1490=, =driver.py:181=); when one
   does so inside the last tick, the loop's end-of-day =compute()= is a
   cached read (dT 1800 s not elapsed), so a booking made just before
   it reached K on the NEXT day (s0-5 before the fix: day 1 s = +0.00286
   with =pid_q= 0).  Booking at each tick start (no tx unless the sum
   changed) puts the previous tick's books in front of every cycle; the
   same mechanism explains why =sh_desk_cap= (the cap as of the last
   real cycle) can differ from =sh_cap= (live) by ~0.3% on some frames,
   exactly as =pid_*= are as of that cycle.
5. The S0 identity and ppm rounding.  =err= and =sErr= are each
   truncated to ppm, so e + r equals the composite's error only when
   bvib and s round identically at the ppm boundary; the forge test uses
   multiples of 1e12 and is exact.  With Kd = Kqd != 0 the derivative
   rounds once per term instead of once on the sum: within 2 wei.  At
   a rail the per-integrator anti-windup can accept one increment the
   composite would reject (e and r of opposite sign): interior-exact,
   rail-approximate, as D7 states.
6. No D4-as-built twin exists in the tree any more (the composite
   =_readReferences= is retired by decision 10), so the WP-13 row's "S0
   reproduces D4 on a 5-d smoke" is evidenced by the forge S0 tests
   (the composite is a Direct instance on a mock returning bvib +
   lambda q / D) and, on a live cell, by the attribution identity K ==
   K0 + sum of the six terms on every frame (s0-5).
7. Cost of the shell's addition: =positionCap()= + =NavUnreadable= =
   +114 B (21,265 -> 21,379); the holding logic (struct fields, refresh,
   flags, per-stabilizer views) lives in the observer (+3,035 B) and the

8. The worktree lacks =build/snark=, so =make nix-test= skips

* Open issues and proposed decisions

1. The position loop's sign convention.  Implemented as a PID on the
   position ERROR r = 0 - s (stored in =S=, ppm, like =P= = 1 - bvib),
   so positive gains give D4's signs (absorbed lowers K) and S0 is
   literally (Kq, Kqi, Kqd) = (Kp, Ki, Kd).  The brief's "output = ...
   + Kq*s" reads the same with s the stored error.  Proposed: keep it
   (both loops are errors with setpoints par and zero); the alternative
   (store s, subtract in the law) flips the anti-windup rule's sense.
2. Exclusion applies under S too.  A stabilizer with held cap 0
   (disabled, or never read) drops out of s_S as well as s_V, and
   =shadowValueInBuck()= (bvib + s_S) follows.  WP-3a's composite
   included every lambda > 0 stabilizer regardless of its bounds; D7 /
   decision 9 say "excluded from the aggregate" without qualifying the
   mode.  Proposed: as implemented (a book that cannot move is not a
   lever in either units); the pseudo-stabilizer stays always-included
   under S (it has no NAV) and needs =shadowCap= > 0 under V.
3. Registry defaults.  =addStabilizer(s, lambda)= keeps its WP-3a
   signature and starts the V weight at 1e18 (the desk's carry class);
   the pseudo-stabilizer's =shadowWeight= starts at 0 and =deploy.py=
   sets it (default 1 per decision 11).  =addStabilizer= also attempts
   the first cap read at once (so an enabled, readable stabilizer is
   included from registration) and rejects codeless addresses
