* Design notes (what the change is, and its limits)

- WHY balances lie.  A V3 pool's balance is the sum over every position
  of what its range holds at the current price, plus uncollected LP and
  protocol fees.  Only a full-range position holds the constant-product
  ratio; a concentrated position adds its funding asset without moving
  the price.  The seeder's USDC range on day 1: balances 1.410, slot0
  1.083.
- WHAT replaces them.  Inside the active tick range the pool IS a
  constant-product market on x = L 2^96 / sqrtP, y = L sqrtP / 2^96
  (x y = L^2, y / x = price).  =_cp_out=, =_impact_cap=,
  =_amount_in_for_out= and =quote_hop= are exact on (x, y) for a swap
  that stays inside the range, so the change is of INPUT, not of form:
  every site keeps its lines and reads (ru, rb) from =active_reserves=.
- THE APPROXIMATION, named.  A swap that crosses a tick boundary meets a
  different L beyond it.  Crossing INTO more liquidity (a corrective
  swap entering the seeder's adjacent range) fills better than quoted,
  so the ex-ante gate errs conservative; crossing OUT of a concentrated
  range into thinner liquidity fills worse than quoted.  The balance
  quote silently made the same within-range assumption with the wrong
  reserves.  An exact multi-tick quote is the on-chain Quoter, not
  taken here: no such path exists in the sim, and the reads would be
  eth_calls per candidate route per tick.
- ORIENTATION.  token0 is the lower address; the sim's BUCK/USDC pool has
  USDC as token0, so a BUCK rally is a FALLING pool price.  =buck_usd6=
  and =active_reserves= take the pool's token0 from the memo, so every
  caller keeps writing (r_usdc, r_buck); test_gauge.py pins both orders.
- MEMO.  =Chain.pool_state= is invalidated wherever the balance cache is,
  so a quote costs no more than the reads it replaced; the cells ran
  FASTER (none 2m38 -> 2m30, seeder 2m44 -> 2m13 wall), partly fewer
  phantom trades.  A bare Web3Session falls back to an uncached read.
  =notes_stack.py= sends through =w3.eth.send_transaction= without
  clearing the balance cache; the memo shares that pre-existing exposure
  (not on any sim cell's path).

Cause: with only full-range positions the virtual reserves equal the
balances less the uncollected fees (0.05% of BUCK/USDC volume, 0.30% of

state drifts, and a direct-mint entry on day 3 is sized off that drifted
state.  The divergence stays at rounding level for 30 days: K within
1.7e-4, bvib within 1.3e-3, three fewer arb cycles.  The design owner's
"rounding-level drift expected" holds; the 1-year identity pair
(readiness table, 2 x 1-year) should be judged with =vecdiff= beside
=cmp= rather than by =cmp= alone.

* Findings

1. The artefact's channel was the excursion-credit arbs' ex-ante gate,
   not the named readers alone.  =ExcursionArbAgent= (5 + 3 in the
   catalogue cast) gates entries on =_cp_out(ru, rb, ...)= over the pool
   balances; with the seeder's USD 15M in the USDC balance the "sell
   BUCK above par + edge" test passed trivially and the credit variant
   minted and sold: 17 entries and +5.8M supply by day 29 in the seeder
   world (=vecdiff none-before seeder-before=), which is WP-8's "+5M
   supply, K 0.787 vs 0.746".  After: 8 entries, and each passed a gate
   that reads the pool's active depth.  Command: the vecdiff lines in L3
   / acceptance 1.
2. The balance/slot0 gap is not fee-sized only when a position is
   concentrated -- it is signed by the position's conversion.  In the
   seeder cell the gap is +28% at placement (USDC beyond the price),
   crosses zero on day 4 and settles at -17% (the range converted to
   BUCK as the corrective flow arrived).  =buck_usd_bal= makes this
   visible in every vector; in the none cell it stays under 6e-6.
   Command: =d8_tables.py= over =seeder-after2.json= (the table in
   acceptance 2).
3. The L3 drift is rounding-level and slow: 30 days of the none cell
   move K by at most 1.7e-4 and bvib by at most 1.3e-3, and the first
   changed action COUNT is on day 25.  Byte-identity cannot be the
   acceptance for a change that removes a fee-sized bias from every
   quote; =vecdiff= is the instrument.  Command: acceptance 1.
4. The cells run faster: none 2m38 -> 2m30, seeder 2m44 -> 2m13.  The
   memo makes a pool read as cheap as a cached balanceOf, and the seeder
   world executes 583 fewer phantom arb cycles.  Command: the =time=
   lines in =build/sim/smoke/d8/*.log=.
5. With the seeder on, the anonymous arbs cycle LESS through BUCK/USDC
   than with it off (ubTrades 539 vs 1107; cycleTrades 957 vs 1434),
   the reverse of before (1165 vs 1110).  The routes C2 / C3 need a
   BUCK/USDC mispricing against the TOKEN pools; the seeder's bid
