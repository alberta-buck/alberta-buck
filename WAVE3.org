* Principles

1. *Proof of concept in Python agents over the real contracts.*  The
   catalogue already runs the deployed Solidity stack in-process
   (pyrevm) with Python agents.  An agent holding the right AUTHORITY
   (SimLP's V3 callback proxy and a zero-premium BuckCredit face) can
   stand in for a contract feature at the economics level.  So the
   undertakings, the ladder, the facility, the seeder and the perp are
   agents first.  The eventual Solidity must reproduce the agent's
   economics, judged by the SAME cells -- "the Solidity contracts are
   the mathematical specification" (=alberta-buck-platform.org=) cuts
   both ways: the agent is the executable requirement the contract is
   later held to.
2. *Solidity early only where K's specification demands it.*  K reads
   =basket.basketValueInBuck()= on-chain; a Python-side PID pushing K
   through =BuckKControllerStatic.setBuckK= would re-implement the
   controller's anti-windup, dT and priming semantics and drift from the
   spec.  The shadow controller is therefore a SMALL Solidity variant
   (WP-3a), with a sim-only setter so agent-booked inventory can feed it
   before the undertakings are contract-level.  The stress fee (WP-5)
   is the other candidate for early Solidity, because =_redeem= is the
   best-tested path in the basket and the change is local.
3. *Every requirement has a gate.*  The gates are those of
   =CARRY-CONVEXITY.org= section 8.2 plus the predictions of 7.9; a work
   package is DONE when its cells pass its gate, not when its code runs.
4. *Determinism.*  Keyed RNG (=[scenario] rng = "keyed"=); a re-run of a
   cell is byte-identical; new agents draw from their own keyed stream
   and never perturb another class's history (=TELEMETRY.md=).
5. *Telemetry in the same commit as the agent* (=alberta-buck-operations.org=,
   "the agent is invisible"): counters into =snapshot.py= with the
   agent; per-agent inventory through =arb_state=; several seeds before
   any peak-damping number is quoted.
6. *Vectors are not committed* (25-30 MB each); summaries, images and
   this ledger are the record; any cell is regenerable from its TOML
   plus =--set= overrides, which the vector's =meta= embeds.
7. *One work package, one branch, one owner, its own files.*  Shared
   files (=catalogue.py= MIXES, =loop.py='s registration import,
   =snapshot.py= counters, =WhaleRaidAgent='s knobs) take APPEND-ONLY
   blocks marked =# WP-n=; rebase on =feature/carry-convexity= before
   merging; the integrator resolves the rest.
8. *House style*: ASCII in docs and commit messages; no attribution
   lines; =make nix-venv-*= for Python, =make nix-*= for forge; never
   bare =forge build=.

** Signs and units (repeat of the record, because they are the silent killers)

- bvib > 1: BUCK cheap (discount); K falls.  bvib < 1: BUCK dear
  (premium); K rises.
- Shadow inventory: absorbed (weak side, facility retire) POSITIVE;
  issued (strong side, facility issue) NEGATIVE.  bvib_shadow = bvib +
  lambda * I / D.
- bvib is BUCK PER BASKET: TOKEN -> BUCK multiplies, BUCK -> TOKEN
  divides (=alberta-buck-operations.org=, "units and orientation").
- Pool orientation: a BUCK rally is a FALLING pool price when BUCK is
  token1.  =BuckPoolInvestorAgent= has the checked logic; reuse it.
- BUCK and USDC are 6-dec in the sim; =basketValueInBuck= is 18-dec.

* The star method: one axis at a time from a baseline

(Design owner, 2026-09-02.)  The full grid of every controllable axis at
every value is unrenderable past two or three axes.  Instead: pick a few
BEST scenarios as baselines; for each baseline vary EACH axis lower and
higher while holding every other axis at the baseline; render one
chosen PANE (the K pane, the depositor-ROI pane, the bvib pane, the
markout pane, ...) per axis with lo / base / hi overlaid; read the
sensitivity table; move the baseline along the axes that improved the
objective; repeat.  A star, re-centred each cycle, searches the space
for its sweet spots organically over a few cycles.

** Definitions

- *Baseline*: a complete experiment TOML (physics, cast, facilities,
  injections) -- a scenario of record.  Several may exist.
- *Axis*: one controllable knob, as a dotted =--set= key, with its
  baseline value and a lo / hi value (or a multiplicative step); an
  axis may also be a TAG axis (=--tag=) for knobs that live outside
  =--set= (e.g. the controller variant).
- *Star*: for each arm in the star's arm list (typically =none= plus
  one or two injections), the baseline cell plus lo and hi cells per
  axis: 1 + 2A cells per arm.  A cells x A axes x 3 arms, ~A=10, is
  ~63 cells -- a night on a MATRIX_JOBS machine.
- *Objective*: declared per star, e.g. "minimize the x4 squeeze AUC
  subject to G1-G8 PASS and depositor realized ROI >= baseline".  The
  driver does not optimize; it reports, and the design owner moves the
  baseline.
- *Pane*: one time series per vector rendered per axis with lo / base /
  hi overlaid, one row per arm: =k= (buckK), =bvib=, =dm_roi= (depositor
  realized P&L), =nav_bsk= (basket NAV in baskets), =carry= (the
  markout ledger's basket netlp), =whale= (raid P&L), =supply=,
  =exc_pnl= (defender P&L).
- *Cycle*: run the star; write the sensitivity table (per axis: the
  metrics at lo / base / hi and the deltas); pick the new baseline;
  commit the star spec and its summary as the cycle's record
  (=experiments/stars/<name>-c<k>.toml=, =build/sim/star/<name>-c<k>/=,
  =images/star-<name>-c<k>-<pane>.png=).

** Caveat, and its mitigation

One-axis-at-a-time misses interactions.  Mitigations: re-centre every
cycle (coordinate ascent finds interacting optima over cycles); run the
PAIR cell of the two most sensitive axes each cycle (four cells); and
run a small full factorial on any two axes whose pair cell disagrees
with the sum of their single effects.  Multi-seed the final baseline.

** The two comprehensibility gates (R14)

- *G7 -- organic equivalence.*  On the organic arms (=none=, the x1
  grinds, the endog/portcast cast) a mechanism under test must leave
  K's trajectory within tolerance of the baseline controller's: max
  |K_test(t) - K_base(t)| <= 0.02 and RMS <= 0.01 over the run.  The
  fast books are near zero in organic regimes, so a mechanism that
  moves K there is acting on something other than an excursion.
- *G8 -- K comprehensibility.*  The =kfc= panel (=eqmetrics.k_forecast=)
  asks three things an observer can check against public state.  (i)
  K OBEYS ITS LAW: day to day dK ~ Kp d(1 - bvib), over a month dK ~ Ki
  mean(1 - bvib) 30; the fitted slopes must be within 25% of the
  designed gains with R^2 >= 0.8 for the monthly law, and the EX-POST
  direction hit (sign of dK_30 == sign of the month's mean deviation,
  on months with |mean| > 0.25%) >= 80%.  (ii) K is CALM in organic
  regimes: persistence within one point on >= 90% of 30-day windows.
  (iii) the ex-ante observer forecast (K + Kp (1 - bvib) + Ki h x the
  trailing mean deviation) is reported beside the naive law and
  persistence -- measured 2026-09-02, persistence wins at 7 and 30 days
  because excursions revert inside a week and the P kick unwinds with
  them.  That is not a defect of the law; it is the P term being
  visible at short horizons, and whether an explainable K should carry
  a P term at all is decisions pending 7.  When the observer exists
  (WP-3a) the same three checks run with its inputs, and the gap is the
  price of the extra inputs, reported per cell.

   2026-09-03 (design owner, with D7): NEITHER -- hold the last good
   cap.  The mechanics, recorded because the failure mode is subtle:

   - WHAT "unreadable" means.  =BuckBasketOps.capacity()= sizes both
     desk bounds against NAV = 2 x B from =poolBuckValues()=, through
     =_navBuckSafe()=, which try/catches the read and returns 0 when
     it reverts.  A zero NAV makes both caps zero, =_room()= returns 0,
     capacity() = 0 and saturation() = 1e18: "pinned".
     =netInventory()= never touches NAV (it is =monetaryBuckHeld -
     max(monetaryOutstanding, 0)=), so the POSITION is always
     readable; only its normalization is not.
   - WHEN it happens.  (a) The desk is disabled (=opsParams.enabled=
     false): pre-bootstrap, and whenever governance switches the desk
     off; capacity() returns 0 before any read.  (b) =NoValue()=: no
     constituent has depositor liquidity beyond the treasury's
     (=totalL <= treasuryLiquidity= in every pool), an empty basket,
     or a pool holding no BUCK -- launch, and a fully redeemed basket.
     (c) =Slippage()=: for ANY one constituent, spot is off its TWAP by
     more than =defaultMaxDeviationBp= (500 bp in the sim deploy; the
     guard is skipped when it is 0) inside =twapWindow= (600 s in the
     sim).  This is the guard that protects depositor redemptions from
     a manipulated spot, and on the controller's path it turns "a fast
     move in one pool" into "no NAV" for the duration of the window --
     exactly the raid days.  (d) A cold pool WITHOUT TWAP history does
     NOT trip it: =peekTwap= reverts, the catch returns, the guard is
     skipped.  (e) A bad pool address or a reverting token is a
     governance error, not an operating condition.

     a bound; it is in the middle of the move it exists to absorb.
     Reporting saturation 1 there made gamma an inadvertent schedule
     on PRICE VELOCITY (spot vs TWAP) -- a D-type kick keyed to the one

     boosted Ki while the error was still ~0, so the boost landed on
     noise.  Reporting idle (0) was also wrong: a desk that IS at a
     bound when the guard trips would have reported free.

     pool's TWAP with a spot fallback and never runs the guard, so K's
     own process variable never reverts; only the desk's cap does.
     The asymmetry is deliberate (a view on K's path must not take
     =compute()= down) and stays.

     derived bound from the last successful read, refreshed on every
     successful =capacity()= and on every desk operation) and its age.

     cannot act), but the observer EXCLUDES a disabled stabilizer from
     the aggregate position and renormalizes the weights, because a
     book that cannot move is not a lever K should mid-range.  Before
     the first successful read (launch) lastGoodCap is 0 and the
     stabilizer reports position 0, capacity 0, saturation 1, stale --
