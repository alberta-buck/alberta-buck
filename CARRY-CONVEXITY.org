* Purpose and lineage

This document joins the excursion-catalogue program (=REBALANCING-EQ.org=)
with an external critique: Panoptic's "Yin (Carry) & Yang (Convexity)
Require Each Other to Survive & Thrive" (X article, 2026-08-27; saved
copies =~/Downloads/Panoptic-carry.{pdf,html}=), itself the onchain
transplant of the Artemis/Cole carry-vs-convexity framing.  The critique,
stated in our terms:

  A passive AMM LP is a carry trader -- a seller of perpetual optionality
  who collects a fixed fee (the implied volatility) while eating adverse
  selection (the realized volatility).  Onchain, the structural oversupply
  of LP positions suppresses IV below RV, and crypto is the most
  momentum-prone, fat-tailed asset class -- so the profitable side is
  BUYING convexity from LPs, not being one.  Carry books show negative
  skew ("sawtooth" P&L: steady fees, rare wipeouts); convexity books show
  positive skew ("stairstep").  A market is complete and stable only when
  both sides exist and both are PRICED.

The critique matters here because the BuckBasket is not merely *an* LP --
it is the SOLE full-range LP in every TOKEN/BUCK constituent pool
(=alberta_buck/sim/deploy.py= pool_meta; =BuckBasketUniswapV3.sol=), its
depositor-facing balance sheet IS that LP book, and =basketValueInBuck=
(the controller's process variable) is read from those same pools.  Every
arb, whale slice and excursion trade in a constituent pool has the basket
(depositor L + treasury L) as counterparty.  There is no third party
absorbing adverse selection.

Three goals organize the program (decisions of intent, not yet of record):

1. *Harvest the benign workforce.*  The BUCK-unaware arbs (AnonymousArb,
   TokenAccumulator lineage) repin the TOKEN/BUCK pools to the world for
   free and pay the basket fees to do it.  Their flow must STAY cheap --
   the fee is "the gate that decides how small a mispricing an external
   arb will bother to close" (=deploy.py= fee note) -- while the basket
   captures it as carry.
2. *Detect adverse events and price them.*  Toxic flow (informed,
   manipulative, raid-speed) must pay a higher price for the convexity it
   consumes, detected from observables (markout, tick RV, |bvib-1|, size
   vs impact norms) and priced via state-dependent fees and spreads.
3. *Position ahead of the system's own enforcement.*  K's trajectory is
   committed, slow and public (integral-dominant PID, tau_I ~90 d).  The
   basket can legitimately place inventory, ranges and fees aligned with
   what the controller will enforce -- the common-mode analogue of the
   fs30 foresight result, available to the system for free.

** Where the critique does NOT land: manufactured reversion

Panoptic's RV > IV inversion is an empirical claim about UNANCHORED,
momentum-prone assets.  The bvib axis is engineered to mean-revert: K
plus the poised cast manufacture the reversion that a TradFi option
seller merely bets on.  The catalogue proved it: attacker P&L is
superlinearly negative (-$0.87M / -$3.06M / -$9.13M at x1/x2/x4
squeezes), defenders are paid countercyclically (finding 8), and the
excursion agents' ~min_edge~ gate means they refuse to sell convexity
below a 1% floor premium -- priced optionality, not passive LPing.

So the two axes split cleanly:

| axis                        | vol regime                  | correct posture                              |
|-----------------------------+-----------------------------+----------------------------------------------|
| common mode (BUCK vs basket) | manufactured reversion      | SELL convexity -- but priced, never for free |
| differential (TOKEN i vs j)  | genuinely trending (macro)  | patience (director), paid foresight, hedges  |

The prescription is not "stop being the carry side."  It is: price the
convexity currently given away (goals 1-2), route carry income to the
standing convexity that defends the system, and buy the convexity the
system cannot manufacture (the trending satellites).  The insurance
company that also owns the fire department should charge premiums, pay
its firefighters a retainer, and reinsure the one block it cannot protect.

* Wave 3 instrument: the markout / LVR ledger

The catalogue's rule applies: no new machinery before the falsifiable
instrument.  Wave 3's instrument is a per-pool, per-counterparty-class
markout ledger.

** Definitions

For each swap =s= in constituent pool =i= at time =t=, with the basket-LP
side's signed base-token flow =q_lp= and effective execution price
=p_exec=:

  markout_D(s) = q_lp x (p_ref(t + D) - p_exec(t))

Negative markout: the basket sold what was about to appreciate (or bought
what was about to fall) -- adverse selection, realized.  Three reference
prices, in order of increasing externality:

- (a) own-pool TWAP at =t + D= -- cheap, self-referential;
- (b) truth-implied TOKEN/BUCK = (TOKEN/USDC) / (BUCK/USDC) from the deep
  truth pools -- the world's opinion, the one the whale cannot cheaply
  move; the default reference;
- (c) the exogenous reference series itself -- the sim's oracle truth,
  the fs30 analogue; sim-only, bounds what any detector could achieve.

Horizons =D= in {next tick, 1 d, 5 d}, matching the dual-timescale
excursion signal.  Aggregates per pool per window:

  fees_i        = fee income accrued to the basket position
  adverse_i     = sum of markout_D over swaps (signed)
  netlp_i       = fees_i + adverse_i          (the LP's true P&L vs hold)
  lvr_bench_i   = integral of (sigma_i^2 / 8) x poolValue_i dt
                  (analytic LVR from realized tick variance)
  carry_ratio_i = fees_i / lvr_bench_i        (the IV-vs-RV verdict)

** Attribution: the sim's unique advantage

Every sim swap has a known sender class (keyed RNG, fixed roster), so the
ledger splits per class x pool x window: whale (accumulate / dump /
re-buy phases separately), BUCK-unaware arbs, director keeper, excursion
agents by base, DM depositors, debtors.  Toxicity of a class = mean
markout per dollar of its flow.  Expected shape, which the instrument
must confirm or refute:

- whale dump-day flow: deeply negative for the basket (the attack IS
  concentrated adverse selection);
- benign arbs: mildly negative gross, ~zero net of fee (they sell the
  basket exactly the rebalancing it wants -- Shannon's demon's other
  half);
- director keeper: the basket trading against its own book (internal
  transfer; must net to ~fees);
- excursion agents: negative for the pool ledger BY DESIGN (they trade
  only at >= 1% edge) -- the defense is partly funded by the basket's own
  LP book, a transfer the system-level P&L hides and this ledger exposes.

** A. Static fee retune (V3, no new machinery)

Let wave 3a's carry_ratio pick per-pool static tiers: plausibly
cbBTC/BUCK and PAXG/BUCK up (trending, fat-tailed -- the underpriced

pool for all regimes; the one-day squeeze stays unpriced; known to be a
compromise (=deploy.py= three-way fee tension note).

** B. Uniswap v4 hooked pools: dynamic fees

V4 pools may declare a dynamic-fee flag; a hook contract sets the fee
per-swap (beforeSwap), with afterSwap available for accounting.  The
basket already owns, bootstraps and registers its pools, so migrating a
constituent to a hooked pool is an internal registry + LP move (the
adoptable-receipt / constituent-migration scaffolding is the path; note
current deps are v3-core/periphery -- v4-core would be a new vendored
dep, EVM target cancun already satisfied).

Candidate fee policies (the wave-3b axis):

| policy      | fee                                        | prices                         | principal risk                              |
|-------------+--------------------------------------------+--------------------------------+---------------------------------------------|
| vol-scaled  | base + kv x EWMA(tick moves^2)             | raid-speed realized vol        | reactive lag on the first block of a dump   |
| dev-scaled  | base + kd x abs(bvib - 1)                  | excursion persistence          | self-referential sensor; needs cap + median |
| directional | high on deviation-worsening side, base on the correcting side | the attack itself | order-splitting; benign-flow asymmetry      |
| size-tiered | convex in size / impact-cap norm           | block toxicity (uncapped dump) | splitting across txs (use per-block cumul.) |

Properties worth having regardless of policy choice:

- Surge revenue accrues to the LP position = depositor L + treasury L:
  the manipulated are paid BY the manipulation, countercyclically --
  the fee-level analogue of finding 8, and the direct repair of the
  =plot_basket_split= expropriation.
- The directors already maintain tick-EMA ladders on these pools
  (=PairsRebalanceDirector=); the vol estimator is largely built.
- The one-day squeeze trough that no daily-gated defender touched
  (wave-2 conclusion) is exactly what a per-swap fee CAN touch: it is
  the only tick-speed responder that needs no agent, no capital and no
  signal faster than the trade it prices.
- am-AMM variant (auction-managed AMM): auction the right to lead-trade
  the pool each block/epoch; the rent converts LVR into income
  mechanically.  Heavier, changes the pool's trust surface; catalogue it
  as a study behind the simpler fee policies.

Sim plan: implement the fee schedule as a state function in the sim's
pool adapter (no v4 port needed to LEARN); gate any Solidity hook work on
a wave-3b win.

** C. Priced-tail liquidity: the fence generalized

Full-range custody sells optionality at every price for free.  The fence
already reframes depth as "a continuum of resting limit orders" in a
+/-15% band.  Generalize into three structural zones per pool:

1. a *band* around TWAP holding most depositor L (dense where benign flow
   lives -- multiplies fee capture per dollar exactly where volume is);
2. *priced tails*: single-sided range orders at entry-grade offsets
   (~3%+), which fill only at a charged discount/premium -- the
   ~min_edge~ discipline expressed as position structure instead of an
   agent.  A raid that dumps through the band fills the discount tail:
   the basket buys the dump at a price it set in advance, which is
   inventory acquisition at a premium, not silent IL;
3. an *idle reserve* (the fence's (1-K) buffer lineage) sized by K.

Wave-2 already showed the basket-rotation defender was the best scaler
but capacity-limited by thin pools; the basket's own book restruck as
band+tails is that same strategy at two orders of magnitude the size.
Note what this changes for valuation: the =NAV = 2 x buckReserve=
full-range identity dies with full-range; =poolBuckValues= must price
the position properly (the fence already crossed this bridge).

** D. Derivatives overlay: perpetuals and options

Three distinct legs; keep them separate in design and in accounting.

*** D1. Hedging the trending satellites (buy convexity)

The differential axis is genuinely trending and the basket is a
structurally underpaid vol seller there (finding 6; the "deflation
forever" trap, =alberta-buck-ethereum-basket.org=).  The 600 bp cbBTC cap
is the first defense; the second is to hold protective tail exposure on
the satellites, bought externally and funded from the rebalancing
premium (+322-496 bp/yr measured, =BASKET-REBALANCE.md=):

- venue options: perp futures on cbBTC/PAXG-proxies at external venues --
  Hyperliquid (CLOB perps; HIP-3 permits builder-deployed markets as of
  this writing), dYdX, GMX -- or perpetual OPTIONS (Panoptic-style, on
  truth-side pairs) where available; options are the honest instrument
  (the exposure being hedged is gamma, not delta), perps the liquid one
  (a delta-hedge program on perps replicates convexity at rebalance
  cost -- acceptable if sized small);
- sizing from the wave-3a ledger: hedge the measured adverse_i of the
  satellite pools, not a model;
- success metric: depositor IL on spike/step arms with hedge on/off,
  net of hedge cost (funding + fees).  If the hedge does not beat its
  carry cost across x1.3 AND x1.6, the correct hedge is a smaller
  satellite weight, not a derivative.

Caveats: venue/counterparty risk (an external CLOB's margin engine and
custody are outside our solvency argument), basis risk (cbBTC vs venue
BTC-perp), and operational keys.  The overlay must be a treasury program
with its own budget and kill switch, never entangled with depositor L.

*** D2. Writing par-reversion insurance (sell convexity, explicitly)

The system's crown asset is manufactured reversion at par.  Sell it
explicitly instead of implicitly:

- form: a funding-rate perpetual on bvib (mark = medianized bvib TWAP).
  Deviation-longs (bets that |bvib-1| grows) pay funding to the writer
  while inside the band; payout on excursions.  The treasury is the
  natural writer because it enforces the reversion it is selling;
- the credit-base excursion agent already IS this trade implicitly
  (mint/sell above par, cover below); D2 formalizes it with explicit
  premium accounting -- streamia for the backstop currently free;
- the solvency condition is the point: an attacker expressing the raid
  THROUGH the instrument (buy deviation-longs, then dump) must pay more
  in funding + premium + fee surge than the defended excursion can
  return.  Wave 2 measured the defended excursion envelope (peak x
  duration at x1..x4); price the instrument off that envelope, cap open
  interest against the defense budget, and verify the superlinear-loss
  property survives listing.  If it does not price safely, do not list:
  D2 is a study with an honest kill criterion, not a default.

*** D3. Perp telemetry as an early-warning sensor (detection)

Raids have a quiet phase (60-day accumulation before the dump).  Off-pool
venues see it first: funding rates, open interest and basis on BUCK and
satellite pairs move while the accumulator is still impact-capped.  Feed
external funding/OI/basis into the adverse-event state that drives the
fee policy (B) and arms the defenders.  In-sim analogue now: the whale's
accumulation is visible in pool-imbalance and flow-toxicity terms --
add a detector metric and measure LEAD TIME on the dump/squeeze arms
(how many days before the dump does the detector fire, at what false-
positive rate on the no-injection control?).

** F. Readiness yield -- carry funds convexity

The article's deepest point applied to the quadrant program: convexity
books die without carry.  The poised cast bleeds demurrage while waiting
(finding 5), Q3 overcrowds into losses at x4 (finding 10), and the x4
bound showed the cast's EXISTENCE is the difference between equilibrium
and permanent failure.  Route treasury income (BUCK-profit accrual,
fee-surge revenue from B, funding from D2, funding-factor tolls) into a
demurrage rebate for capital verifiably parked in defense posture (a
vault whose funds can only trade toward par).  With endog-style arrival
hazards on defenders, the cast then SIZES ITSELF to the yield.  Wave-3
question: the minimum readiness yield at which the x4 dump bound holds
with an endogenous cast.

* Policy foresight: positioning ahead of K

The unique, legitimate edge.  Sign conventions first (decision of
record): bvib > 1 = basket dear = BUCK cheap = DISCOUNT excursion;
error = 1 - bvib < 0; K falls; credit contracts; BUCK strengthens; bvib
returns to 1.  Premium excursion mirror-image (bvib < 1 -> K rises ->
supply grows -> BUCK weakens back to par).

Because the controller is integral-dominant and slow (tau_I ~90 d), its
trajectory over the next weeks is a committed function of its readable
state.  During a sustained discount the system KNOWS contraction is
coming and BUCK will be pushed up; during a sustained premium, the
reverse.  Three legitimate uses -- this is not front-running external
information, it is the central bank buying its own currency ahead of its
own announced tightening:

1. *Inventory*: BuckBasketOps Q1 absorbs the discount (accumulate BUCK
   the policy is about to make scarce); Q3 supplies the premium.  Both
   coincide directionally with the excursion agents' reversion trades --
   the gradient gate adds confirmation and timing, not direction.
2. *Ranges*: skew the priced tails (option C) toward the enforcement
   direction -- deeper bid-side tails during a discount.
3. *Fees*: pre-emptively raise the anti-enforcement side's fee (option
   B directional policy keyed on the K gradient rather than raw bvib).

A reconciliation flag, raised rather than silently re-litigated: the
roadmap ruling (=REBALANCING-EQ.org= quadrant-balance model) phrases the
ops condition as "absorb ... when BUCK is at a discount and K is
RISING"; under the sign convention above a discount drives K DOWN
(integral dominance makes dK/dt track sign(error) almost immediately),
so the phrase cannot describe K's contemporaneous response to the same
discount.  A 2026-09-01 code audit found the tension lives ONLY in
prose -- every shipped signal is correctly signed (see the audit note
below) -- and that "K rising" has TWO coherent readings as a gate on
the unimplemented BuckBasketOps Q1 condition:

- *Gate A -- enforcement alignment (absorb while K is falling)*: buy
  what policy is actively making scarce; the K forcing closes the
  position.  Supported by =BuckPoolInvestorAgent= (which already
  places ranges on the side K is pushing toward, docstring stating the
  convention exactly) and by =BuckBasketOps.monetaryDeviationOffset='s
  stranding analysis (the desk's position needs the K forcing behind
  it -- "if K then stops tightening, the long-term forcing the desk's
  position is a bet on never arrives").
- *Gate B -- deploy-only-when-healthy (absorb transient discounts
  against a rising TREND-K regime)*: K read at regime scale (tau_I
  ~90 d) classifies the discount -- transient dip in an expansionary
  regime (safe to buy; the corner the slow lever cannot serve) vs
  structural oversupply in a K-falling regime (stand down; the
  controller is handling it, and the monetary book would be spent
  fighting a correction).  Supported verbatim by the fence's
  established K-coupling (=BuckBasketFence.sol=: "K falling forces it
  to withdraw and burn; K rising lets it deploy") -- the same
  design instinct for the same organ family.

The readings differ in timescale (instantaneous dK vs regime trend)
and in objective (profit alignment vs book preservation), and both
have design-text support.  Wave 3d settles it empirically: gate
variants {none, dK<0 fast, trend-K rising, composite} on the Q1/Q3
ops program, measuring both ops P&L and stability -- per the ops-desk
law ("profitability and stabilization ... come apart" only when the
signal lags), and whichever gate wins IS the decision of record.

Audit note (2026-09-01): no implemented agent trades on an inverted
signal.  ExcursionArb and subclasses buy the discount / sell the
premium / close on reversion (bvib EWMA only; K used solely as mint
capacity); SaverAgent code is correct (~discount = max(0, bvib-1) ->
buy~) though its inline comment at
=alberta_buck/sim/equilibrium_agents.py:2099= states the convention
INVERTED ("BUCK is discounted when ... basketValueInBuck < 1") -- a
stale prose slip contradicted by its own next line; the endog hazards
recruit issuers when BUCK is rich (correct); and the shipped monetary
path is correctly signed end-to-end
(=PairsRebalanceDirector.monetaryEffort=: "cm > 0 == BUCK CHEAP ==
retire (negative)" -> =BuckBasketOps= ~effortBp < 0 -> _absorb~), with
NO K-gradient gate implemented anywhere yet.  The catalogue results
corroborate: a backwards cast would amplify excursions and bleed; the
measured cast damps peaks and is paid more the worse the attack.

Two corollaries worth writing down:

- The controller's state is public, so EVERYONE has policy foresight.
  That is a feature: third parties front-running committed policy do the
  enforcement for the system, so K itself has to move less (forward
  guidance).  The basket's edge is not informational -- it is that it
  can position at zero adverse-selection cost against its own book and
  it earns the reversion it enforces.
- fs30 priced perfect 30-day COMMODITY knowledge at +$4-5M on an $8.6M
  book.  Wave 3d prices perfect POLICY knowledge -- which the system
  possesses for free -- on the common mode.  If the number is material,
  the ops program pays for the readiness yield (F) by itself.
