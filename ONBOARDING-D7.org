* The state in one paragraph

BUCK is a stablecoin whose value is defined against a basket of
commodity-token pools; =bvib= (basket value in BUCK) is the process
variable, par (1.0) the setpoint.  A slow lever, K, scales the credit
limits BUCK can be minted against; it is a PID on the error 1 - bvib
(=src/BuckKControllerDirect.sol=, ppm units, integral-dominant, rails
(0, 0.95), K0 0.75).  Several FAST facilities now act on bvib directly by
taking positions: the basket's monetary desk (=BuckBasketOps=, Q1 absorb
/ Q3 supply), the Convertibility Undertakings and their weak-side ladder
(=undertaking_agents.py=), a population of latent-credit facilities
(=facility_agent.py=), and a treasury seeder (=seeder_agent.py=).  Each
holds a bounded inventory.  The design question of record, D7
(=CARRY-CONVEXITY.org=), is how those inventories feed back into K: S
reconstitutes a price (bvib + sum lambda_i q_i / D), V reports a
cost-weighted fill (sum w_i q_i / cap_i); both are consumed by a SECOND
PID loop in K, on the position, with its own gains and one integrator.
Every facility also gets a local skew on its own band so that n
positions have n levers.  The grid that decides between S and V is the
"controller-alternatives test plan" section of =WAVE3.org=: ~130
two-year simulation cells of an equilibrium economy (real contracts on
an in-process EVM) under ten disruption classes.

As of 2026-09-06 the two critical-path packages are MERGED on
=feature/carry-convexity=: decision 8 (46c3b7d: the sim quotes
BUCK/USDC from slot0 through =alberta_buck/sim/gauge.py=; =vecdiff= is
the drift gauge) and WP-13 (b4c1b75: the two-loop controller, the
observer's S / V modes, the hold-last cap, and =shadow_book.py=, which
books the Python stand-ins' inventory into the observer).  Their
reports are =reports/wp-d8.org= and =reports/wp-13.org=; read them
before the package you claim, because they name the surface you build
on and the open questions (WAVE3.org decisions pending 14-19).

* Signs and units (the silent killers)

- bvib > 1: BUCK cheap (discount); K falls.  bvib < 1: BUCK dear
  (premium); K rises.  error = 1 - bvib in ppm inside the controller.
- Inventory / position: BUCK ABSORBED (weak side: bought and held;
  facility retire) is POSITIVE; BUCK ISSUED (strong side: minted and
  sold; facility issue) is NEGATIVE.  Absorbed inventory drives K
  DOWN; issued drives K up.  Under S the aggregate is sum lambda_i q_i
  / D (price units); under V it is sum w_i q_i / cap_i in [-1, 1].
- bvib is BUCK PER BASKET: TOKEN -> BUCK multiplies, BUCK -> TOKEN
  divides.  A BUCK rally is a FALLING pool price when BUCK is token1;
  =BuckPoolInvestorAgent= has the checked logic -- reuse it.
- BUCK and USDC are 6-dec in the sim; =basketValueInBuck= is 18-dec;
  gains are stored real x 1e12; time is seconds.
- The controller's sign is CORRECT; do not "fix" it.

* Glossary (ten terms)

- *bvib*: basket value in BUCK; > 1 BUCK cheap, < 1 BUCK dear.
- *K*: the credit-limit scale the controller sets; the slow lever.
- *the desk*: =BuckBasketOps=; Q1 absorb / Q2 retire / Q3 supply / Q4
  issue.
- *the undertakings*: standing offers at par +/- eps in bundle units;
  strong side unlimited, weak side a NAV-bounded ladder.
- *the facility*: latent credit issuing into premiums and retiring into
  discounts on user thresholds x / y.
- *the seeder*: the treasury's temporary one-sided BUCK/USDC range.
- *position / netInventory*: absorbed minus issued, BUCK; the state a
  stabilizer reports upward.
- *S / V*: the two aggregations of positions under judgment (price
  units with D; cost-weighted fill without D).
- *the position loop*: K's second PID, on the aggregate position, with
  its own gains and one integrator.
- *habituation*: the fast actuators' inventory returning to zero as K
  takes over the steady-state load; the metric the grid gates on.
