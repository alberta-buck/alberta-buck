1.1 The question
────────────────

  The Alberta Buck stabilizes BUCK against a defined basket of commodity
  tokens.  Two kinds of flow act on that equilibrium:

  • *Organic flow*: BUCK buyers and sellers arriving at varying rates
    and intensities for their own reasons – savers accumulating and
    spending, debtors converting mortgages and retiring, depositors
    entering and leaving the basket, and their waves and runs.  Organic
    behaviour is to be SUPPORTED (deep liquidity at par, cheap
    execution, no gates) and REWARDED (the basket's yield against
    demurrage).
  • *Inorganic flow*: agents attempting to disrupt or corner the
    BUCK/TOKEN markets for profit or malice.  Inorganic behaviour is to
    be DETECTED and HARVESTED: the attacker's funds transferred to the
    BuckBasket investors' ROI.

  The basket's issuance capacity and its reserves from prior profits are
  to stand ready to amplify that harvest for all position holders –
  while never holding positions that work AGAINST the restoration of
  equilibrium (a wall of basket positions that K must push the credit
  issuers through, or inventory that stalls the recovery it was bought
  to hasten).  Ideally the cost of the basket's positions is borne by
  the malicious actor, who must eat through them to recover their funds
  after disrupting the equilibrium.


1.2 The answer, in one paragraph
────────────────────────────────

  The system already holds equilibrium against organic flow and, when a
  private defender cast exists, against inorganic flow.  But the harvest
  goal is not met, and until 2026-09-01 was unmeasured: at x4 an
  undefended attacker breaks the peg at zero cost to itself while
  depositors realize -$2.0M to -$2.7M; with the full cast, depositors
  are spared, the harvest (+$1.8M realized) goes to private defenders,
  and the attacker's -$10.6M lands on third-party pool LPs and arbs.
  Harvest accrues to whoever is the COUNTERPARTY at the venue where the
  attack lands, and the basket is not that counterparty today.  The
  remedy is a laddered target zone: private liquidity harvests
  day-to-day traffic near par; citizens' latent credit – unleveraged
  insured assets behind BuckCredit limits, paying no demurrage while
  poised – issues into premiums and retires into discounts through an
  automated facility; and the basket stands behind both as a currency
  board in bundle units, with an announced UNLIMITED strong-side
  undertaking (sell BUCK for the bundle at par + eps) and an NAV-bounded
  weak-side undertaking (buy BUCK for the bundle at par - eps).  K sees
  the SHADOW deviation – bvib plus the inventory the desk and the
  facility have absorbed or issued – so the slow lever mid-ranges every
  fast actuator it can see and the wall never blinds the controller.
  Restoration dominates harvest; the harvest is a small, certain margin
  on both edges plus a stress fee on duress exits, shared pro rata by a
  single receipt class; and the real return to investors is the
  excursion that no longer happens.  A bvib-settled perpetual with a
  funding rate keyed to the shadow deviation charges the DURATION of
  positions held against the band – the missing long-side toll – and its
  telemetry sees the accumulation phase.  Every element is specified as
  a catalogue cell with a pass/fail gate before any contract work.

2.1 The common-mode loop: par, K, and the funding factor
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  • *Par is definitional.* In the direct embodiment BUCK measures itself
    against its basket: `basketValueInBuck' (bvib) = sum_i
    basketAmount_i x price_i-in-BUCK, read as a 600 s TWAP over the
    basket's own TOKEN/BUCK pools with spot fallback on a cold pool.
    Setpoint 1.0; error = 1 - bvib (`alberta-buck-ethereum-direct.org';
    master doc, "Direct Variant").
  • *Sign convention (decision of record).* bvib > 1: the basket costs
    more BUCK than it should, BUCK is CHEAP (a DISCOUNT excursion),
    error < 0, K FALLS, credit contracts, supply shrinks, BUCK recovers.
    bvib < 1: BUCK is DEAR (a PREMIUM excursion), K RISES, credit
    expands.  Canary tests guard the direction.
  • *K is the LTV cap*: creditLimit = activatedValue x K.  K0 = 0.75,
    rails (0, 0.95) so a railed K is still solvent; integral-dominant, a
    sustained 10% deviation spends rail-to-rail authority over tau_I =
    90 days with a 2% proportional kick; conditional-integration
    anti-windup at the rails; bumpless retune and re-prime; dT 1800 s
    (`EQUILIBRIUM.md'; `BuckKControllerBase.sol').  "Deliberately slow:
    K is a structural lever, not a market maker" (`deploy.py').
  • *The funding factor* is the on-chain counter-cyclical toll on
    insured issuance: ff = max(0, 1 + 10 (b - p)/b), 2x at a 10%
    discount, ZERO once BUCK is 10% dear – issuance is cheapest exactly
    in a premium.  It scales the pre-mint reserve on the insurance
    principal, not the gross mint; zero-premium credit is exempt.  The
    sim's issue-rate arm (ff_amp x rate^ff_pow) has no on-chain
    counterpart.
  • *The theta law* (sim-only, the debtors' decision rule): deploy
    credit while the all-in execution discount <= theta x apr; under an
    oversupply flood the marginal issuer stops at theta x apr, so the
    standing discount is set by the most aggressive unconverted debtor
    (~8% median, ~19.5% tail) – the flood is untested
    (`REBALANCING-EQ.org' recommendation 4).
  • *Latent credit* costs nothing until drawn: activation happens only
    inside `Buck.mint', premium is charged on present insured value at
    activation (`CREDIT-IMPROVEMENT.org' D6), demurrage accrues only on
    HELD BUCK, and a drawn position "becomes cheaper to close the longer
    it is carried" through Jubilee relief.  D8 – unvetted insurers can
    write themselves zero-premium credit and mint for free – is the
    unclosed mainnet blocker.


2.2 The basket: custody, issuance, redemption, treasury
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  • The BuckBasket registers one TOKEN/BUCK Uniswap V3 pool per
    constituent and custodies a SINGLE FULL-RANGE Buck-owned LP position
    in each; it is the sole LP in every basket pool (`deploy.py'
    pool_meta).  Full-range means the CPMM identity NAV = 2 x
    buckReserve and a 50/50 value split at any price
    (`BASKET-REDESIGN.md' 5.1).
  • *DirectMint*: a depositor's TOKEN is paired with basket-minted BUCK
    at spot – 100% LTV, K-immune (`Buck.mintFromBasket' bypasses credit
    and funding factor; master doc #10) – and LP'd full-range; the
    depositor receives an ERC-721 receipt.
  • *Redemption is sell-high*: the value claim theta x NAV is drawn from
    the overweight pools by a closed form over the BUCK reserves, valued
    against a price-scaled fixed-quantity target; value conservation
    keeps the remaining holders' coverage ratio unchanged.  The
    depositor is paid in TOKEN ONLY; BUCK profit (Bw - R) accrues to the
    treasury; a BUCK shortfall (Bw < R, the deflation branch) is covered
    from the depositor's TOKEN within `maxConversionLossBp' (1%), and
    NAV < principal reverts (`BASKET-REDESIGN.md' 5, 6).
  • The regime table (`BASKET-REDESIGN.md' 6) predicts treasury revenue
    "largest exactly under inflation, when the peg most needs defending"
    and a costly exit under deflation "so holders keep P outstanding";
    the measured version (`plot_basket_split.py') is that a single agent
    pushing BUCK 19% dear made every redemption take the deflation
    branch and cost depositors ~$1.54M "rather than the agent causing
    it".
  • The directors (`alberta-buck-rebalance.org'): `pairs' (deployed)
    runs a seven-scale tick-EMA ladder per leg, pairwise quorum 4/7 on
    the confirmed turn, leash 30/25%, matched-pair trades; `vrate' is
    the single-window engine; `factor' (trend regime) is Python-only.
    The COMMON MODE is the mean of the same ladders and was being
    discarded.
  • The ops desk (`alberta-buck-operations.org'; `BuckBasketOps.sol'):
    the common mode drives four quadrants – Q1 absorb / Q3 supply as
    self-reversing range orders (repo), Q2 retire / Q4 issue outright
    under a 30-day-past-the-leash persistence test.  Three bounds, each
    found by a runaway: per-leg 0.4% of the pool's BUCK reserve,
    position 10% of NAV with inventory-based escalation, cumulative 10%
    of NAV.  The desk is capitalized by `capitalizeMonetary' ("a
    currency board can only defend with reserves it built up earlier")
    and may not fund itself from depositor TOKEN.
    `monetaryDeviationOffset()' exposes what the desk's inventory has
    taken out of K's error, deliberately not acted on.  Default
    `enabled: false'; it has never run inside a matrix or catalogue arm.
  • The fence (`BuckBasketFence.sol'): caps basket issuance at K x TOKEN
    value at TWAP in a +/-15% band – "a continuum of resting limit
    orders" – so that K falling forces withdraw-and-burn and K rising
    lets it deploy.  Its motivating measurement: K at its floor 21% of a
    730-day run with the K-immune basket tranche at 47-52% of supply.
    Limitation 1: the budget is denominated in the unit that moves.

3 The critique, where it lands, and the basket's own side of the catalogue
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3.1 The critique
────────────────

  Panoptic's "Yin (Carry) & Yang (Convexity) Require Each Other to
  Survive & Thrive" (X, 2026-08-27; [saved copy]) is the onchain
  transplant of the Artemis/Cole framing.  In our terms:

  A passive AMM LP is a carry trader – a seller of perpetual optionality
  who collects a fixed fee (the implied volatility) while eating adverse
  selection (the realized volatility).  Onchain, the structural
  oversupply of LP positions suppresses IV below RV, and crypto is the
  most momentum-prone, fat-tailed asset class – so the profitable side
  is BUYING convexity from LPs, not being one.  Carry books show
  negative skew ("sawtooth": steady fees, rare wipeouts); convexity
  books show positive skew ("stairstep").  A market is complete and
  stable only when both sides exist and both are PRICED.


[saved copy] <./doc/Panoptic-carry.pdf>


3.2 The exposure and the measured evidence
──────────────────────────────────────────

  The basket is the sole full-range LP in every constituent pool; its
  depositor-facing balance sheet IS that LP book; the controller's
  process variable is read from the same pools; every arb, whale slice
  and excursion trade in a basket pool has the basket (depositor L +
  treasury L) as counterparty.  Static fee tiers (TOKEN/BUCK 30 bp,
  BUCK/USDC 5 bp): one implied vol for every regime and counterparty.
  The depositor is the residual claimant on both commodity moves and par
  deviation; the treasury holds a one-sided call.

  Evidence already in the repo, not previously named: the $1.54M
  deflation-branch expropriation (`plot_basket_split.py'); finding 6
  (the MA-reversion differential rebalancer loses ~6%/yr trading macro
  drift as reversion – RV > IV on the differential axis); finding 11
  (foresight turns the same book to +$4-5M – the informed convexity
  buyer's profit is real and the quoting side pays it); the ops desk
  losing on `drift' (2/9 seeds) and the law "a lagged operator is a
  profitable destabilizer"; the fence test's "genuine impermanent loss
  that swamps the fee income"; and the absence of any LVR/markout
  accounting (`snapshot.py' LP APR is gross fee yield).

4 Theory: eight results and the repo text each one clarifies or contrasts

4.1 Carry and convexity: what the basket sells and what it buys
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Kyle (1985) and Glosten-Milgrom (1985): a standing quote is a free
  option to informed traders and a paid service to uninformed ones; the
  quoter's profit is the uninformed flow's spread minus the informed
  flow's markout.  Milionis, Moallemi, Roughgarden and Zhang (2022) put
  the AMM case in closed form: loss-versus-rebalancing sigma^2/8 per
  unit time against a martingale price.  Panoptic's contribution is the
  empirical inversion onchain (RV > IV) and the framing that carry and
  convexity books need each other to be PRICED.

  *Clarifies* `alberta-buck-ethereum-basket.org': "the world's
  arbitrageurs, working for free" is true of the BENIGN flow – the
  uninformed cycles that repin the pools and pay the fee – and false of
  the informed flow, which the same pools sell the basket's optionality
  to.  The basket doc's yield thesis (Shannon's demon, +322 to +496
  bp/yr measured in `BASKET-REBALANCE.md') is a DIFFERENTIAL harvest
  that requires reversion; the COMMON-MODE convexity the same full-range
  positions sell is a different exposure with a different owner of the
  reversion (K), and the two must be separated in the position
  structure.  *Contrasts* the routing doc's sim, which measured that
  BUCK-unaware arbs genuinely route through the basket pools (~1100
  routed trades vs ~120 whale snaps) – that flow is the workforce the
  treasury seeder of D5 exists to lubricate, and it must stay cheap.


4.2 Target zones: a credible band stabilizes inside itself
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Krugman (1991): with a credible commitment to intervene at the edges
  of a band, the exchange rate inside the band is LESS volatile than the
  fundamentals – the honeymoon S-curve – because speculators front-run
  the edge.  Bertola and Caballero (1992): with IMPERFECT credibility
  the effect reverses; expected realignment makes the rate more volatile
  near the edges.  The asymmetry is empirical: Hong Kong's strong-side
  Convertibility Undertaking (unlimited HKD sales at 7.75, 2005) has
  never been abandoned and never been beaten; the SNB's EUR/CHF floor
  (2011-2015), mechanically identical, was abandoned because unlimited
  issuance accumulated a balance sheet approaching GDP in a foreign
  asset against a FUNDAMENTAL pressure it could not make revert.

  *Clarifies* `BuckBasketFence.sol': "a continuum of resting limit
  orders" is a band without announced edges and with a budget
  "denominated in the unit that moves" (limitation 1); D3 gives the band
  announced, bundle-denominated edges and keeps the fence for what it is
  good at – K-scaling the PERSISTENT depositor stock.  *Contrasts* the
  ops doc's reserve doctrine ("any bound it publishes will be probed –
  the 1992 ERM crisis is the standing lesson"): that is the WEAK side's
  truth, where the defender spends finite reserves; on the STRONG side
  the defender issues its own liability, and the correct doctrine is the
  opposite – announce it unlimited.  *Explains* the catalogue: the x4
  failures are all on the premium side because the weak side of the
  current design (private absorbers, the desk's TOKEN book) is reserve-
  bounded AND the strong side (issuance) is K-scaled and slow.


4.3 The backstop at a penalty rate
──────────────────────────────────

  Bagehot (1873): in a panic, lend freely, at a penalty rate, on good
  collateral.  The penalty rate is what keeps the backstop from being
  used in normal times and pays it when it is.

  *Clarifies* the design owner's ruling that "the BuckBasket isn't a
  market-making liquidity provider, but a stabilizing backstop": the
  basket's depth sits OUTSIDE the private band, at a small fixed premium
  eps, and earns that premium for depth-on-demand.  *Contrasts* the ops
  doc's measured trade-off ("stabilization and extraction trade off":
  10-day signal peak 1004 bp / desk +$623k; 80-day 1344 bp / +$746k;
  outright inverts past 80 days): the penalty rate is a FIXED small
  premium, never a stress-scaled extraction, and the recovery is never
  held hostage (D1).


4.4 Mid-ranging: two actuators, one process variable
────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Shinskey's valve-position (mid-ranging) control: when a fast small
  actuator and a slow large one act on the same variable, the fast one
  controls the process variable and the slow controller does NOT look at
  the process variable – it looks at the fast actuator's POSITION and
  drives it back to mid-range.  Two controllers on one variable without
  this separation oscillate or wind up.  The Federal Reserve's pre-2008
  open-market desk is the monetary instance: the FOMC sets the target;
  the desk holds the funds rate there with daily temporary repos;
  ever-larger temporary operations in one direction are the signal for
  an outright purchase.  The FX literature (Jurgensen 1983;
  Dominguez-Frankel 1993) is the negative result: sterilized
  intervention – the desk acting while the policy lever is unaware – is
  weak and transient.

  *Clarifies* `alberta-buck-operations.org' runaway one ("the operator
  suppresses its own alarm") and `BuckBasketOps.monetaryDeviationOffset'
  ("exposed rather than acted on, because the right response is a
  controller-side choice"): the controller-side choice is mid-ranging
  (D4).  *Clarifies* `deploy.py''s "K is a structural lever, not a
  market maker; it glides over months while private demand does the fast
  stabilization" – that is a frequency separation with the coupling left
  unspecified; D4 specifies it.  *Overturns* v1.0's "K-gradient gate"
  question: the desk and the facility do not gate on dK; their books ARE
  what K mid-ranges.


4.5 Corners and the ability to deliver
──────────────────────────────────────

  A corner breaks when the cornered asset can be delivered in any size
  at a known price.  The Hunt silver corner (1980) was broken by
  changing the rules mid-game – effective, and corrosive to trust in the
  venue.  Hong Kong's strong side breaks any HKD corner by standing
  rule.  The SNB shows the one way unlimited delivery fails: when what
  it accumulates is a liability you cannot make revert.

  *Clarifies* the ops doc's "what only the contract can do – Q4 is not
  capped by K" and the fence's "the most K-immune half of the float":
  the K-immune `mintFromBasket' is the system's ONLY anti-corner power,
  and it must not be blunted by K-scaling; D3 resolves the conflict by
  duration (persistent stock K-scaled; standing facility K-immune and
  self-reversing).  *Explains* why the basket's unlimited undertaking is
  SAFER than the SNB's: it sells BUCK dear for TOKEN that is cheap in
  BUCK, on an axis where par is definitional and K forces the reversion,
  and Q2 closes the round trip.  The only path to the SNB's fate is
  governance abandoning par.


4.6 The elastic currency and the real-bills doctrine
────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  The Federal Reserve Act (1913) created an elastic currency: money
  supply expands when banks discount real bills at the window and
  contracts as the bills self-liquidate.  Its historical flaw is
  pro-cyclicality (credit expands as collateral values rise), which a
  counter-cyclical lever exists to correct.

  *Clarifies* the design owner's D6 ruling: the poised capital is NOT
  BUCK but LATENT CREDIT – unleveraged insured assets behind BuckCredit
  limits – issued into premiums and retired into discounts.  That is a
  privately discounted elastic currency with K as the haircut and the
  funding factor as the discount rate, and the on-chain funding factor
  already makes issuance cheapest in a premium.  *Contrasts*
  `alberta-buck-demurrage.org' (demurrage punishes idle BUCK) with the
  demand for standing defense capital: no conflict, because the standing
  capital is not BUCK.  *Sharpens* `EQUILIBRIUM.md' approach forward
  (C), "a direct TOKEN/BUCK stabilization channel for savers" – the
  auto-pay-down facility of D6 is that channel, on the credit side.


4.7 Swing pricing: the exiting investor pays for the liquidity they consume
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  The Reserve Primary Fund broke the buck in September 2008 and those
  who STAYED bore the loss; the SEC's 2014 and 2023 reforms made
  liquidity fees on stressed redemptions mandatory for institutional
  prime funds.  Liquity's Stability Pool (opt-in, paid) versus passive
  LPs in the UST/3CRV pool of May 2022 (conscripted, expropriated) is
  the same lesson in DeFi.

  *Clarifies* `BASKET-REDESIGN.md''s regime table – its deflation branch
  is the pre-2014 money-market fund, and the measured expropriation
  ($1.54M; -$2.7M at x4) is the proof – and the ops doc's "the desk may
  not fund from depositor TOKEN" (it refuses to conscript explicitly
  while full-range custody conscripts implicitly).  D2's stress fee is
  the fix the one industry that learned it the hard way adopted.


4.8 Reserve adequacy and the two generations of crisis model
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Krugman (1979) / Flood-Garber (1984): a peg defended with finite
  reserves against a persistent fundamental misalignment fails at a
  computable date.  Obstfeld (1986, 1994): with costly defense, attacks
  can be self-fulfilling.  Both say the same thing about the weak side
  of a band: its credibility is its reserve relative to the plausible
  outflow over the horizon on which the slow lever can correct.

  *Clarifies* the ops doc's "reserves are finite and the bound is
  public" and the catalogue's x4 grind-down with the full cast (parity
  held, rail margin spent: K 0.936, 17% railed) – that cell is the
  empirical adequacy frontier.  *Sets* the sizing rule of 7.5.

  The first-generation model has a constructive corollary: the attack is
  timed to the DISCONTINUITY – the moment a flat edge backed by a finite
  reserve stops being honored.  Remove the discontinuity and the timing
  argument has nothing to bite on.  A defender whose bid price declines
  smoothly with its remaining reserve is never "out"; it is only ever
  "dearer", and the attacker's marginal cost rises with every unit sold
  into it.  This is the shape of every constant-product bid (the
  catalogue's superlinear squeeze cost is this property of the BUCK/USDC
  pool), of Curve's stableswap between its flat centre and its convex
  tails (the amplification parameter A is the dial), and of a central
  bank that sells reserves by declining-price auction rather than at a
  fixed rate.  *Clarifies* the weak-side design: the design owner's
  tranche ladder (D3 refinement) is the discrete form, and the flat edge
  of a classical target zone is its delta -> 0 limit – the one limit
  with a cliff.

5.1 D1. Restoration dominates; the backstop earns a fixed premium
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  /Question./ When harvest and restoration conflict, which dominates?

  /Historical./ Hong Kong, August 1998: the "double play" (short HKD to
  force rates up under the currency board; short Hang Seng futures to
  profit from the crash).  The HKMA spent HK$118bn over ten trading days
  buying 33 constituents – a bid wall in the venue where the attackers'
  PROFIT was booked – lifting the index from ~6,700 to ~7,800 into the
  August expiry; the shorts were forced to cover into the wall (harvest
  at ENTRY).  The unwind was restoration-first: held ~15 months, then
  disposed through the Tracker Fund (Nov 1999; HK$33.3bn first tranche
  to 180,000 retail investors; >HK$100bn recouped over three years;
  ~HK$100bn profit) – from the RECOVERY, into organic demand, never sold
  back to the attackers at a premium.  Then the commitment was hardened
  (the September 1998 technical measures); no comparable attack since.
  Sterling 1992 is the contrast: reserves spent against a misaligned
  fundamental.  The difference is whether the defender can MAKE the
  reversion; par is definitional and K manufactures it.

  /Synthetic./ The ops doc's measurement-scale ladder (above):
  extraction bought +$123k of desk profit for +340 bp of peak.  A ~20%
  deviation held a year swung depositors' realized P&L by ~$2.7M: the
  organic cost of prolonging an excursion is of the order of the ENTIRE
  harvest.

  /Decision./ Restoration dominates.  The conflict exists on ONE of four
  legs:

  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
   side                 entry leg                                               exit leg                                                              
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   squeeze (BUCK dear)  Q4 sell issuance at a premium: harvest AND restoration  attacker dumps back -> Q1/Q2 buy cheap, burn: harvest AND restoration 
   dump (BUCK cheap)    Q1 buy the dump at a discount: harvest AND restoration  selling inventory back at a premium: harvest AGAINST restoration      
  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Harvest is taken on the three coinciding legs.  The fourth is unwound
  TraHK-style – rate-limited into organic flow at par over K's
  correction horizon – or burned (Q2) when persistence says the move was
  real.  Extraction is not an objective function.

  /Refinement./ The basket is a stabilizing backstop, not a market
  maker, and earns a SMALL FIXED premium eps for depth-on-demand
  (Bagehot).  Private LPs inside +/-x% harvest the traffic; the basket's
  deep liquidity sits outside.  A recovering dump reaches the basket's
  ask side only if it OVERSHOOTS into a premium, so inventory bought on
  the bid side is sold at a premium only when the market pays it; the
  remainder is disposed at par.  Spot tails (V3 now, V4 directional-fee
  tails later) are the facility; a perpetual is not (a trader needing
  depth wants conversion, not a derivative – D5).


5.2 D2. Single receipt class; a stress fee on duress exits
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  /Question./ May depositor liquidity be wall ammunition, and who bears
  the cost among depositors?  (Depositors already ARE the wall –
  involuntarily, unpaid, at every price.)

  /Historical./ Liquity's Stability Pool (opt-in, paid, held through
  2022); the UST/3CRV LPs (conscripted, expropriated); money-market fund
  reform (the exiting investor pays; a fee is not a gate).

  /Synthetic./ Defended x4 cells show the return to depositors of a paid
  wall: +$0.67M instead of -$1.99M.

  /Decision./ A SINGLE receipt class – every depositor pro rata in band
  and tails; the harvest (undertaking margins, stress fees, rebalancing
  premium) shared pro rata.  A STRESS FEE on duress exits: a redemption
  landing in the deflation branch during a measured excursion pays a fee
  proportional to the deviation, credited to remaining holders.  "If you
  need to exit under duress, you take the hit."  Held long enough, K
  drives bvib back to par and rebalancing garners the reversion premium.
  Compatible with "always redeemable, no gate".

  /Caveat of record./ "IL does not bite the long-term holder" is true by
  construction on the common mode and true on the differential mode only

5.3 D3. Two issuance classes; the Convertibility Undertakings
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  /Question./ The x4 failures are corners the K-scaled issuers cannot
  answer; the only actor that can deliver BUCK on demand is the basket
  via K-immune `mintFromBasket'; the fence wants basket issuance
  K-scaled.

  /Historical./ Hong Kong's strong side (unlimited, standing, never
  beaten); the SNB floor (unlimited, abandoned – the balance sheet it
  accumulated); the Hunt corner (rules changed mid-game).  Standing
  rules beat emergency ones.

  /Synthetic./ Squeeze x4 undefended: whale -$9.13M, bvib 0.69, K railed
  all year.  In the isolation runs Q4 fired 2 and 10 times and Q4+Q2 was
  what the external agent could not do.  The fence's coupling has the
  SAME sign as the ops quadrants; its concerns – a persistent K-immune
  STOCK and a budget in the unit that moves – do not apply to
  self-reversing issuance retired by Q2.

  /Decision./ Two issuance classes, distinguished by DURATION:

  1. *Depositor issuance* (persistent stock: DirectMint partner BUCK
     living as long as the deposit) – K-scaled via the fence once
     fence-in-the- loop passes its cell, so K governs the stock the
     economy carries.
  2. *The Convertibility Undertakings* (standing, announced, UNLIMITED
     in aggregate on the strong side):
     • *Strong side.* Anyone may buy BUCK from the basket at par + eps
       paying the basket BUNDLE in `basketAmount' proportions; or at par
       + p*eps paying ANY token with a conversion path known to the
       basket at deposit time (USDC, BUCK, WBTC, PAXG, …), the basket
       spot-converting the incoming asset atomically into its
       underweight constituents by its rebalancing metrics.  p is a
       convenience factor (~2x the route conversion fees; may scale with
       size for impact).  "Unlimited" is the bundle path; the
       convenience path is bounded by conversion capacity (`maxLegBp' on
       the route, so the redemption guard never bricks).
     • *Weak side.* Anyone may sell BUCK to the basket for the bundle at
       par - eps, or for a single token at par - p*eps, the mix chosen
       by the basket's under-/over-weight metrics (sell-high) and
       converted to one TOKEN atomically in the redemption transaction.
       Reserve-bounded by NAV; the stress fee of D2 composes under
       duress.
     • *The weak side is a LADDER, not an edge* (refinement,
       2026-09-02).  Because the weak side's reserve is finite and "must
       outlast K's correction", its bid price scales DOWN with the
       reserve capacity remaining: a set of positions sized and priced
       so that liquidity is always available, but successive tranches
       are available at successively poorer rates.  Tranche k is struck
       at a discount eps + (k-1)*delta and sized at a fraction phi of
       the reserve remaining when it is struck, so the reserve depletes
       geometrically and never reaches zero at a finite price.  There is
       no cliff for an attacker to time (4.8), the attacker's marginal
       cost rises with cumulative size by construction, and the
       deviation itself becomes an honest gauge of reserve stress –
       which is the coupling to K the shadow term wants (7.4).  delta ->
       0 is the flat edge; a fixed delta/phi ratio is the
       constant-product bid (the shape the basket's full-range position
       already has); Curve's stableswap amplification is the
       interpolation.  The same rule applies to the STRONG side's
       capacity-bounded convenience path (the premium p*eps rising as
       the epoch's conversion capacity is consumed); only the unlimited
       bundle path is flat.
     • K-immune by design (fires only at a premium/discount, exactly
       when the funding factor already says issuance/retirement is
       cheap); accounted to the monetary book; retired/unwound by Q2/Q1
       under the persistence test; bundle-priced so it carries no
       differential exposure.
     • *Announced unlimited.* A published cumulative cap would be
       probed; what unlimited issuance accumulates here is reversion
       inventory on a controlled axis, and Q2 guarantees the balance
       sheet shrinks back.

  /Consequence./ A currency board in bundle units: while both sides are
  honored, bvib is pinned inside [1/(1+eps), 1/(1-eps)] and Krugman's
  honeymoon applies inside.  The x4 squeeze re-run: the pool price
  cannot exceed par + p*eps; the whale's $40M buys BUCK from the basket;
  the basket books ~2 p eps over the round trip; the excursion never
  exceeds the band; K barely moves; depositors never enter the deflation
  branch.  Deterrence replaces harvest – and for basket investors (from
  -$2.7M to ~+$1M) deterrence wins.


5.4 D4. K sees the shadow bvib (mid-ranging)
────────────────────────────────────────────

  /Question./ The desk absorbs a dump, bvib improves, K sees less error
  and stops tightening, the desk is stranded; under D3 the desk's book
  is unbounded on the strong side.  Sterilized or unsterilized?

  /Historical./ The FX-intervention literature; the Fed desk's
  temporary- to-outright rule; mid-ranging control (4.4).

  /Synthetic./ Runaway one (49.9% of pool depth, profitable in 7/9
  seeds, never escalated); the 90-day run (1.25M BUCK held while K still
  fell 0.745 -> 0.584 – "not suppressed AT THAT SIZE").

  /Decision./ MID-RANGING.  K's process variable becomes the shadow
  deviation

  bvib_shadow = bvib + lambda * (net monetary inventory / reference
  depth)

  with BUCK ABSORBED under the weak side counting positive and BUCK
  ISSUED under the strong side counting negative – bvib as it would have
  been without the desk.  Equivalently, K is tasked with driving the
  desk's inventory to zero.  Three things follow: the desk's position is
  never a bet on "K might keep forcing"; Q2 burns need no special
  treatment (burned inventory leaves the book and the shadow term drops
  to zero – the temporary/outright and sterilized/unsterilized
  distinctions are the same distinction); and it IS the achieved-action
  anti-windup the ops doc asked for.  The design owner's statement of
  intent: "even if we manage to raise bvib back to parity, K must
  reflect the error we've damped by our action."


5.5 D5. No USD exposure; the treasury seeds BUCK/USDC temporarily; the perp is a funding-rate lever
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  /Question./ The attacker's losses land on the BUCK/USDC pool's LPs.
  Must the basket own that venue, taking BUCK/USD exposure it has no
  policy control over?

  /Historical./ Counterparty vs venue (COMEX earned fees from the Hunts
  and harvested nothing; the HKMA stood as counterparty); Hong Kong
  after 2005 (attacks stopped: deterrence replaces harvest); the cheap
  band-break bet and the currency board's automatic interest-rate
  mechanism (overnight HIBOR ~280% in October 1997; the aggregate
  balance from ~HK$337bn to ~HK$45bn in 2022-23 until the shorts gave
  up), and its 1997 flaw – the rate spike crushed every organic borrower
  too.

  /Synthetic./ Under D3 the basket owns the EDGES; it need not own the
  pool.  What the undertakings do not do: cap the DURATION of a position
  against par (inside the band a corner is cheap to hold – a short-BUCK
  bettor draws credit and pays premium, funding toll and K's
  contraction; a long-BUCK bettor pays only 2%/yr demurrage; the squeeze
  flank is weak because holding a squeeze is cheap), or see the
  ACCUMULATION phase.

  /Decision./
  1. The basket takes NO USD exposure and does not own the BUCK/USDC
     venue; it becomes a private venue for inside-the-band traffic.
  2. *The treasury seeds BUCK/USDC depth TEMPORARILY at launch*, to
     lubricate the BUCK-unaware arb route that repins the TOKEN/BUCK
     pools via BUCK/USDC: concentrated positions from the CURRENT price
     toward the IDEAL (the bundle's USD value at par, read from the
     truth pools) and not beyond – private LPs supply depth beyond
     ideal.  A single-sided V3 position from current toward ideal is
     always funded with the asset the CORRECTIVE arb wants to take (BUCK
     if the price must rise, USDC if it must fall), so it is the
     counterparty to corrective flow by construction, self-reversing,
     and withdrawn as private depth arrives – `BuckPoolInvestorAgent''s
     placement with a different objective (lubricate, not earn).
     Orientation is the "silent killer" of the ops doc; verify in sim.
  3. *The perp is a bvib FUNDING-RATE LEVER, not a trading venue.*
     Settled on bvib (par-denominated), never on BUCK/USD; funding set
     by the system as a function of the shadow deviation; the monetary
     book as the house, mid-ranged by K.  Hong Kong's automatic
     interest-rate mechanism TARGETED: it charges positions held against
     the band and nobody else.  Its OI, basis and funding are the
     accumulation-phase telemetry; its payoff is bounded by the band.
     Built as a sim agent first, or an installed contract if easily
     obtained (wave 3f).


5.6 D6. Demurrage stays; poised capital is latent credit; the auto-pay-down facility
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  /Question./ Should capital verifiably parked in defense posture be
  rebated its demurrage?

  /Historical./ Woergl (demurrage's target is the idle hoard); interest
  on reserves vs negative deposit rates; catastrophe bonds (coupon for
  committed capital, funded by premiums); the elastic currency (4.6).

  /Synthetic./ Which poised capital EARNED in the catalogue: the USDC
  absorbers (no demurrage) realized +$0.26 / +0.28 / +0.39M; every
  BUCK-holding mix bled; the credit base was scale-stable, +$0.63M
  marked, and paid nothing while poised because it holds no BUCK.

  /Decision (design owner)./ Demurrage on poised BUCK is not something
  that should happen – because the poised capital is NOT BUCK.  It is
  LATENT CREDIT: a hopefully revenue-generating insured asset (farms,
  homes, businesses – the plentiful pent-up un-leveraged asset base)
  behind a BuckCredit contract with an unused limit, ready for
  deployment, with no BUCK sitting in an account accruing demurrage.
  When BUCK is at a premium, holders issue against their limit and buy
  assets with the temporarily too-valuable BUCK; at a discount they buy
  BUCK cheap and pay their credit down.  Four rules:

  1. *No readiness rebate.* Demurrage stays; BUCK parked in the basket
     earns the single-class harvest (the basket doc's "yield against
     demurrage" made concrete); weak-side defense capital is
     TOKEN/USDC/NAV and never paid demurrage; the strong side is
     issuance, not inventory; the monetary book's own BUCK pays
     demurrage as an internal transfer to the Jubilee.
  2. *eps is LARGER than a backstop alone would choose*, leaving an
     inner zone where ordinary citizens with available credit "get in on
     the deflation action" and "buy back their credit limit at a
     temporary inflation moment".  The private latent-credit cast is the
     middle of the ladder; the basket is the wall behind it.
  3. *The auto-pay-down facility.* Automated, user-parameterized
     machinery attached to a BuckCredit position: issue against the
     limit when bvib < 1 - x (premium) and place the proceeds (default:
     a basket deposit under the single receipt class); when bvib > 1 + y
     (discount), redeem/buy BUCK cheap and repay (retirement happens on
     the BUY; the burn closes the line).  Parameters: x, y, maximum
     fraction of limit to deploy, target asset.
     `ExcursionCreditArbAgent''s Q4/Q2 logic as an on-chain facility,
     pre-priced by the funding factor – an offset mortgage that draws
     when your currency is dear and repays when it is cheap.
  4. *Facility utilization enters K's shadow term* (7.3): deployment in
     a premium is net issued BUCK, retirement in a discount is net
     absorbed, exactly like the desk's book.  Weak-side extension beyond
     NAV, if ever wanted, takes the cat-bond form (capped vault,
     only-toward-par, coupon from realized eps and fee-surge income),
     never a rebate.

6.4 The hierarchical structure: the stabilizer seam and the monetary Diamond
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  (Design owner + Claude, 2026-09-02.)  Once several fast actuators act
  on bvib – the pools, the desk, the undertakings and their ladder, the
  facility population, the house – "which contract holds K" stops being
  a question about addresses and becomes a question about signals.  The
  rule from mid-ranging control (4.4) settles it:

  K consumes the fast actuators' POSITIONS and their SATURATION, never
  their filtered signals.


6.4.1 The two seams, and which one is artificial
╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌

  `Buck' holds two authorities: `buckK' (an `immutable'; the `IBuckK'
  surface `currentBuckK' / `compute' / `fundingFactor') and `basket'
  (one-shot).  The `IBuckK' seam is NOT artificial: it is what lets the
  controller be swapped and forge-tested as the mathematical
  specification without touching `Buck'.  What has become artificial is
  the ADDRESS behind it.  `BuckKControllerDirect._readReferences' reads
  one number from one contract; under this design the process variable
  is assembled from several books that all live on the basket side.  The
  observer that assembles them belongs where the state lives – the
  monetary Diamond – and `Buck''s two authorities should both point at
  it.  Because `buckK' is immutable, that address must be the Diamond
  (or an upgradeable controller proxy) from the day `Buck' is deployed;
  the decision is recorded in `WAVE3.org'.

6.4.3 The stabilizer seam
╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌╌

  Every level-1 actuator implements one interface:

  interface IStabilizer { function netInventory() external view returns
      (int256); // absorbed - issued, BUCK (6-dec) function capacity()
      external view returns (uint256); // remaining, 1e18 = all: rho
      (ladder), // C_leg room (strong-side convenience // path),
      headroom (facility), OI room (house) function saturation()
      external view returns (uint256); // 0..1e18, degree at a bound }

  The OBSERVER registers stabilizers with governance weights lambda_i
  and computes

  shadowValueInBuck() = bvib + sum_i lambda_i * netInventory_i / D
  saturation() = weighted max_i saturation_i

  The CONTROLLER's `_readReferences' reads the observer instead of the
  basket, integrates the shadow error exactly as today (rails,
  conditional integration, priming, dS, dT), and uses `saturation()' for
  bounded gain scheduling – Ki_eff = Ki (1 + gamma saturation), gamma
  governance-set and swept – so that when the fast actuators are pinned
  at their bounds the structural lever moves faster.  That is the ops
  doc's "escalate on inventory, because inventory is the one signal the
  operator's own action cannot suppress", generalized to the whole
  level.  With every lambda_i = 0 and gamma = 0 the controller IS
  `BuckKControllerDirect'; that identity is the regression test.

  Three things the structure deliberately does NOT do, each for a reason
  already measured:

  • *It does not feed the fast controllers' filtered deviations into K.*
    The desk's 20-day common mode is a filtered bvib, which K already
    integrates; feeding both double-counts, and the ops doc's ladder
    scan showed a lagged reading INVERTS the outright quadrants past 80
    days.  Positions carry the information without the lag.
  • *It does not let the fast controllers set K, and K does not set
    their setpoints.* Cascade control is the wrong topology: the fast
    controllers' setpoint is par, which is definitional; K mid-ranges
    THEM.
  • *It needs no priority logic among the fast controllers.* The
    laddered target zone (6.1) is already a split-range structure: each
    actuator's active zone is a band of bvib.  The one real coupling is
    shared pool depth (the ops doc's "the desk and the rebalancer
    compete for the same actuator"), which the observer resolves with a
    per-epoch capacity budget (C_leg accounting), not with priorities.
    The persistence escalation (level 2) is the handoff: a Q2 burn or Q4
    acknowledgment moves inventory out of level 1 and into K's stock,
    and the shadow term drops accordingly.

7.2 The plant, reduced
──────────────────────

  The ops doc's `monetary_ops.py' abstraction is adequate here: one
  common-mode price with constant-product impact against depth and an OU
  reversion whose rate is what the private cast and K supply.  A flow F
  (BUCK sold, positive) moves bvib by ~F/D at impact; the cast reverts
  it on its threshold-gated timescales; K moves supply through
  creditLimit on tau_I.  Nothing below depends on the reduced form
  except the qualitative claims; the catalogue runs the real contracts.


7.3 The controller and the shadow term
──────────────────────────────────────

  bvib_shadow = bvib + lambda_d * I_d / D + lambda_c * I_c / D e = 1 -
  bvib_shadow dK/dt = Kp * de/dt + Ki * e (conditional integration at
  the rails)

  Signs: BUCK absorbed under the weak side (or retired by the facility)
  makes I positive, raises bvib_shadow, makes e more negative, and K
  falls harder – the contraction the absorption was betting on.  BUCK
  issued under the strong side (or by the facility) makes I negative,
  lowers bvib_shadow, makes e more positive, and K rises – restoring the
  headroom the fast actuators spent.  A Q2 burn removes the inventory
  from I_d; the shadow term drops; K sees the real bvib; no double
  count.  lambda -> 0 is today's controller; lambda large makes K chase
  every temporary operation.  The mid-ranging claim: with lambda at its
  knee, the desk's inventory returns to zero within ~tau_I of any
  excursion that K can correct, and grows without bound only when the
  deviation is structural – which is the signal the persistence test
  uses to escalate to Q2/Q4.


7.4 The undertakings
────────────────────

  Strong side: the basket sells 1 BUCK for (1 + eps) bundles' worth of
  TOKEN (bundle path) or (1 + p*eps) bundles' worth of any convertible
  token, converting atomically.  Whenever the pools price BUCK above (1
  + p*eps) bundles – bvib < 1/(1 + p*eps) – an arbitrage exists: buy
  BUCK from the basket, sell it in the pools.  So

  bvib >= 1 / (1 + p*eps) always, up to conversion capacity,

  and >= 1/(1+eps) with no capacity bound at all on the bundle path.
  Weak side: the basket buys 1 BUCK for (1 - eps) bundles; whenever the
  pools price BUCK below (1 - p*eps) bundles the arbitrage runs the
  other way:

  bvib <= 1 / (1 - p*eps) while R_w > 0, up to conversion capacity.

  The band is asymmetric in what it is backed by: issuance (unbounded)
  on the strong side; NAV on the weak side.  The monetary book records
  issued and absorbed BUCK; the persistence test escalates a standing
  book to outright (Q2 burn of absorbed BUCK; Q4 acknowledgment of
  issued BUCK as permanent supply, K then carrying it) exactly as the
  desk does today.

  *The weak-side ladder.* Let R_0 be the weak-side reserve in bundle
  units and rho = R / R_0 the fraction remaining.  Tranche k (k = 1, 2,
  …) bids for BUCK at (1 - eps - (k-1) delta) bundles per BUCK and is
  sized at phi R_{k-1}, where R_{k-1} is the reserve remaining when it
  is struck.  After n filled tranches

  R_n = R_0 (1 - phi)^n (never zero at finite n) b_n = 1 - eps - n delta
  (the next bid, bundles per BUCK) bvib <= 1 / b_n (the deviation the
  ladder permits) S_n = R_0 (1 - (1 - phi)^n) / b_avg (cumulative BUCK
  absorbed)

  so the deviation an attacker can force grows only as the LOGARITHM of
  the reserve fraction spent, while its average sale price worsens
  linearly in n.  Limits: delta = 0 is the flat edge (finite reserve at
  one price – the cliff); delta/phi held fixed as both -> 0 is the
  constant-product bid, whose price-versus-size curve is the basket's
  existing full-range position; the stableswap family interpolates.  Two
  couplings to K follow.  As tranches fill, bvib rises past 1/(1 - eps)
  into the sloped region – the raw error grows – AND the absorbed
  inventory I_d grows, raising bvib_shadow; both push K to contract, so
  reserve depletion is what ACCELERATES the structural correction rather
  than what precedes its failure.  lambda_d is therefore tuned with the
  ladder present (3c before 3d).  The strong side's convenience path
  uses the same rule against conversion capacity: p_k rises as the
  epoch's C_leg is consumed; the bundle path is flat because it is
  unbounded.

  The per-leg bound C_leg is a liveness constraint (the redemption guard
  must never brick), not a policy bound; it is the reason a one-day
  squeeze larger than the day's conversion capacity still leaves a
  transient trough, and the reason the directional dynamic fee (3b) and
  the perp's funding (3f) exist.


7.5 The attacker's cost, before and after
─────────────────────────────────────────

  Today (squeeze of size S in the BUCK/USDC pool): cost = slippage
  against the pool's depth on the way in and out + fees, paid to the
  pool's LPs and the arbs; damage unbounded until the cast and K
  respond; at x4 undefended, damage lasts a year and the whale ends
  flat.

  Under the ladder: the pool price is capped at par + p*eps by the
  undertaking, so beyond the band the whale buys from the basket; cost
  >= S * (p*eps entry + p*eps exit) + fees + f * duration (if the
  position is expressed or hedged in the perp).  Damage: |bvib - 1| <=
  p*eps outside the conversion-capacity window; inside it (S > C_leg per
  epoch), the transient is bounded by what the cast and the directional
  fee price at tick speed.  The harvest per attack is small and certain
  – ~2 p eps S – and accrues to the single receipt class; the attacker's
  expected value is negative at every size, so the rational attacker
  does not attack.  This is the target-zone result: the band converts
  the harvest into deterrence.


7.6 Reserve adequacy on the weak side
─────────────────────────────────────

  The weak side is the reserve-bounded flank.  With a flat edge its
  credibility condition would be

  R_w >= max over plausible scenarios of integral over tau_I of (net
  BUCK sold to the basket at par - eps)

  – the reserve must outlast K's correction of the deviation producing
  the outflow, and the attack is timed to the day it does not.  With the
  ladder the condition changes character: the reserve is never exhausted
  at a finite price, so adequacy becomes a statement about the DEVIATION
  the system tolerates per fraction of reserve spent – the choice of
  (delta, phi) – and about whether K's correction arrives before the
  deviation the ladder permits at the plausible cumulative outflow
  exceeds the tail gate.  Formally: for the worst plausible outflow S
  over tau_I, the ladder's n(S) satisfies 1 / (1 - eps - n(S) delta)
  within the deviation the gates allow, with the shadow-coupled K
  restoring rho toward 1 inside tau_I.  The catalogue's x4 grind-down
  with the full cast (parity held, K 0.936, 17% railed) is the empirical
  frontier at today's NAV with an implicit CPMM ladder; wave 3c
  re-measures it across (delta, phi) and wave 3h with the seeder.
  Sources of R_w beyond NAV: the desk's capitalization grant and
  treasury accrual (existing), and the cat-bond vault (D6, optional).
  Nothing else.


7.7 The facility
────────────────

  Participant i has limit L_i, drawn d_i, thresholds (x_i, y_i), and a
  deployment cap m_i.  Hazard of issuing rises with the premium beyond
  x_i and with headroom (L_i - d_i); hazard of retiring rises with the
  discount beyond y_i and with d_i.  Aggregate utilization u = sum d_i /
  sum L_i is a fast actuator's position and enters I_c.  Pro-cyclicality
  (4.6) is bounded by K: a premium regime raises K and headroom, so the
  facility can issue more into the premium – which is the correct
  direction – and a discount regime lowers K, which forces retirement on
  holders near their limit, again the correct direction.  The structural
  risk is D8: a participant whose "insured asset" is fictitious issues
  free BUCK into every premium with no retirement obligation that bites
  (9.1).


7.8 The perp funding lever
──────────────────────────

  A perpetual settled on a medianized bvib TWAP, with funding f = g(e)
  paid continuously by the side positioned AGAINST par (longs-bvib pay
  when bvib > 1; shorts-bvib pay when bvib < 1) to the house, which is
  the monetary book.  Because the band bounds bvib, the perp's payoff is
  bounded, and its equilibrium funding is the market's price for holding
  a band-break bet – the currency board's automatic interest-rate
  mechanism applied to speculators only.  Solvency condition for
  listing: for the defended excursion envelope measured in wave 2 (peak
  x duration at x1..x4), the funding + eps + fee surge an attacker pays
  to express the raid through the perp must exceed the payout the
  bounded excursion can return; OI is capped against the defense budget.
  If it does not price safely, it is not listed.


7.9 Predictions the catalogue can falsify
─────────────────────────────────────────

  1. *Band integrity.* With the strong-side undertaking at eps = 3%, p =
     2, the x4 squeeze ($40M in a day) never takes bvib below 1/(1 +
     p*eps) ~ 0.943 beyond the conversion-capacity window, and K ends
     interior (< 0.90).  Today: 0.69 and 0.95 railed.
  2. *Depositor fairness.* With the stress fee, depositor realized P&L
     in every x4 cell is >= its value in the x1 cell of the same arm.
     Today: -$1.99M / -$2.72M.
  3. *Attacker cost.* Whale P&L <= -2 p eps S on every
     undertaking-bounded squeeze; the whale never ends flat on a dump
     (today: $0.00M at x4).
  4. *Mid-ranging.* With lambda_d at its knee, the desk's inventory
     returns to within 10% of zero inside 1.5 tau_I after every x1/x2
     injection, and K's rail margin at x4 is at least that of the "all"
     mix today (K end <= 0.81).  With lambda_d = 0 the desk's inventory
     grows monotonically on the drift arm (runaway one).
  5. *Benign-flow health.* Quiet-regime tracking error of every basket
     pool against the truth-implied price is unchanged within x1.25
     under the undertakings, the seeder, and the directional fee.
  6. *The middle of the ladder.* A facility population scaled to the
     "plentiful un-leveraged asset base" cuts the x1 squeeze peak and
     AUC below the "credit" mix's and REALIZES (not merely marks) its
     harvest on the retire leg.
  7. *Duration pricing.* With the perp, the whale's accumulation-phase
     cost rises with the days it takes, and the detector fires at least
     20 days before the dump with a false-positive rate on the control
     below one event per two years.
  8. *The ladder has no cliff.* Across the (delta, phi) grid on the x4
     dump and grind-down, no cell shows the weak-side reserve fraction
     rho below 0.1 while bvib stays inside the first edge; the whale's
     average sale price worsens monotonically with cumulative size in
     every cell; and the ladder cells reach a LOWER peak deviation per
     dollar of reserve spent than the flat-edge (delta = 0) cell at the
     same eps.

  Any of these failing changes a decision; 9.6 says which.

8.1 Instrument: the markout / LVR ledger, with the basket-side columns (3a)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  /Definitions./ For each swap s in constituent pool i at time t, with
  the basket-LP side's signed base-token flow q_lp and execution price
  p_exec:

  markout_D(s) = q_lp x (p_ref(t + D) - p_exec(t))

  Reference for the COMMON-MODE ledger: PAR (reversion to 1 at horizon
  D).  For the DIFFERENTIAL ledger: the truth-implied TOKEN/BUCK, and
  (sim-only) the exogenous reference series.  Horizons D in {next tick,
  1 d, 5 d}.  Per pool per window: fees_i, adverse_i (sum of markouts),
  netlp_i = fees_i + adverse_i, lvr_bench_i (differential only),
  carry_ratio_i = fees_i / max(lvr_bench_i, -adverse_i).

  /Attribution./ Every sim swap has a known sender class: whale
  (accumulate / dump / re-buy separately), BUCK-unaware arbs, director
  keeper, excursion agents by base, DM depositors, debtors, and – new –
  the undertakings, the facility, the seeder, the house.  Toxicity of a
  class = mean markout per dollar of its flow.  Expected: whale dump-day
  flow deeply negative for the basket; benign arbs mildly negative
  gross, ~zero net of fee; the keeper netting to ~fees; excursion agents
  negative for the pool ledger by design (the defense partly funded by
  the basket's own book – confirmed in outline by the x1 dump: defenders
  +$0.26M, basket NAV -$0.75M).

  /Where it lives./ `snapshot.py': swap-event capture with class tags
  and deferred markout at t + D; `basketNav' / `treasuryBuck' /
  `dmProfitUsd' promoted into the catalogue summary.  `eqmetrics.py': a
  `markout' panel beside `excursion_windows' / `excursion_response'.
  `catalogue.py': grid columns – basket fees, adverse, netlp,
  carry_ratio (worst pool), NAV USD and NAV-in-baskets delta vs
  undefended, treasury delta, depositor realized delta, whale toxicity.

  /Cells./ The existing 86 cells, re-summarized from their vectors
  (`--report-only' after the metric lands); no new physics.

  /Gate./ None new for 3a; it is the instrument.  Deliverable: the table
  in 3.3 produced by `catalogue.py' rather than by hand.

8.4 Implementation notes for the sim
────────────────────────────────────

  • *UndertakingAgent* is the fastest honest stand-in for D3.  SimLP can
    mint against a zero-premium credit with an arbitrarily large face
    and hold both sides of a V3 position; the basket-rotation execution
    path already trades TOKEN <-> BUCK in every constituent pool in
    `basketAmount' proportions.  Standing orders at par +/- eps in
    bundle units are then eight range orders (one per pool) re-struck
    daily, or, more simply, a daily check that fires when bvib crosses
    the edge and trades up to C_leg per pool.  The agent must book its
    issued and absorbed BUCK so 3d can read I_d.
  • *The shadow controller* needs Solidity: `BuckKControllerDirect'
    reads `basket.basketValueInBuck()'; a `Shadow' variant reading a new
    `shadowValueInBuck()' on `BuckBasketOps' (bvib + lambda x
    `monetaryDeviationOffset()' / depth) is small, and the sim deploys
    whatever the repo compiles.  Until the undertakings are contract-
    level, the agent's booked inventory must be pushed into the offset
    (a governance setter on the ops shell for the sim, removed later).
  • *The facility* is the credit base with two changes: the issue leg
    deposits into the basket instead of selling into BUCK/USDC, and the
    retire leg redeems the receipt and repays.  Utilization telemetry is
    a counter in `snapshot.py' (add it in the same commit – the ops
    doc's "the agent is invisible" lesson).
  • *The perp* is a Python-side ledger (positions, mark = medianized
    bvib, funding accrual) whose funding settles on-chain as BUCK
    transfers between the whale's proxy and the house's proxy each tick;
    the whale gains a `via = "perp"' knob.  No contract until 3f wins.
  • *The treasury seeder* is `BuckPoolInvestorAgent' with the range
    struck from the current BUCK/USDC tick toward the truth-implied
    ideal, a withdraw rule keyed to SimLP-independent depth, and the ops
    doc's orientation checklist applied.
  • *The directional fee* is a fee schedule in the pool adapter used by
    the router quoter and the sim's swap path; the v4 hook comes later
    behind the venue seam of `BASKET-REDESIGN.md' 4.
  • *Telemetry discipline* (ops doc): counters into `snapshot.py' in the
    same commit as the agent; per-agent inventory through `arb_state';
    several seeds before any peak-damping number is quoted.

9 Risks, open questions, and what would change the decisions
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

9.1 D8 and the facility
───────────────────────

  The facility lets latent credit issue into premiums.  A participant
  whose insured asset is fictitious (D8: `createCredit' is
  permissionless and zero-premium credit is exempt from the funding
  gate) issues free BUCK into every premium with a retirement obligation
  that never bites (no real asset, no reason to close).  This is the
  oversupply flood of `REBALANCING-EQ.org' recommendation 4, automated.
  The facility must be restricted to vetted insurers' credit (the
  on-chain solvency-plus-rating scheme the master doc names as the
  mainnet blocker) or, until then, to a whitelisted insurer set in sim
  and pilot.  3h measures the flood with and without the facility to
  size the exposure.


9.2 Band-break credibility on the weak side
───────────────────────────────────────────

  The weak side is NAV-bounded.  Bertola-Caballero says an imperfectly
  credible edge destabilizes near it, and Krugman-Flood-Garber say the
  attack is timed to the day a flat edge stops being honored.  The
  ladder (D3 refinement, 7.4) is the structural mitigation: there is no
  day on which the weak side stops being honored, only successively
  poorer prices, so there is no discontinuity to time and no size to
  probe – the RULE (the tranche schedule against the on-chain reserve)
  is what is announced.  The residual risk moves to the ladder's
  parameters: a delta too small reproduces the cliff, a delta too large
  lets a modest outflow force a deep deviation.  Wave 3c's (delta, phi)
  grid, the perp pricing band-break bets, and a reserve visibly large
  relative to tau_I outflow (7.5) are the answers.


9.3 Sensor damping by the invisible cast
────────────────────────────────────────

  Private defenders damp bvib and K cannot see their books.  Their
  capital is finite and their positions temporary by design, so the lag
  is bounded by their size; but a very large private cast makes K late.
  The shadow term could include an estimate of private inventory from
  flow attribution; it is deliberately left out until 3d shows the
  desk-only term is insufficient.


9.4 Conversion capacity and the one-day trough
──────────────────────────────────────────────

  The bundle path is unlimited but the convenience path and every
  constituent-pool leg are bounded by C_leg for liveness.  A one-day
  squeeze larger than a day's capacity still troughs.  The directional
  fee is the tick-speed answer; if 3b shows it insufficient, the
  remaining lever is a larger per-leg bound with a redemption guard that
  tolerates it (a TWAP-window-aware guard rather than a flat
  `maxLegBp').


9.5 Governance abandonment
──────────────────────────

  The SNB failure mode is available to any governance that can move par.
  Par is definitional here and the undertakings are rules, not
  operations; the residual is a governance that changes eps, p, lambda
  or the basket under pressure.  Time-locks on those parameters, and the
  announcement discipline of Hong Kong's technical measures, are the
  answer; they are policy, not code.

11 Glossary
═══════════

  • *bvib*: basketValueInBuck, the BUCK cost of one basket bundle; the
    controller's process variable; > 1 = BUCK cheap (discount), < 1 =
    BUCK dear (premium).
  • *common mode / differential mode*: BUCK against the bundle (K's job)
    vs constituent against constituent (the director's job); the mean
    and the differences of the same tick-EMA ladders.
  • *the desk*: `BuckBasketOps'; Q1 absorb, Q2 retire, Q3 supply, Q4
     issue.
  • *the undertakings*: the strong-side (sell BUCK, unlimited) and
    weak-side (buy BUCK, NAV-bounded) standing offers at par +/- eps in
    bundle units; the currency board.
  • *the weak-side ladder*: the weak side struck as tranches at
    successively deeper discounts (eps + (k-1) delta), each sized at a
    fraction phi of the reserve remaining; no cliff, superlinear
    attacker cost, and a deviation that gauges reserve stress.
  • *the facility*: the credit auto-pay-down facility; latent credit
    issuing into premiums and retiring into discounts on user-set
    thresholds.
  • *the shadow bvib*: bvib plus the inventory the visible fast
    actuators have absorbed minus issued, scaled; what K sees.
  • *the seeder*: the treasury's temporary BUCK/USDC position from the
    current price toward the ideal.
  • *the house*: the monetary book as counterparty and funding recipient
    of the bvib perp.
  • *the cast*: the private excursion agents of the catalogue (usdc,
    buck, credit, basket, crb).
  • *manufactured reversion*: the property that par is definitional and
    K plus the cast force bvib back to it – what makes selling
    common-mode convexity sound.
