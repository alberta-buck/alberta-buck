#+TITLE: Household decisions, settlement and observations
#+OPTIONS: ^:nil toc:2

The Python and JavaScript households share the policy in
=household_policy.py= / =household-policy.js= and operate on the same canonical
Buck contracts. The adaptive policy is the default; always-invest and
retirement policies remain explicit comparisons. The savings sandbox and
five-year runner use these existing households and population arrivals.
The [[file:doc/review/bucknet-economy-implementation.org][accounting implementation plan]] is the authoritative completion checklist.

Household wallets remain Ordinary. The basket they invest in is a Carrying
service: its nominal BUCK is separate from shareholders' net value. Receipt
marks and redemption minimums use net BUCK; carrying fees cannot inflate
reported household wealth or create credit collateral.

An insured household now defaults to one asset and one BuckCredit NFT.
An explicit =asset_count= portfolio (1--32) divides its declared aggregate
collateral across that many facilities; the last receives the integer
remainder. Investors have no insured asset/NFT. Four-asset fixtures retain
mixed-age and distinct insurer-pool accounting coverage. Changing the count
does not move any other keyed random threshold or change total collateral.

* Current execution boundary

Setup, personal portfolio books, selected-facility operations and guarded
markets are implemented. An assessed refinancing plan can now acquire a
bounded net BUCK reserve when needed, then execute
through =start_net_refinance= / =startNetRefinance= with a checkpoint after
each authenticated receipt. The explicit calendar component now schedules
arrival capital, wages, bills and mortgage payments with receipt recovery.
Normal household decisions remain gated while automatic policy scheduling,
saving/paydown and exits are ported. Save/resume is deferred; it is not a
prerequisite for uninterrupted household or five-year runs. An isolated funded-plan test is not a
completed household economy or five-year stability result.

Insurance activation (=joined=) and a completed mortgage closing are separate.
=refinance_closed= / =refinanceClosed= becomes true only when a confirmed bank
receipt applies the first issuance-backed mortgage allocation. Before that,
new-credit assessments still include the closing cost. Later tranches do not
pay it again. A completed wallet-only prepayment does not switch insurance.

After a proved bank refusal, the machine exposes =payment_due= and retains
its confirmed issue/export and bank allocation. New refinancing is refused
until =retry_net_refinance_payment= / =retryNetRefinancePayment= completes
that bank leg. The retry keeps its amount and cash-buffer bounds and shares
normal receipt recovery: an unknown hash never permits another send. If the
mortgage has fallen below the saved principal, that allocation needs explicit
reassessment. Calendar flows and buyback reconciliation remain serialized.
These fields also appear in personal observations and decision events.

the primary-world, household, receipt and reporting tests:

#+end_src

* Calendar, income and mortgage counterexample

Call =advance_net_calendar(d, day, counters)= or
=await advanceNetCalendar(world, day)= on arrival, then at least on each
30-day payday. Daily observation is supported and required by the eventual
normal runner. The integer day must match elapsed EVM time; changing an
actor's day label cannot substitute for advancing the world. Missing a payday
is an explicit error because today's activated insurance coverage cannot
reconstruct an unobserved historical premium.

Both Python runners publish one shared household epoch. Their ticks use
absolute day/fractional-tick targets, including tick counts that do not divide
86,400 evenly. The JS =DayClock= pins each day to one shared epoch; the
wheel's settlement waits and transaction seconds consume that day's time,
instead of being added to every later day. A whole-day work overrun fails
explicitly. Households arriving later bind the same epoch. Standalone
component fixtures without a runner bind a local epoch on arrival.
Use =day_step=1= for household economies: coarse diagnostic strides cannot
skip an arrival or payday and then invent the missing historical coverage.

The calendar keeps independent actual and counterfactual mortgage books.
External USD interest is =floor(balance * APR_ppb / (365 * 1e9))= once per
bank day, with payments after that day's interest. This fixes observation-
dependent rounding in the former floating-point advance. It does not
calculate BUCK cash, demurrage or Jubilee relief. Shared five-year debt
vectors use an independent rational-arithmetic oracle.

Arrival capital is an endowment, not income. Each later payday queues one
wage mint, living costs, the uncovered external insurance premium and the
fixed mortgage payment. Wages require one exact USDC mint event; expenses
use the existing exact settlement-token evidence. Insufficient cash leaves
living/insurance arrears and outstanding debt; it never creates spendable
cash. Counterfactual wages/bills/loan payments follow their original schedule
even while a real operation is unresolved.

Only the authenticated bank allocation changes the actual mortgage.
Its block timestamp determines the effective payment day, including for
refinancing. Recovering old evidence replays interest from that payment day,
not from the discovery day. Known hashes recover once; missing or mismatched
evidence stays pending, and a hashless outcome cannot authorize another
send. A proved revert permits a later retry. Recurring and discretionary
operations share one owner dispatcher: earlier refinance/buyback recovery
finishes before queued recurring sends. A confirmed but unfinished buyback
target is an intention, not an unresolved transaction: due wages/bills run
before another retirement attempt, without spending its protected BUCKs or
discarding the target. Refinancing cannot start while the calendar has
queued work.

Personal rows expose =calendar= status: epoch, current day, queued periods, phase/hash,
=income_due= and =mortgage_provisional=. Income due includes a pending arrival
endowment while opening; it is pending accounting,
not extra liquid wealth: its mint might already have executed. A provisional
mortgage awaits bank evidence and/or its block date. The existing public
decision stream records calendar transitions, due/effective dates, actual
accounts, arrears and counterfactual balances. These supply future household
charts and decision tables without a new reporting runner. The automatic
policy loop remains a completion gate; full-state import is a later task.

* Market estimates for reserve funding

The Python SDK's =observe_net_market= / =NetMarketQuote= and JavaScript's
=observeNetMarket= / =netPurchaseExpected= / =netPurchaseCost= /
=netSaleExpected= share integer vectors. They pin price, active virtual
reserves, pool nominal/net cash and fee to one block, and require the expected
pair, fee and verified Carrying mode. The refinancing event uses this same
reader rather than another valuation implementation.

Sales estimate nominal input and USDC output. A buy estimate discounts
nominal output by the pool's current net/gross ratio. Exact-net acquisition
cost first grosses up for carried fees, then rounds input/trading fees up
with a small raw-unit cushion. No local quote is represented by null/None,
including exhausted active reserves or zero deliverable net value.
These are constant-active-liquidity estimates; crossed ticks and subsequent
prices can change execution. The household must retain a maximum USDC
budget and use the actual net-output guard or positive sale minimum. A quote
is neither a fair-value oracle nor evidence of an executed trade.

External premiums decline only for the activated nominal share of the
household's declared insured value. Both calendar implementations compute
the remaining premium as =ceil(full premium * (face - activated) / face)=.
The counterfactual keeps the full premium. The first small issuance no longer
removes a whole asset's insurance expense; retiring coverage restores that
expense proportionally. The current household uses one common insurance
rate and a fixed declared asset value across its explicitly split facilities.

Quoted refinancing accepts an explicit incremental annual USD insurance
saving for that candidate. It defaults to zero: marked BUCK coverage alone
cannot determine an external insurer's USD valuation. Existing coverage and
wallet-only prepayments add no saving; top-ups can save on new coverage even
after joining. A wallet-only prepayment also has no insurance switching cost.
The legacy comparison retains its historical whole-asset switch separately.

* Observed Jubilee rate

=net_credit_ops.quote_global_relief_rate()= /
=netCreditOps.quoteGlobalReliefRate()= now supplies the fund-wide observation
needed by a fresh issuer. At one pinned block it reads aggregate eligible
claim seconds, Jubilee net cash, the guarded last-published position mark
and the separate shared block allowance. The annualized ratio is
=fund marked net value * seconds/year / eligible BUCK-seconds=.
Deferred matured principal remains in that denominator, as defined by Buck.

No aggregate age means no observed rate. Missing/unsafe marks and an unpaid
maturity checkpoint propagate as failures. A selected older NFT can have its
relief capped at principal; this global ratio avoids mistaking that capped
sample for the whole fund's rate. The current ratio is an input to a future
forecast, while the block allowance remains the actual liquidity constraint.

* Refinancing state machine

The read-only =assess_net_refinance= / =assessNetRefinance= entry point now
chooses a plan from actual per-NFT quotes. Its default relief forecast uses
the current observed global fund/age ratio. Wallet and insurer-pool carry
use the modeled idle-cash annual decline, =expm1(-0.02)=; this is a forecast
approximation checked against the native decay kernel, not a token-balance
calculation. An actively managed insurer needs an explicit return forecast.
No aggregate claim age uses a labeled conservative zero relief forecast;
an observed zero rate remains distinguishable from missing age. Unsafe marks
and maturity failures propagate.

Supply all three annual =expected_relief_rate=, =expected_refund_growth= and
=expected_wallet_growth= values to use an explicit comparison instead.
Partial overrides are rejected. Default context records the source block,
actual marked assets/age/allowance and forecast basis. All forecasts are
assumptions for cost comparison, not guaranteed fund payments or ROI. It returns
=plan= (or null/None), =verdict= and =context=; assessment changes neither
accounts nor the household's ledger. Normal external keeper/controller work
must precede a decision, especially after a time jump. Otherwise Buck's
own K refresh can invalidate a boundary quote; an atomic refusal is the
correct result, and does not justify relaxing K.

The menu tries the existing five size fractions, each facility's exact
remaining activated capacity, and the SDK's bounded coverage options. It
compares new issuance, existing-wallet prepayment and mixed funding. The
configured utilization and price-impact allowance bound all choices. Each
premium uses its own observed cash-based insurer refund fraction, and each
coverage increment saves only its corresponding external insurance cost.
The policy can refinance at a modest trading loss when the interest and
insurance saving justify it; a mortgage refinance does not require BUCK to
be trading at a premium.

A reserve purchase counts its acquisition friction and annual opportunity /
demurrage cost, while retaining the reserve's owned mark value. A previously
held ordinary reserve can have declined through demurrage: retain its still
available net value, and replenish only when a current issuance quote
requires it. Zero-premium activated-room issuance does not acquire another
premium buffer. Switching cost and penalty must fit old USDC above the cash
buffer; the sale minimum goes to mortgage principal. This keeps bank costs
separate from the forecast trading loss. The actual payment is rounded up
in raw units; forecasts remain whole-unit floating-point comparisons.

The context supplies rejection counts, quote/option counts, explicit
forecasts and the selected cost breakdown for a decision table. The pinned
books/market are reused; a changed observation block aborts assessment.
This finite menu is not an exhaustive optimizer. The normal household loop
still needs its calendar/surplus/exit wiring.
An assessed plan can be reviewed or passed to the same execution machine:

#+begin_src python
result = agent.assess_net_refinance(d, day)  # observed fund + modeled idle-cash forecasts
if result["plan"] is not None:
    agent.start_net_refinance(d, result["plan"], day, counters,
                             context=result["context"])
#+end_src

The plan selects one assessed NFT, new spendable issuance, a bounded BUCK
export, protected BUCK/USDC buffers and a maximum external principal payment.
It includes switching costs and an exact rational prepayment penalty. All
financial quantities are integers in raw six-decimal token units.

| State | Requires | Confirmed effect |
|-------+----------+------------------|
| fund (optional) | A positive net BUCK target and maximum USDC budget, funded above the protected cash buffer | The existing guard buys full net reserve value directly from the Carrying pool. Receipt evidence commits the actual payment/delivery once; no NFT debt or mortgage payment. |
| issue | Selected owned NFT; freshly rechecked premium/coverage/principal/pool and retained reserve; existing SDK and on-chain authorization/K checks | Actual new NFT principal includes the full-value premium; record that premium. No mortgage payment. |
| export | Actual wallet money can satisfy the sale while retaining the BUCK reserve; positive USDC minimum | Direct user-paid sale produces authenticated USDC. NFT principal and external mortgage are unchanged. |
| mortgage | Bank amount fits the remaining USDC above the cash buffer | One exact USDC transfer pays principal plus switching cost plus the rounded-up prepayment penalty. Only its principal allocation reduces the mortgage. |
| pending | A send's outcome is uncertain | Save the phase, terms and hash if known. No new send or book entry until the matching successful receipt is authenticated. A missing/mismatched receipt stays pending. |
| waiting | A pre-submission issuance observation is unavailable | Retry the read later without another reserve purchase or issue submission. |
| refused | A proven revert, changed issuance terms, or a changed balance violates a buffer | End this attempt. Earlier confirmed purchases, issuance, exports and payments remain real; do not claim a mortgage payment that did not occur. |
| complete | Every required phase was confirmed and applied | One refinancing count; actual principal/cost counters and remaining balances agree with receipts. A repeated recovery call does no work. |

Set =reserve_buy_net= and =reserve_buy_max_usdc= together for optional funding;
both default to zero. A reserve-shortfall candidate is accepted only with
enough planned net funding. After funding, the machine rechecks the exact
selected terms. A change requires reassessment of the retained holdings,
rather than silently broadening the original plan. Trading approvals must
already be prepared; an invalid plan never submits an approval transaction.

A wallet-only prepayment skips funding/issuance and cannot claim a switch of insurance.
The bank leg combines costs in one simulated-bank transfer and records their
allocations separately. This eliminates partial fee-payment states. The
simulation's bank is a standard-USDC sink; this does not establish how an
external lender credits a real loan.

The caller serializes each owner's work. Existing pending buybacks and
authorizations block a new refinance. Recovery runs before new household
income or decisions. Receipt evidence assumes the local EVM's immediate
finality; full saved-world/pending-state import remains separate work.

* Data for charts and decision tables

Daily personal books provide cash, actual issued principal, remaining basket
basis/mark/ROI, insurer refund marks, Jubilee claims, the external mortgage
counterfactual and cash-flow totals. They retain their observation block.
The insured asset is common to both financial comparisons and is excluded
from financial net worth; basket returns precede exit costs.

The household keeps one exact append-only event stream for refinancing
and calendar transitions:

| Field | Meaning |
|-------+---------|
| schema, member, decision, seq | Format version and stable per-household decision/event identifiers |
| day, block, phase, status | Calendar time, observation block and current transition posture |
| kind, due_day, effective_day | Calendar events use kind=calendar, retain their scheduled day and record the bank payment's effective day when applicable |
| plan, context, limits | Exact selected terms, caller's economic explanation and resolved randomized thresholds |
| market | Pinned K/BVIB, exact nominal USDC/BUCK price fraction, fee tier and pool nominal/net balances; unavailable is explicit |
| accounts | Wallet cash, issued principal, credit limit, premiums, reserve/buffer levels, custody cash, per-NFT face/activation/principal/age, actual/counterfactual cash/debt/interest, accrued bills, calendar status and comparison ratios |
| basket | At decision start, actual receipt basis, mark, shares and ROI; no receipts is distinct from an unavailable mark |
| operation, amounts, transaction_hash, receipt_block | A confirmed monetary transition and its evidence; bank principal/cost allocations remain separate |
| reason | Why an attempt is pending or refused |

Money, scaled values, NFT IDs, shares, rational numerators/denominators and
BUCK-seconds use decimal strings. Only finite comparison ratios and threshold
estimates use floating point. Undefined ratios are null; a failed observation
is an explicit =unavailable= record. Ratios and marks do not authorize a
transaction. In a confirmed event, =operation= names the completed action and
=phase= names the next state.

Python emits only new events in =frames[].household_events=. This field is
retained in both the savings server's full and lite JSON streams and their
existing replay history. Successful frame capture advances its event cursors;
a failed required capture cannot discard transitions. =SIM_ACTS=none= only
disables the older compact action log, not these monetary records. Join
=agent/member= to =meta.telemetry.agents[].knobs= for the resolved profile.
The frame's per-member =machine= field exposes current phase/status/cursor.

JavaScript exposes =agent.householdEventsSince(seq)=, returning a detached
copy of events after that cursor. Store the last returned =seq= and request
only new rows. Personal daily ledger rows include the current =machine=
status. A UI can group by member for an individual timeline or aggregate
confirmed amounts and current books across the population. It must not count
=planned=, =pending= or a sale as mortgage retirement.

The current event scope is refinancing. Extend the same records to declined
economic decisions, saving, paydown and exits as those transitions
are ported. No separate dashboard or second simulation runner is introduced.
