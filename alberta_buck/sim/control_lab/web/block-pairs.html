Controlled signals

Three-phase is clean. Noise20 and noise80 add independent TOKEN disturbances of 20 or 80 basis points per observation. Flat-noise80 removes the sine. The two replay venues share these disturbances.

Separate evidence

Spot ratios, filtered ratios, rates and empirical error allowances appear in separate panes. Positive A/B motion means A gains relative to B. An allowance is not a statistical confidence interval.

Unchanged trading

The replay, linked market and full basket stages use the existing trading policies. New signals have no trading authority. Matched observer-off controls check that this phase leaves their outcomes unchanged.

Timing and reproduction

The experiment uses a 12-hour response time and a two-day readiness period. Pools retain prices between the six-hour external updates, so the smooth reference is a benchmark rather than the exact continuous pool path. Rates are plotted at publication time; the JSON retains each interval midpoint.
