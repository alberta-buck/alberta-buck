* 1. What this document is

The design owner's goal of 2026-09-27: bring the ideas of the last two
months together and find

- (A) the best approach we have to maintaining equilibrium, under normal
  price regimes and under bursts of organic and inorganic activity (a
  BUCK demand shock, a BUCK supply shock, and the rest of the catalogue);
- (B) the best BuckBasket architecture for harvesting reversion to the
  mean, and -- where it can -- opportunistic arbitrage;

and then build a MINIMAL implementation -- agents, contracts and the
contract-invokers that call the contracts' ticks -- that makes the best
available BuckBasket a savings facility in the JS + HTML sandbox, where
simulated time can be advanced to show commodities cycling, arbitrage
forcing TOKEN prices to track real commodity prices, and the BuckBasket
exploiting BUCK / USDC / TOKEN divergences for its LP holders.

This document is the map from here to there.  It selects from the
documents of record (=CARRY-CONVEXITY.org=, =K-AND-OPS.org=,
=BASKET-WHEEL.org=, =ORGANIC-SCALE.org=, =REBALANCING-EQ.org=,

* 3. (A) Equilibrium: the architecture the evidence points to

Four layers, separated by timescale and by what each is allowed to see,
over an organic load:

| layer | mechanism                                     | acts on                            | timescale      | sees                                  |
|-------+-----------------------------------------------+------------------------------------+----------------+---------------------------------------|
| L0    | the consistency wheel                          | venue coherence (and the gauge)    | blocks         | the three pools of each triangle      |
| L1    | undertakings (strong side unlimited, weak side ladder), stress fee, facility | the PRICE, inside the band | ticks to days | bvib against its own edge |
| L2    | the persistence seam (dwell test -> Q2 burn / Q4 acknowledge) | a book that will not go away | weeks | its own dwell |
| L3    | K, price-only                                  | the credit-backed STOCK            | months         | raw bvib                              |
| load  | the organic population (retirees now; savers, businesses, demography next) | produces the flows | -- | carry rates |

How each burst is met:

| burst                                   | catalogue arm(s)                  | L0                         | L1                                          | L2 / L3                                        |
|-----------------------------------------+-----------------------------------+----------------------------+---------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------|
| BUCK DEMAND shock (buyers want BUCK)    | squeeze x1 / x4; cohort-add       | keeps venues coherent      | strong side ISSUES at par + eps, unlimited  | persistent: Q4 acknowledges; K rises and offers |
| BUCK SUPPLY shock (holders dump BUCK)   | dump x1 / x4; cohort-step         | keeps venues coherent      | weak side ABSORBS down the ladder; stress fee on duress exits | persistent: Q2 burns; K lowers, withdrawing the offer |
| a grind (slow one-way pressure)         | grind-down / grind-up             | --                         | the ladder's tranches pace it               | the seam's dwell decides "persistent"          |
| book-loading (an attack on the books)   | bookload                          | --                         | the local skews unwind the book             | K never reads the book (F2): the attack cannot move K |
| a sensor fault                          | guardtrip                         | --                         | hold the last good cap, flag stale (decision 9) | --                                          |
| liquidity leaving                       | lpexit                            | cycle sizes shrink with depth | caps shrink with NAV                     | --                                             |
| organic bursts (harvest season, renewal waves, commodity moves) | ORGANIC-SCALE item 6 (to build) | as above | as above | as above |

* 4. (B) Harvest: three legs, one per mispricing

A basket's excess return over holding its constituents comes from three
different mispricings, each with its own harvester, timescale and risk:

| leg | the mispricing                                        | the harvester                         | risk                                           | measured                                              |
|-----+-------------------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------|
| 1   | venue INCONSISTENCY: TOKEN/BUCK vs TOKEN/USDC x BUCK/USDC | the wheel's consistency cycles       | none on the cycle (atomic, realized-profit check); a gauge side effect | ~2-3% of NAV/yr on the thin, noisy cast (BASKET-WHEEL 7.1) |
| 2   | commodity RELATIVE VALUE: constituent vs constituent, vs its own trend | the director's rebalancing (vrate / factor / pairs) | trend whipsaw (BTC x9)                         | +322..+423 bp/yr synthetic; -1..-4% CAGR vs hold in the 2020-25 history (BASKET-REBALANCE 4) |
| 3   | BUCK vs its basket (bvib vs par)                       | the undertakings / desk / ladder      | inventory that persists; the monetary rules govern it | defenders +$1.77M, depositors +$0.67M on the x4 dump under A (cycle 1) |

Plus the base: fees on the basket's own pools, less the LVR the wheel
does not recapture.  Legs 1 and 2 are the SAVINGS basket's; leg 3 is the
monetary system's and belongs to the Ops shell.

*The basket of record for the savings facility (proposed):* the ProRata
shell with the Uniswap V3 venue facet (the eqworld demo already runs it;
~8.4 KB of headroom), a director (vrate or pairs), and the wheel with the
arb kind plus the upkeep kinds (director poke, treasury sweep, the
controller's =compute=) -- deployed INSIDE the ProRata shell where it
fits, beside it otherwise (BASKET-WHEEL 8.4).  The Ops quadrants and the
undertakings are the monetary add-on, not the savings minimum.

What is NOT known yet, and decides (B):

1. *Do legs 1 and 2 add?*  The wheel re-prices the TOKEN/BUCK pools the
   director reads; the director's trades open gaps the wheel closes.
   Experiment E3.
2. *Trend.*  Leg 2's history loses to hold in a secular trend; the
   savings story must show both a reverting regime and a trending one.

* 6. The demonstration

** 6.1 The starting point

- *The public sandbox* (=core/js/sandbox/=) is an identity, credit and
  BUCK/USDC market tool in an in-browser EVM (Tevm): Buck, BuckCredit,
  the identity registry, =BuckKControllerDirect= (K held at 0.75), a

  TOKEN/USDC to a seeded walk, =ParityArb= routing USDC triangles so

  Its limits: one constituent; a synthetic walk, not commodity prices;
  savers only as agents (no direct deposit or redeem); a receipt value
  that leaves out the position's fees; and Tevm keeps every block in
  memory (OOM beyond ~6 simulated months in the Node runners).

** 6.2 The story it must show

A saver deposits into the BuckBasket and advances time.  Commodity prices
move as they really did (or through a synthetic cycle); a market pins
TOKEN/USDC to them; external arbitrage and the basket's wheel keep
TOKEN/BUCK coherent with TOKEN/USDC and BUCK/USDC; the director sells
what has run ahead and buys what has fallen behind; the saver's receipt
is shown against holding the commodities and against USDC, with the
wheel's captured value and the director's premium broken out.  Two
buttons inject a BUCK demand shock and a BUCK supply shock, and the page
shows the price leave the band and come back.

** 6.4 The engineering risks, named now

1. *Tevm memory.*  Every block stays in memory; a multi-year run dies.
   Candidates: mine many transactions per block, dump / reload state to
   drop history, or cap a session's horizon.  A spike before the build.
2. *Receipt value with fees.*  The venue must expose the position's
   uncollected fees, or the page understates the harvest.
3. *Bundle size.*  The basket, venue, director and wheel add artifacts
   to a 2.4 MB hosted script.
4. *The wheel in Solidity* is the one new contract: forge tests for the
   mixin layout, the amortized scan, the flash mint's invariant (supply
   unchanged, principal back), the realized-profit revert, and a
   manipulation test (a pool moved first only pays the basket).

* 7a. Step 5, revised: two tiers of sandbox (design owner, 2026-09-27)

The Tevm spike (=doc/reports/tevm-memory.org=) solved the in-browser memory
ceiling (prune the history, trim the system rings: a flat heap) and found
the CPU one: every read deep-copies the state, ~100-190 s of wall time
per simulated day for six constituents -- a year in 10-20 hours.  So the
design owner's ruling: SMALL, SIMPLER SANDBOXES stay hosted in the browser
-- the sandbox we have now, preserved as it is -- and the COMPLEX ones run
on server-hosted pyrevm.  The savings demonstration is the second kind:

- *The world* runs in =alberta_buck.sim.server= (pyrevm; one world per
  session; frames over WebSocket; controls at each day boundary; JSON-RPC
  to the world's chain), the full stack of section 6.3 with the real
  contracts: the basket, its director, the undertakings and the desk,
  the wheel (Solidity), the market (whale, arbs), savers and retirees,
  six constituents on the real 2020-2025 histories -- or on reverting
  series with the same volatilities (the toggle).
- *The page* is a new Savings tab in the sandbox, beside the existing
  in-browser tabs: it opens a session, plays / pauses / sets the pace,
  charts the world from its frames, and acts as the visitor's wallet
  through the session's RPC (a faucet, a deposit into the basket, the
  receipt's value, a redemption), with the demand / supply shock buttons
  and the L1 / L2 toggle as controls.
- *Serving* is one server process per port behind the cloudflared tunnel
  (the tunnel's public-hostname rules are the design owner's dashboard).

Server work: a lean demo cast at interactive speed; the reverting price
mode for the six-token world; pause / resume, shock and chain-toggle
controls; a =sim_info= method (the world's addresses for the page); lite
frames (no per-agent telemetry).  Page work: the tab, its charts and the
visitor's wallet flow; a Playwright test against a local server.

*Built (2026-09-27).*  All of the above, and what building it taught:

- *The chain is shared fairly.*  A released =threading.Lock= is not handed
  to its waiter: the sim thread re-took the chain at once and a visitor's
  deposit could wait forever.  Between days the sim thread now lets queued
  RPC through, and while a page holds an rpc channel it waits half a second
  after each frame (and each answer) for the calls a frame prompts.  A JSON-RPC
  batch is answered under one hold, so the page's faucet + approve + deposit,
  each with a preflight =eth_call= (its refusal comes back with the basket's
  reason) and its receipt, land together between two days.
- *Signed transactions are real ones.*  The pyrevm backend answers
  =eth_sendRawTransaction= with keccak of the raw bytes (the page names the
  receipt in the same batch), refuses a nonce that is not the sender's next
  (no replay), and decodes EIP-1559 transactions (that path had never run).
- *Every frame carries the savings book* (=sv=: the outstanding principal
  O, the credited bonus S, the depositors' BUCK side B, the treasury's
  pending BUCK), read from the positions themselves: the venue's
  =poolBuckValues= applies the slippage guard and reverts on any day a pool
  is off its average.  From it, the SAVINGS INDEX D = min(B, 2B - O)/(O - S)
  -- what a redemption pays, in BUCK value, per BUCK deposited (the TOKEN
  side of the claim, =BuckBasketProRata._redeem=): 1 at the start, raised by
  the wheel's credits and the harvest of reversion.
- *The deposit guard shows.*  A deposit into a pool more than 2% from its
  10-minute TWAP is refused (=Slippage=).  With the clock still between days,
  that measures the last tick's move, and in the world's first days (BUCK
  from $1.09 to $0.99 in three) every pool may be past it.  The tab reads
  each pool's tick and TWAP tick, says how far each is, and "the basket's
  choice" is the director's hint when admitted, else the most underweight
  pool the guard admits; a refused save keeps its minted TOKEN for the retry.
- *A step begun in a pause ran one day too many* (the paused day was never
  counted); fixed, and the browser test is what caught it.
- *Reloads and links.*  The server keeps each world's lite frames and
  replays them to a new subscriber (=&replay=1=): a reloaded page redraws
  its charts, and =?world=<sid>= opens the same world elsewhere.
  =--static DIR= serves the built page on the websocket port, and the page
  opens on the Savings tab when served so (the in-tab Tevm world then boots
  only if one of its own tools is opened).
- *Reverting prices* (=scenario.prices = "revert"=,
  =gen_prices.revert_like=): each history's first price, its own realized
  volatility, the established OU walk pinned to end where it began
  (=gen_revert=, tau 120 days), fixed seeds, written beside the window's
  history files on first use.
