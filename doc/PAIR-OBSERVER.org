The default grid contains 11 cases, three quote-noise levels (0, 20, 80 bp
standard deviation per observation) and three seeds: 99 runs of 120 days.
Each run has 481 live observations. The scalar daily EMA bank gets 1280
historical days; the new observer gets 128 pre-run days at its live six-hour
cadence. Reference prices are supplied baseline means, not the first live
observation. Historical spectra use only days -1280 through -1.

* What is observed

Write a pool's quote as =P[i,q]= units of quote currency =q= per TOKEN =i=.
For synchronized quotes through the same currency:

#+begin_example
z[i,j,q] = log(P[i,q]) - log(P[j,q])
         = log(V[i]) - log(V[j]) + venue_error[i,q] - venue_error[j,q]
#+end_example

The quote currency's common value cancels. A fall in USDC that raises both
TOKEN/USDC quotations equally is not a relative TOKEN gain. Local pool
dislocations, different effective timestamps, stale quotes, or differing
TWAP windows do not cancel merely because the pool labels share a currency.
The prototype therefore requires a complete coherent snapshot for a venue;
a missing venue is omitted. Partial connected subgraphs are future work.

The displayed spread subtracts =log(meanPrice[i]/meanPrice[j])=. Zero means
the historical normalized ratio, not equal absolute TOKEN unit prices.
Neither that reference nor a spectral cycle proves eventual convergence.
Actual over/under-weight determines permissible transfer direction.

There are N(N-1)/2 pair outputs but N-1 independent relative coordinates:
=z[A,C] = z[A,B] + z[B,C]=. Assimilating all pair measurements as independent
would count the same pool errors repeatedly. A fixed orthonormal Helmert
basis removes the common component once. The filter keeps the covariance
between the remaining coordinates and projects it onto every pair.
Consequently all published pair estimates obey the same graph identities.

This is related to estimation from relative measurements on graphs;
[[https://web.ece.ucsb.edu/~hespanha/published/tr-analogyrep.pdf][Barooah and Hespanha]] describe the additive-reference ambiguity and the
role of measurement-graph covariance. Their static sensor-network results
do not establish the financial dynamics or independence of our pool errors.

BUCK and USDC routes offer redundant measurements of the same relative
state. They are additional information only to the extent their errors
differ. Arbitrage can correlate them. The observer retains cross-route
covariance; it does not assign two independent votes automatically.
Disagreement identifies a conflict, not which of two venues is correct.
Pairwise cancellation deliberately loses common TOKEN appreciation and
absolute currency value: K, monetary diagnostics and stablecoin-breaker
logic still need separate common-mode observations. Two quotes alone cannot
identify which currency lost an external peg.

* Observer state, uncertainty and known interventions

Each independent coordinate has level, velocity and acceleration. For a
time increment =dt=, level advances by =v*dt + a*dt^2/2= and velocity by
=a*dt=. Integrated white jerk supplies process uncertainty. There is no
assumed 30-day cycle or mean-reversion force in this motion model.

Measurements observe levels. A joint Kalman update uses the full venue
measurement covariance and a Joseph-form state-covariance update. Every
pair exposes spread, velocity, acceleration, their standard deviations,
closure speed, change in closure speed, freshness and a descriptive regime.
Acceleration opposing outward velocity identifies slowing departure before
velocity reaches zero. That is different from confirmed closure.

The noise estimator uses past third differences of the corrected relative
measurements. For temporally white noise, the coefficients 1,-3,3,-1 give a
variance multiplier of 20; a causal EWMA estimates the full covariance.
Its eigenvalues have a nonzero floor. A sample changes this covariance only
after its own assimilation. Gaps reset the difference history. Noise priors
and process parameters are fixed across the grid; the simulator's true noise
level, truth prices and derivatives never enter the observer.

This noise decomposition is a hypothesis. Genuine rapid motion can enter
the estimated measurement noise; serially correlated errors or overlapping
TWAPs violate the white-noise assumption. The lab measures interval coverage
instead of calling these intervals calibrated confidence. Innovations and
route disagreement remain visible, including when the model fits poorly.
No sample is automatically rejected for being far from the estimate.

An intervention input is a predicted /current log-price offset/ for each
affected pool, plus its uncertainty. It is subtracted from that measurement,
not repeatedly from returns. The ideal experiment uses fee-free constant
product marginal impact from known trade/reserve ratios and a known decay.
The mismatch experiment deliberately uses the wrong magnitude and decay,
with additional observation uncertainty. A third arm omits impact correction.
Actual post-trade pool state can inform immediate impact; later arbitrage
transmission and recovery are uncertain. A real implementation must expire
or revise this model rather than permanently subtract a supposed price
effect. Unattributed venue shocks and genuine repricing receive no own-trade
offset. Atomic route arbitrage remains a separate decision.

* Rebalance on cessation, then accelerate on closure

The desired progression is:

1. Reliable continuing departure quenches discretionary rebalancing.
2. Credible braking releases that quench before prices reverse.
3. Near a stall, small participation remains possible even if acceleration
   also settles toward zero.
4. Closure adds a stronger rate contribution proportional to closure speed.
5. Remaining allocation excess/shortfall limits the amount available.

For each potential overweight seller and underweight buyer, orient velocity
so that positive =v= means outward departure. The prototype uses:

#+begin_example
release = exp(-max(v + z*sigma_v, 0) / release_speed)
          * max(braking_evidence, near_stall_evidence)
closure = max(-v - z*sigma_v, 0)
rate    = release_rate * release + closure_gain * closure
#+end_example

Braking evidence requires inward acceleration beyond its uncertainty;
near-stall evidence decays rapidly with outward speed. Small release below
a threshold gives zero rate unless closure is already resolved. These are
explicit heuristic factors to test, not probabilities or an optimal law.
The z multiplier is not a guarantee when the observer is miscalibrated.

Each overweight TOKEN first gets a sale budget from its excess allocation,
the shortfall-weighted average rate across eligible counter-tokens, and
=1-exp(-rate*dt)=. It distributes that budget among eligible underweights
in proportion to their remaining shortfalls. Thus a stronger pair changes
the total urgency, while the destination split obeys the requested allocation
rule. A final common scale prevents combined sellers exceeding any buyer's
shortfall and preserves each seller's destination proportions. This is
conservative and may leave capacity unused.

The diagnostic book holds fixed initial TOKEN quantities and is marked
using the observer. Candidate requests are never executed, so their sums
are neither turnover nor return. A deployed allocator additionally needs
fees, pool depth, liquidity/credit limits and once-consumed elapsed-time
budgets. Emergency deleveraging is a distinct reason to trade.

* Commodity time scales from historical frequency content

The second response arm estimates spectra from the /past observations/,
without using the generator's periods or live-run data. It removes a linear
trend, applies a Hann window, computes a one-sided periodogram, estimates
a high-frequency white-noise floor, and examines periods from 8 to 640 days.
The [[https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.signal.periodogram.html][periodogram documentation]] describes the underlying spectrum and
normalization choices; this implementation uses NumPy directly.

For a resolved spectrum, a power-weighted geometric period and spectral
moments provide typical spread, velocity and acceleration. These set a
candidate release-speed scale, a slower baseline response for longer periods,
and a closure gain scaled by historical spread amplitude. Unresolved spectra
fall back to the fixed response configuration. Both arms use the same
observer, book and allocation rule. The constants and all derived parameters
are included in JSON; no parameters are selected from the measured outcome.

Frequency content alone does not identify gestation time, shipping delay,
construction time or monetary transmission lag. A pair contains both TOKENs'
dynamics; a centered TOKEN contains part of every other TOKEN's motion.
Broad spectra, structural breaks and only a few observed long cycles weaken
the interpretation further. Physical supply-cycle information can be an
external prior, tested against observed dynamics. Keep multiple bands and
their uncertainty as a later extension rather than treating one dominant
period as a permanent property of the commodity.

must accompany any claim about improvement.

* Initial findings and next acceptance checks

The generated report is authoritative for measured numbers. Smooth cycles
are favorable to the local-acceleration observer. The existing lagged/OU
generator is a deliberate counterexample: its rough daily innovations can
make velocity estimates worse than the matched EMA and leave uncertainty
severely understated. Neither visually early turns nor a good sinusoidal
fit justify giving this observer execution authority.

At 20 bp quote noise, averaged across the three seeds, the first completed
grid gives the following velocity errors (bp/day):

| Case | Two-route observer | BUCK-only observer | Six-hour two-route EMA5 | Observer ±2 sigma coverage |
|------+--------------------+--------------------+------------------------+----------------------------|
| Three-phase | 26.48 | 30.91 | 142.80 | 87.0% |
| Changing cycles | 23.81 | 27.55 | 100.92 | 89.7% |
| Lagged commodities + OU | 255.17 | 257.14 | 197.94 | 20.3% |
| Constant TOKENs, moving numeraires | 15.87 | 17.36 | 8.96 | 98.3% |

These results support further investigation of early motion estimates;
they do not support treating their model uncertainty as reliable throughout
the commodity universe. In the noiseless numeraire-only arm the relative
state stays flat and both allocators request zero transfers, including
through the USDC devaluation. The noisy constant-TOKEN arm also requests
zero in this grid because the allocation band absorbs the small residuals.

The cessation allocator is not immune to false braking. In the lagged/OU
case at 20 bp, roughly 28.5% of fixed-scale requested notional occurs while
the relevant pair is departing, and only about 8.0% of total requests occur
while it is both departing /and slowing/. This is a substantive failure of
the present estimator/response combination, not evidence of useful early
capture. The analogous smooth-cycle shares are both about 0.14%, meaning
pre-closure participation is currently small. Those factors need calibration
on held-out paths and subsequent executed-trade comparisons.

* Eventual wheel implementation

The Python reference uses dense covariance and batch solves. A three-TOKEN
state has six entries (two relative coordinates times three derivatives);
six TOKENs have fifteen entries. All pair outputs can be derived on demand.
Dense covariance storage grows quadratically; matrix factorization is not
constant-cost. This is a reference for correctness, not an EVM gas estimate.

An EVM design should capture raw observations with effective timestamps,
decimals, orientation, depth and receipt-linked intervention inputs; transform
one frozen epoch; perform bounded prediction/measurement work; then publish
a coherent state/covariance generation with freshness metadata. Correlated
measurements require whitening or an equivalent joint update, not repeated
independent scalar updates. Repeated ticks must not repeatedly assimilate the
same information. Consumers may request pair projections from a completed
generation and must recheck execution prices and current allocation limits.

The wheel can amortize computation, but information has a deadline. Measure
gas and worst-case publication delay together, including starvation under
sparse user activity. A cheaper well-calibrated approximation can outperform
a sophisticated estimate that arrives after the opportunity has passed.
