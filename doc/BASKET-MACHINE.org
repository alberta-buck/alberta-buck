#+TITLE: The basket machine, as built
#+DATE: 2026-10-10
#+OPTIONS: ^:nil toc:2 num:t

This describes the contracts that turn the equity basket: what each one is, what it reads, what
it decides and what bounds it. It replaces the proposal that carried this name and the working
notes written while the machine was built. What the machine earns, and on what evidence, is in
[[file:BASKET-MACHINE-RESULTS.org][the results]]. What is left to do is in [[file:BASKET-MACHINE-TASKS.org][the task list]].

The older machines still ship beside this one: the pro-rata, fence and ops baskets, the Deploy /
Fund / Trim facet, the rebalance directors, the turn director and the shadow observer. Nothing
here imports them. They stay until the simulators show this machine is better in every regime.

* The parts

A /facet/ is code the basket shell delegatecalls, so it runs on the shell's storage. A /service/
is a contract with its own state that the others call.

| Contract | Kind | What it owns | Who calls it |
|----------+------+--------------+--------------|
| =BuckBasketEquity= | Shell | The books: receipts, shares, wallet TOKEN, liquidity counts, the credit | Depositors, governance, the wheel |
| =ValidatedBasketVenue= | Facet | Nothing: Uniswap V3 reads, swaps and positions with strict price checks | The shell and the upkeep facet |
| =PairBasketEquityResponse= | Facet | Nothing: the Daily, Sync and Respond steps | The wheel, through the shell |
| =PairRebalancePolicy= | Service | Each pair's last rate and change, the basket's own recent price impact, the time of the last transfer | The upkeep facet |
| =BlockPairSignals= | Service | One level-and-rate tracker per TOKEN per quote route, and their error statistics | Anyone (=observe=); read by the policy |
| =BasketSignals= | Service | The last valid reference-basket value in BUCK, its freshness and day count | The wheel's Sample slot, the desk policy, K's reference |
| =EquityDesk= | Service | The desk's own account, TOKEN, credit, turnover budget and capital floor | The wheel's Ops slot |
| =OuterBandMonetaryDirector= | Service | Which side of parity the basket is on and for how many days | The desk and the wheel |
| =BasketMachineWheel= | Wheel | The cursor, the reserve, the arbitrage triangles | Any keeper (=tick=) |

=BuckBasketEquityResponse= is the base of the upkeep facet. It holds the three step entry points,
the sell and buy primitives and an older allocation plan, which the pair facet now uses only as
its risk fallback (see [[#fallback][below]]).

* The books and the mark

The basket is an ordinary BUCK credit holder. It holds one self-issued credit marked at its own
equity, so Buck enforces its limit: it can owe at most K times that mark.

- /Gross/ is every TOKEN/BUCK position at fair value (twice its BUCK side, full range) plus the
  wallet's TOKEN, at a chosen price mark.
- /Equity/ is the gross plus the signed BUCK account plus the relief accrued on it. A negative
  account is the lien. Equity already deducts it.
- Marks are TWAP, HIGH (the higher of spot and TWAP, an entrant's price) and LOW (an exiter's).
  Every spend is preceded by a mark of the credit at equity(LOW).
- A constituent's /allocation/ is its position (both sides) plus its wallet TOKEN, as a share of
  the gross. Targets are the declared weights; the pair facet requires a zero target lean.

About 72% of the TOKEN sits inside liquidity in the lab's settled book. A position's value
follows the square root of price, so the basket's allocations move about 60% as far as a wallet's
would for the same price change.

* Signals

** Relative price: =BlockPairSignals=

A price-only observer of every TOKEN on every registered quote route (BUCK and USDC in the lab).
It never trades and values no book.

- /Observation./ Each call reads every pool's cumulative tick. The difference from the previous
  call, over the time between them, is the interval's mean log price; no outlier is rejected.
  Within a route the mean across TOKENs is removed, so a move in the quote currency cancels.
- /Tracking./ Per TOKEN and route, an alpha-beta filter keeps a level and a rate. Its gains come
  from one response time (12 hours in the lab) and the actual time between samples.
- /Allowance./ Per pair and route it keeps a running mean of the prediction error and of the
  rate's acceleration. The rate's allowance is a floor, plus the error over the response time,
  plus the lag this filter is known to have for that acceleration. It is empirical, not a
  confidence interval.
- /Readiness./ A route is ready after three samples and a warm-up (two days), and falls out when
  a pool cannot be read or the gap exceeds a day. A route that fails restarts from nothing.
- /Pair./ For TOKENs a and b it reports the level and rate of a against b averaged over ready
  routes, and the envelope (lowest low, highest high) of their rates. Routes are not treated as
  independent evidence.

** Parity: =BasketSignals=

The reference basket's cost in BUCK from each pool's TWAP, refused when any pool has no
liquidity or its spot is more than a tolerance (5%) from its TWAP. It publishes the last valid
value, whether it is fresh, and how many consecutive days have had a valid sample. K reads the
same quantity through the venue. No offset or filter is applied to either.

The contract also carries seven-window EMA ladders of each TOKEN's relative tick and of the
common value, with an imported history. Nothing in the desk or in paired rebalancing reads them.
They are read only by the inherited fallback plan, and are to be retired with it.

* The rebalance policy: =PairRebalancePolicy=

The policy owns no assets and authorizes no spending. For an ordered pair (sell a, buy b) it
returns a /desire/: the fraction of an eligible allocation gap to move per day.

1. /Evidence must be current./ Every route ready, and the policy's motion sample taken from the
   observer's latest sample. Otherwise zero.
2. /Separation./ The pair's log price must stand more than 1% above its accepted reference
   (governance-supplied historical means), on the filtered level and on every live pool. A ratio
   that has crossed its reference is a renewed departure, not a reason to keep selling.
3. /Motion./ =rate= is the pair's rate, positive when a is still gaining on b. =change= is its
   change over one response time. The basket's own recent price impact widens the allowance,
   decaying over two days.
4. /Braking release./ =releaseRate × release × braking=. =braking= is the fall in the rate over
   one response time against the allowance, at most 1; a rate within the floor of zero counts
   as one fifth. =release= halves at an outward speed of =releaseSpeed=, or at the rate's own
   allowance when =releaseSpeed= is zero.
5. /Closure./ When even the upper allowance is below zero the pair is closing. It adds
   =closureGain= per unit of that closing speed, and =closureRate= times its confidence (the
   closing speed against the allowance, at most 1).
6. /Ceiling./ The sum is capped at =maxRate=.

The =Sizing= (the five rates and scales above, and the share of own impact counted) is fixed at
construction. =fraction(desire, elapsed)= converts a desire into the share of a gap to move now:
=u / (1 + u)= with =u = desire × elapsed=, elapsed counted from the last executed transfer and
capped at a day. Calling more often cannot spend more.

* The upkeep facet: =PairBasketEquityResponse=

The wheel turns three kinds of step on the basket.

- /Daily (0)./ Once a day: update the flow statistic that sizes the liquidity target, settle
  relief against the lien, re-mark the credit.
- /Sync (1, pool i)./ Once a day per pool: collect the position's fees into the wallet and the
  account, re-mark.
- /Respond (2)./ At most once per interval: observe, then one of the following.

** A paired transfer

1. Value every constituent. A gap is /material/ when it exceeds the allocation band (2 points of
   the gross in the lab).
2. For each overweight donor, the eligible recipients are the underweight constituents for
   which the policy's desire is positive, provided the donor or the recipient is material.
3. The amount is the smaller of the donor's excess and the recipients' shortfalls, times
   =fraction= of the shortfall-weighted desire, then capped by the step (5% of the donor's value)
   and the pool's depth (1% per swap). Recipients share it in proportion to their shortfalls and
   are scaled down together to respect each one's step, depth and shortfall.
4. Amounts at or below the minimum (the grain, widened for the venue's price tolerance) are not
   requested. The donor with the largest executable total wins.
5. Execution sells the donor's wallet TOKEN first and burns liquidity only for the remainder.
   Each recipient's share buys TOKEN with half and places the other half beside it as liquidity.
   No fresh credit is drawn: recipients spend the sale's proceeds.
6. If any planned recipient is refused, or the mark would leave the basket over its limit, the
   whole response reverts, the sale included.
7. The measured tick change of each pool is reported to the policy as own impact, and the
   policy's transfer clock restarts.

** Otherwise

With no transfer to make, the facet places wallet TOKEN into liquidity where the funded
allocation is shortest, if the account has spending room beyond its liquidity target. This swaps
nothing and does not restart the transfer clock. Failing that it re-marks a stale credit.

** The fallback
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: fallback
:END:

The facet hands the response to the inherited plan when the book is empty, when the lien exceeds
K times equity(LOW), when spendable BUCK is below the liquidity target's band, or when any
constituent is beyond its leash (30% of its declared weight). That plan sells for cash first and
is where the EMA ladders are still consulted. In the lab's cyclic runs it is taken in two to
four of 481 responses.

* The venue: =ValidatedBasketVenue=

The Uniswap V3 venue with nothing optional. A missing TWAP is an unavailable price, including on
K's reference path. A swap must execute within the price tolerance of the TWAP and leave the
spot within it, or the step reverts. A position is minted only when spot is within tolerance.

* The desk: =EquityDesk= and =OuterBandMonetaryDirector=

The desk is its own credit holder beside the basket: its own account, its own marked credit, its
own TOKEN. Nothing it holds or owes enters the basket's books. It mirrors the basket's
constituents and trades the same pools through the same venue facet.

The policy leaves the inner band to K.

- /Side./ It enters a side when the raw parity error exceeds the entry band (3%) and leaves when
  it returns inside the release band (2%).
- /Persistence./ It counts consecutive valid days beyond the entry band on the same side. A
  missed day, a reversal or a return clears the count.
- /Effort./ A share (25%) of the error beyond the release band, capped (40 bp of the desk's
  size basis per operation), and only while TWAP and spot agree on the side.
- /Authority./ The desk sells BUCK it holds, or buys BUCK with TOKEN, on any advice. It draws
  credit to issue only on advice that has persisted (30 days).
- /Guard./ After the trade the terminal spot must still be outside the release band on the same
  side, or the operation reverts. The desk cannot spend through K's band.
- /Limits./ A finite turnover budget that nothing replenishes, a capital floor on the desk's net
  value, position and debt caps, and Buck's own limit on its lien.

* The wheel: =BasketMachineWheel=

One permissionless =tick= advances a cursor over slots, each due or not: Compute (K's cycle),
Ops (the desk), the basket's Daily, Sync per pool and Respond, Arb (triangles between TOKEN/BUCK,
TOKEN/USDC and BUCK/USDC, taken only at a realized profit) and Sample (the parity reference,
through the desk policy when there is a desk). Ops publishes its observation dependency first. A
failing target cannot trap the cursor.

Arbitrage profit goes to a configured creditee. The basket accepts income from its wheel and
from one governance-appointed arbitrage creditor, which can deposit and nothing else.

lab model, so earlier runs reproduce frame for frame.

* How it is deployed today

There is no single recipe yet. The lab builds the older equity machine with the simulator's
=deploy=, then swaps in the venue, the upkeep facet, the parity reference and the desk policy
(=control_lab/engine.py=), and wires the pair observer and policy separately
(=control_lab/block_pairs.py=). The general simulator reaches part of this with
=SIM_EQUITY_MACHINE=1= and has no pair policy at all. The shell is not a proxy with upgradeable
storage: a new facet needs a shell built from these sources.

The order that matters: seed and age every pool through a TWAP window; wire venue and upkeep
facet; open and fund the desk's credit; deploy the observer, then the policy with the accepted
reference means; set the desk's policy, guard, budget and floor; point the wheel's Ops at the
desk, Sample at the desk policy, Equity at the basket.
