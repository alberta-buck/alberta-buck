BUCK · Real EVM experiment

Signals into action

Compare policy settings on the same realized price path. Noise σ is the standard deviation of the daily log-price increment. Historical inputs use observed monthly anchors joined by deterministic bridges. The default grid adds daily log-price noise with standard deviation 0% or 1%. Each selection shows saved execution observations, not a browser simulation.

Parameter grid

Incremental growth relative to matched maintenance, by onset and speed. Select a cell to inspect its saved run. Blank combinations were not planned.

Matched outcome

Return and maximum drawdown use each arm’s first captured BUCK-denominated share mark as its baseline. The two BUCK marks are endogenous to their respective runs, so differences are not a common-dollar valuation. Maintenance shares the regime, noise and seed. Incremental growth is the ratio of active to maintenance growth factors minus one; the daily figure takes its geometric root over the recorded day span. Both require matching observation endpoints. Trade counts are observed outcomes; changes cannot by themselves be attributed to the pair signal. Marks exclude caller gas.

Price and signal timeline

Prices are indexed to each token’s first observation. Triangles mark actual buys and sells by observation day. Pair rates are in basis points per day; observations without both routes ready are omitted. Rates do not imply execution.

Signal and execution trace

Daily pair values and actual trade events are recorded from the selected active run. Readiness is the contract’s bit mask; an unready pair is not a zero signal. Expand a day for pair and execution detail. The pair effect compares planned buy and sell with the same prestate after removing the discretionary pair amount; it is not itself a fill. Pair rate is zero when the policy is not consulted because eligibility or leash gates block it. Amounts and caps are in BUCK, gap is a signed fraction, and urgency is in share-days.

Method and regeneration

Cells are precomputed with the pyrevm backend. A group shares its realized input path across arms; compare only matching groups. A missing or failed arm remains visible as unavailable. Returns and drawdowns depend on the recorded window and valuation, and do not include caller gas.
