#+TITLE: The Alberta Buck sandbox: implementation plan
#+DATE: 2026-09-25
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil ^:nil

Phase G2 of doc/review/public-plan.org: a browser app in which a newcomer issues and registers
identities, insures simulated assets into credit, keeps wallets and moves BUCK -- in a private
world running in their own tab, in minutes, with nothing to install but a browser.

* Scope

*v1:* the world bar and four tools -- Issuer, Wallet, Credit, Observer -- over one Tevm world in
one page.  Every key, credential and balance is simulated and says so.

*Not v1 (later, in order of value):* AB-RCPT/2 receipts in the Wallet; one world shared by
several tabs (a =SharedWorker= holding the chain); connecting to the Python sim server's shared,
agent-driven world; Notes from fixture proofs; the insurer gate (=configureInsurerGate=,
=attestInsurer=); revocation.

* Architecture

- *Where.*  =core/js/sandbox/=: =index.html=, =styles.css=, =src/app.js= (the controller:
  =SandboxApp=, chain logic only, no DOM -- the =BuckWorldApp= pattern), =src/ui/*.js= (one module
  per tool, presentation only), =src/main.js= (boot and wiring).  esbuild bundles it into
  =core/js/sandbox/dist/= (gitignored) beside the web wasm and the contract bundle.
- *Chain.*  =tevmSession()= -- a Tevm =MemoryClient= in the page.  =buildBuckWorld= deploys
  =IdentityRegistry=, =BuckCredit=, =BuckKControllerDirect= and =Buck= from the published
  =alberta-buck-contracts= bundle in =core/js/node_modules= (no Foundry needed to build the
  sandbox); the identity/wallet/registry kernels are the web wasm (=make core-build-wasm-web=).
- *Accounts and keys.*  Every actor is a viem local account generated in the page and funded with
  gas from dev account 0 (=fundAccount=).  The issuer's PS keys live in the Issuer tool; each
  wallet's identity key (ElGamal =sk=) lives in its wallet.  One "acting as" selector per tool.
- *Persistence.*  After every action the app saves ={chain: tevmDumpState(), app: {issuer,
  credentials, wallets, credits, labels, journal tail}}= to IndexedDB, and restores it at load.
  *Export* writes the same object as a JSON file (BigInts as decimal strings); *Import* reads it;
  *Reset* deploys a fresh world.
- *UI.*  Vanilla ES modules and one stylesheet, as the existing demos: no framework to learn or
  upgrade.  Labels on every control, keyboard reachable, numbers formatted as BUCK with six
  decimals, addresses shortened with the full value on hover and copy.

* Core additions (=core/js/src=, tested with =node --test= on =tevmSession=)

The sandbox needs the ceremony in its two real halves, and credit with a separate insurer.
Existing functions keep their behaviour, composed from the new ones.

- =issueCredential(world, fields, {rng, issuer})= :: the issuer's half: canonical record -> m ->
  PS signature; returns ={issuer, fields, canonical, m, sigma}=, a credential card.
- =registerWallet(world, account, credential, {rng})= :: the holder's half: checks the card
  against the issuer key the registry publishes (=issuerKey=), then presentation, identity key
  and ElGamal, registration proof, =register=; returns the handle =onboard= returns.
- =onboard(...)= :: unchanged, now =registerWallet(issueCredential(...))=.
- =insureAsset(world, insurer, holder, terms)= :: the holder accepts the insurer
  (=setCreditIssuer=), the insurer calls =createCredit=; returns the token id.
- =activateCredit(world, holder, amount)= :: =Buck.mint(amount)= against the holder's credit;
  returns the =Minted= event's figures (=coverage= is face activated: a depreciating credit
  needs a little more face than the present value asked for).
- =createCredit(...)= :: unchanged, now =insureAsset= + =activateCredit=.
- =creditView(world, tokenId)= :: holder, insurer, class, face, floor, depreciation, premium,
  =currentValue=; also =creditsOf(world, owner)= and =accountView(world, address)=.
- =loadWallet(wasm)= (=wallet-web.js=) :: the browser loader for =wrapWallet=, sharing the
  identity wasm module.
- =observe(world, fromBlock)= (=observer.js=) :: decoded transactions and events since a block,
  each field marked public or opaque.

- =snapshotTevm(session)= / =restoreTevm(session, snap)= (=backends.js=) and
  =worldRecord= / =attachBuckWorld= :: the chain and the world as data, and back.
- =codec.js= :: =encodeJSON= / =decodeJSON=: BigInts as ={"$bigint": "<decimal>"}=.

These ride in =alberta-buck-core= 0.2.1 when the sandbox ships.

* The tools

** World bar (always visible)

Shows the block, the simulated date and day number, BUCK_K (=kctrl.buckK=), total BUCK supply
and the wallet count.  Actions: +1 hour, +1 day, +30 days (=advanceTime=), Export, Import,
Reset.  A journal drawer lists every transaction with its outcome and gas (the existing
=JournalWriter=).

** Issuer (the registry and its KYC desk)

- The issuer is created and trusted with the world (=trustIssuer=, by governance).
- A form for the core record: given and family name at first certification, date of birth, a
  registry person number (generated), and the certification date.
- *Issue* -> a credential card (the fields, the identity scalar's commitment, the signature):
  shown, listed, downloadable as JSON, and offered to a wallet directly.
- Says what the chain learns at issuance: nothing -- issuance is off-chain.

** Wallet (a citizen)

- *Create* a wallet (label -> fresh account, funded with gas).
- *Import a credential* (from the Issuer's list, or pasted JSON) and *Register*
  (=registerWallet=): the identity is proved on chain without revealing who it is.
- Shows: address, registered or not, balance, signed balance (credit drawn), credit limit,
  demurrage owing (=feeOwing=).
- *Introduce* two wallets (=identityApprove= both ways -- the handshake private payments need),
  then *Send* BUCK (=transfer=).  Each step names what it discloses and to whom.

** Credit (an insurer)

- The insurer is a sandbox account, "Sandbox Mutual".
- A form: the holder (a registered wallet), an asset class, face value, floor, depreciation
  (none, linear, declining balance) and rate, premium rate.  Asset classes are sandbox labels over
  =BuckCredit='s opaque =uint8=, each with a sensible default: home (linear, slow), vehicle
  (declining, 15 %/yr), equipment (declining), farmland and gold (none).
- *Insure* -> a BuckCredit NFT (=insureAsset=); *Activate* an amount -> the holder mints BUCK
  (=activateCredit=).
- A table of credits with their depreciated value (=currentValue=), which moves as the clock does.

** Observer (Mallory)

- Every transaction since the world began, decoded (=observe=): block, from, to (by contract
  name), function, arguments, events.
- Identity material shows as what it is on chain -- curve points and proofs, not names -- with a
  one-line note on what each field would take to open.  The privacy argument, made visible.

* Build, run, test

- =make sandbox-build=: web wasm, then an esbuild bundle of =sandbox/src/main.js= into
  =sandbox/dist/= with the wasm and =alberta-buck-contracts='s =contracts.json= copied beside it.
- =make sandbox=: build, then serve =sandbox/dist= on =http://localhost:8000= and print the URL.
- =make sandbox-test=, part of =core-test-js=:
  1. the core additions, each on =tevmSession=, including a =tevmDumpState= / =tevmLoadState=
     round trip that restores balances, credits and the clock;
  2. the whole story through =SandboxApp= with no DOM: issue two credentials, register two
     wallets, insure an asset, activate credit, introduce, send, advance 30 days (demurrage
     accrues, the credit depreciates), the observer's rows, export then import to an equal world;
  3. the built page in headless Chromium through Playwright (=test/sandbox.browser.test.js=; the
     browser comes from the flake).

- *S6* :: a hosted build (a GitHub Pages workflow), once the repository is public.  /Accepted
  when/ the README links a live sandbox.

* Risks to settle early (in S1)

- *Tevm state round trip.*  =tevmLoadState= must restore the block number and timestamp as well as
  accounts and storage; if it does not, the app records the clock and replays it on load.
- *Registration cost in a tab.*  One registration proof plus a 3M-gas =register= in Tevm; measure,
  and show progress if it passes a second.
- *BigInt in storage.*  Every persisted and exported value is a decimal string; one codec,
  tested by the export/import round trip.

* S1, done (2026-09-25)

The core additions above, in =core/js/src/{buckworld,backends,observer,codec,wallet-web}.js=,
with =test/roles.tevm.test.js= (the ceremonies by role, the observer, the round trip) and
=test/wallet.web.test.js=; the existing suite unchanged (68 of 68 with the demo bundles).  The
risks, settled:

- *Tevm state round trip.*  =tevmLoadState= restores accounts and storage (hex strings, JSON-safe)
  but not the clock: the chain restarts at block 0 and its next block takes the wall-clock time,
  which would run every contract clock backwards.  =snapshotTevm= records the head's number and
  timestamp; =restoreTevm= presets the next timestamp, mines the height back in one =tevmMine=
  (one block a second, 21 blocks in 0.23 s, about 300 a second), and ends on the snapshot's own
  timestamp.  No contract reads =block.number=; the height is kept so the Observer's numbering
  carries on.  The dump holds *no history* -- blocks, transactions, logs -- so the app keeps the
  Observer's decoded rows in its own saved state (S2).  A world after the whole test story is
  147 KiB of JSON.
- *Registration cost.*  About 200 ms in Node for the proof and the =register= transaction; no
  progress display needed unless a browser is far slower (measure in S3).
- *BigInt in storage.*  One codec, =codec.js=, tagged (={"$bigint": ...}=, ={"$bytes": ...}=) so a
  string that looks numeric stays a string; the test round-trips a credential card and the whole
  saved world through it.

* S2, done (2026-09-25)

=core/js/sandbox/src/app.js= (=SandboxApp=) and =store.js= (=idbStore=, falling back to
=memoryStore= where storage is refused), with =test/sandbox.app.tevm.test.js=: the whole story
with no DOM, 12 steps, about 10 s.  The shape the UI builds on:

- *Actions* (queued, one at a time; each then observes, saves and notifies =onChange=):
  =reset=, =advance=, =issue= (the core record of the privacy paper's cast: name at first
  certification, date of birth, a random =AB-P-NNNN-NNNN= person number never reused, the chain's
  date; =id_type=, =issuer_id=, =jurisdiction=, =epoch= stamped), =importCredential= (the same
  card twice is one credential), =createWallet=, =register=, =introduce= (both directions,
  skipping any already done), =send= (to a wallet or an address), =insure= (asset-class
  defaults), =activate=, =importText=.  Reads: =view()= (everything the screens show, in one
  queued pass), =labels()= (who is who, for the observer's "you know this" layer),
  =credentialText=, =exportText=.
- *Refusals* -- the protocol's (=BUCK: recipient not verified=, the handshake, a premium
  without funding) and the sandbox's own (a missing field, an unregistered wallet) -- throw
  =SandboxError= with a =reason= for people; the world is saved all the same.
- *Saved world*: ={format, version, chain: snapshotTevm, app}=; the app part holds the world
  record (issuer keys included -- simulated), credentials, wallets (private keys in the clear --
  simulated), insurers, credits, introductions, the observer's rows (the chain keeps no history
  across a restore) and the journal's tail.  After the story: 187 KiB, serialized in 6 ms.
- *Import* builds the new world in a fresh session and swaps it in only once it stands; a bad
  file leaves the current world untouched.  An unreadable stored world starts over and sets
  =notice=.
- *Asset classes* (=ASSET_CLASSES=): home (linear 2.5 %/yr above a 30 % land floor), vehicle
  (declining 15 %/yr to 10 %), equipment (declining 10 %/yr to 5 %), farmland and gold (none).
  Premiums default to 0: with no basket the controller's funding factor is 1.0, so a premium
  credit activates only against an existing balance or unused credit, and =Buck.mint= draws the
  cheapest credit first unless told which.

* S2b, done (2026-09-25): the market and real premiums

Asked for by the author: premiums paid for real, which means wallets start with dollars and buy
BUCK in the BUCK/USDC pool -- the pools being as much a part of the BUCK world as the basket that
will join them.

- *What a premium is, on chain.*  Paid up front, not yearly: activating =net= spendable BUCK at r
  %/yr pays =net * 10r / (100 - 10r)= into the insurance pool (=Buck.POOL_ROI_INV=: the pool's
  assumed 10 % return funds the premium in perpetuity; returnable when the credit is repaid), and
  =Buck.mint= refuses unless the holder already has that principal times the funding factor (1.0
  with no basket) in held BUCK or unused credit.  So a first activation needs a deposit bought in
  the pool; later ones are covered by the first's unused credit.  Rates stay under 10 %/yr.
- *Routers need no identity -- by Permit2.*  BUCK's transfer gate checks only the two ends of a
  movement, never the spender, so a router that never HOLDS BUCK needs no binding, and the
  deployed routers BUCK must work with cannot be bound anyway.  Proven on Tevm against an unbound
  Universal Router v2.1: buying works; selling works through Permit2 (the pool pulls from the
  holder) and fails if the holder pre-funds the router; an EXACT-INPUT route with BUCK as an
  intermediate hop fails whatever the payer (the router takes custody of each intermediate
  output); an EXACT-OUTPUT route through BUCK works (intermediates pass pool to pool); BUCK first
  or last works.  A private holder needs a one-time identity handshake with each pool it trades
  with (=Buck._identityCheckedTransfer=: the operator of a public contract must be able to decrypt
  its counterparties).
- *=core/js/src/market.js=* (=buildMarket=): mock USDC; a =UniswapV3Factory=; the BUCK/USDC pool
  (0.05 %) created and bound public+carrying by the production =UniswapV3BindingAdapter= under the
  market operator (the deployer, registered); =SimLP= authorizing its own binding and LP-ing $1M
  full range from a zero-premium credit; Permit2 and the Universal Router as Uniswap ships them,
  unbound.  All production contracts but the mock USDC and =SimLP=.  Helpers: =openTrading=,
  =tradingOpen=, =buyBuck=, =buyBuckExact=, =sellBuck= (all Permit2, the output straight to the
  holder; amounts read from the transfer events, since a BUCK balance moves by demurrage between
  blocks), =buckPrice=, =poolReserves=, =fiatIn=, =marketRecord= / =attachMarket=,
  =marketContracts= (for the observer).  =buckworld.js= gains =quoteActivation= (=Buck.quoteMint=
  times the funding factor, against the balance: the shortfall) and =cheapestFirst=.
- *Permit2* is vendored beside the router (=alberta_buck/sim/artifacts/Permit2.json=, MIT; the
  Makefile builds it from the source the router pins, =lib/universal-router/lib/permit2=, solc
  0.8.17 via_ir); =nodefs.loadAnyArtifact= and the browser bundle carry both.
- *=SandboxApp=*: every new world has the market and a separate insurance-pool account; every
  wallet starts with 10 ETH and 10,000 USDC (=ENDOWMENT=); new actions =deposit=, =openTrading=,
  =buy= (={usdc}= or exactly ={buck}=), =sell=, and the read =quote= (with =buy=: the shortfall
  plus 1 %); asset classes carry premiums (home 0.35 %/yr, vehicle 3 %, equipment 1 %, farmland
  0.2 %, gold 0.12 %); the view adds BUCK's pool price, the pool's reserves, the insurance pool's
  balance, each wallet's USDC and whether it trades; the observer decodes the pool, router, Permit2
  and USDC.  Saved-world format version 2.
- *Tests*: =test/buckmarket.tevm.test.js= (the bindings; no trade before the handshake; buy exact
  in and out and sell through Permit2 with the router never holding BUCK; a premium refused, then
  bought and paid into the insurance pool, equal to the quote) and the whole story, now 13 steps
  with the premium path, a sale that moves the price and the observer's swaps.

* S4, done (2026-09-25): Credit and Observer

- *Credit* (=src/ui/credit.js=): Sandbox Mutual's form -- owner, asset class (its schedule, floor
  and premium filled in from =ASSET_CLASSES=, all editable), value, floor, depreciation and rate,
  premium; an activation panel quoting live as the amount changes -- coverage, the premium (ten
  years' worth, due now), what the owner must already hold, what it holds, the shortfall -- with

- *Observer* (=src/ui/observer.js=): every transaction newest first -- block, time, sender,
  contract and function, gas, refusals marked -- with its arguments and events; identity material
  shown as an =opaque= chip, the first word of its value, and the note on what opening it would
  take; BUCK and USDC amounts at six decimals, approvals of everything as "unlimited"; a filter
  (identity, payments, credit, market, deployments; the plain Permit2 approve files under market);
  "show what only you know" adds the sandbox's own labels to addresses (case-insensitively: Tevm
  reports deployed addresses in lower case).  Mallory's default view holds no name, birth date or
  person number, and the browser test asserts it.

  premium lands in the insurance pool), thirty days of depreciation, the observer's view with and
