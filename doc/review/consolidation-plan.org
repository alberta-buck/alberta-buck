What the move showed, for the owner:

- /Five equity tests encoded the old components/ (a gross mark, a liquidity target the wheel keeps
  spent, deleveraging by trimming after a K cut). On the machine they assert its rules instead:
  the credit is marked net (=_netMark()=: equity at the exits' marks plus the positions' BUCK side
  over K), the lien stays within K x that mark, and a deposit after a cut calls nothing back and
  adds K x its value to the limit. A K cut alone no longer puts the machine under water much: the
  BUCKs it issued sit in its positions and count whole.
- /The machine's venue refuses to sell into a displaced pool/: after a large outside TOKEN sale the
  Jubilee fund's scheduled sale reverts =Slippage()= (spot outside its TWAP band) where the old
  venue sold at the dropped price. The tests now assert the refusal, then that the sale goes
  through once the average catches up, at well under the earlier mark.
- /The desk invariant/ measures the basket's mark against the net mark; the desk's grant is still
  never in it.
- /A forge trap/: the equity fixture's loops read =block.timestamp= after =vm.warp=, and via-IR
  cached it in some inheriting contracts, so time stood still in those suites (the arb-income
  suite's wheel ran one response in sixty rounds). The fixture now reads =vm.getBlockTimestamp()=.
- /Known issue, pinned/ (=test_nearZeroKExitOfAFullyPairedBasketIsShortByTheMarkedHeldBuck=): at a
  near-zero K a fully paired machine basket's exit reverts =InsufficientSpendableBalance=. The
  shortfall rule retires the marked share of the held BUCK (502.41 in the test), but the exit
  recovers only the BUCK it actually releases (499.99) after paying the leaver. At K = 0.05 the
  exit proceeds. The owner (2026-11-21): no contract change; agents recovering BUCK for savings and
  liability retirement resolve it (doc/review/bucknet-economy-implementation.org).
