#+TITLE: Atomic renewal of one BuckCredit facility
#+DATE: 2026-10-29
#+OPTIONS: ^:nil toc:2

* Scope

This is the implementation design for renewing one existing facility with its
current insurer. The insurer proposes an exact replacement, and the NFT owner
independently authorizes the same proposal and requests execution. An insurer
change would require an additional ownership/authorization design and remains
separate. The BuckCredit NFT and its coverage stay in place throughout the
transaction.

The replacement specifies the face, depreciation terms, premium rate, desired
activated face, new issuance, maximum new premium, minimum acceptable relief,
deadline, current credit-terms hash, and expected outstanding principal. The
digest binds the token contract, chain, owner, NFT, all replacement terms and
limits. The insurer can replace/cancel its proposal; the owner can replace or
cancel its own authorization. A stale proposal fails if the credit terms or
principal changed. Credit terms include existing coverage, not merely a
reappraised face. No signature scheme or new on-chain identity service is
necessary for this first implementation.

* Transaction and accounting

1. Validate owner and insurer approvals and the deadline before the fund sells
   anything. Read the selected facility's capped claim seconds and the aggregate
   benchmark. Checkpoint the fund reserve controller.
2. Sell the facility's proportional share of the Jubilee basket position and
   compute the facility's share of pre-sale cash plus actual net BUCK proceeds,
   capped by its outstanding principal. Respect failed, partial and in-kind
   exits and preserve the other fund claims. The owner's minimum relief protects
   the entire transaction. TOKEN is not BUCK relief.
3. NFT principal and total issued supply are whole raw BUCK units. Round the
   computed fractional relief *down* to whole raw units before paying it and
   leave the fractional remainder in the fund. The owner explicitly accepts the
   minimum; this can be zero. This avoids creating fractional NFT principal or
   charging the owner for the fund's rounding. The selected facility's claim
   age is then reset; an unpaid target is not a guaranteed future entitlement.
4. If the original insurer deposit is invested, request its liquidity before
   taking Buck's lock. Inside the atomic settlement, return the entire original
   *nominal* deposit from the originally recorded Carrying pool, with that
   pool's average carried demurrage. Apply the full-value Jubilee amount to
   extinguish the same number of BUCK units directly from the fund.
5. Apply the insurer-approved replacement terms in place and activate any
   additional coverage. Do not remove or lower the old activated coverage.
   Determine the replacement premium from the resulting current coverage and
   new rate, bound it by the approved maximum, and pay full-value BUCK to the
   insurer's currently registered pool. The owner may fund it from held BUCK,
   the nominal pool refund, and the explicitly authorized additional issuance.
   Apply the funding-factor check before the new issuance supplies any reserve.
6. Record the selected NFT's new principal and reset its age, synchronize the
   capped aggregate, and ensure its final K shortfall is no worse than before:
   =max(0, new principal - K * new current value) <=
    max(0, old principal - K * old current value)=. A failed check reverts the
   sale, refund, issuance, approvals and every other change.

The replacement premium's rounding, any fixed-point floor on fund relief and
its actual effects on value must be tested independently. Other NFTs belonging
to the owner are not implicitly settled. Existing issued principal is never
called back merely because K falls.

* Contract boundaries and validation

- BuckCredit validates the appraisal and regulator envelope using the recorded
  insurer even when called by Buck, and permits rate changes on outstanding
  net-model issuance only through the authorized renewal path. Legacy issuance
  remains unaffected until migration.
- BuckNet validates both digests, current terms, owner, actual principal,
  premium limit, funding factor and K. Only the configured fund may execute
  the settlement. Neither the insurer's proposal alone nor a fund call without
  the owner's authorization suffices.
- The fund sells before Buck is locked and prepares the original pool outside
  that lock. Reentrancy guards and transaction rollback cover both calls.
- Test 0 and 50-year relief, multiple facilities and insurer pools, K cuts,
  repricing, fresh issuance, fractional rounding, expired/cancelled/stale
  proposals, failed pool liquidity, insufficient funding, cap violations,
  malicious callbacks and the identity/attestation gate. Measure deployed
  bytecode; BuckCredit is close to the EVM size limit.
