Rewrite of =alberta-buck-privacy.org= (first draft 843222d) into an executable, story-led walk through
identity issuance, EOA payments and the three Notes flavours, for prospective users and government
staff.  This file is the resume document: decisions, phases, status.

* Direction (from the author, 2026-09-24)

- Audience: citizens and public servants, sharp but not cryptographers.  Story-like, PG-brief,
  positive: privacy, security, liberty, rule of law, lawful access to evidence.
- Motivate with the problems: payment processors and data brokers harvest nearly every citizen
  payment and its metadata; issued identities leak (IDScan.net, September 2026: 153M+ US and
  Canadian driver's licences, ~1.1M Canadian, scans with photos, exfiltrated for over a year and
  sold on the "Nexus" dark-web service); criminals buy privacy while ordinary citizens are watched
  from every direction.
- B1's public issuer is normally a bank or credit union.  The draft's "Bob's public note desk"
  wrongly made Bob public.
- Before each flow figure: an executable code fragment showing the data gathered in that phase,
  where each value comes from, and the artifact it produces, with a visualisation where it helps
  (rendered note, receipt, proof inputs/outputs).  Add library helpers where the code would
  otherwise bury the concept.
- Refine the payload renderings (notes, deliveries, envelopes, receipts) while here.

* Decisions

1. *Cast.*  Bob (private citizen, payer), Carol (private citizen, payee), Mallory (outside
   observer), Alberta's identity issuer, and *Aspen Mutual Credit Union* (fictional; a public
   identity; Bob's financial institution).
2. *Four ways to pay Carol 100 BUCK*, each suited to a situation:
   - EOA transfer: two people who have exchanged accounts.  Visible edge, private names.
   - B1: Bob buys a credit-union bearer note ("cash") and hands it over.  Chain shows Aspen
     Mutual minting and later a pseudonymous account cashing one of its notes; Bob never appears.
     The credit union learns who cashed it (depositor envelope), as a bank does.
   - A1: Aspen Mutual draft payable to Carol (addressed; interception-proof; no mint handle at
     spend).  Bob still never appears on chain.
   - A2: Bob writes his own private cheque; only Carol learns who wrote it.
   Bob pays the credit union by an ordinary identity-checked EOA transfer (he is its customer).
3. *Executable, fixture-backed.*  Python, one org-babel session, live anvil (as the receipt paper).
   Sigma proofs, envelopes, deliveries and registrations are produced live; the Groth16 proofs
   (mint, spend, folds, B1 membership) come from pre-generated *privacy fixture worlds* built from
   the same inputs by the fixture pipeline, with their prover wall times.
4. *Privacy fixture worlds* (=alberta_buck/test/vectors/privacy/{b1,a1,a2}.json=): the e2e world
   builder gains a cast parameter.  Issuers: Aspen Mutual (b1, a1, public), Bob (a2, private).
   Depositor: Carol.  Accounts derive from Ethereum keys (no vanity addresses).  Each party's
   account secrets are stored so the paper registers the same keys through EOA =register=.
   The existing e2e worlds stay byte-identical.
5. *Library helpers.*
   - =alberta_buck/sim/observer.py=: decode a transaction and its logs into what an outside
     observer sees (Mallory's column, from the actual calldata and events).
   - =alberta_buck/wallet/cards.py=: payload "anatomy" cards.  Each field carries its source and
     its protection: public, sealed-for-<reader>, holder-secret, proven.  Text and PNG drivers.
     Cards for identity record, credential, registration package, approve envelope, bearer note,
     addressed delivery, spend package; receipts keep =render.py=.
   - NotesStack: fixture set selection, stored party secrets, EOA register / identity-approve /
     transfer with real proofs, public-issuer binding.
6. *Figures.*  Keep =scripts/render_privacy_figures.py= and its style; rewrite =figures.json= for
   the new cast and story.
7. *Mutable particulars (author's question, 2026-09-24).*  Settled so far (accumulator spec 8):
   the record's =epoch= is first certification and M is stable for life; validity is live-set
   membership; categorical mutable facts (region, age band) re-associate into per-value subtrees
   under fresh salts.  Missing: a schema for the record itself, whose test instances carry a
   document number, issue date and current names, and a home for free-form mutable details
   (legal name, street address, phone, photo) that per-value subtrees cannot hold.  Decision:
   - The *core record* (M's preimage) holds only facts fixed at first certification: registry,
     a registry person number never reused, date of birth, name at first certification, epoch of
     first certification.  Existing test records are read this way; no vector churn.
   - *Particulars certificates*: versioned, registry-signed statements over M of the current
     free-form details, each field committed with its own salt (SD-JWT style), so the holder
     discloses only the fields a counterparty needs.  An update issues a new version; M, the
     registration, receiving leaves, issued notes and past receipts are untouched.  A superseded
     version remains a true historical statement, which is what a receipt of that date wants.
   - Implementation now: =alberta_buck/registry/particulars.py= (issue, disclose, verify) with a
     v2 domain tag and a card; the spec gains a section.  Later: carrying a disclosed particulars
     subset in AB-RCPT receipts (additive).

* Findings

- The in-note issuer pair =(sigma_R, sigma_s)= hashed into B1 and A1 idHash has no defined
  message and no verifier; every fixture fills it at random.  The batch Schnorr, checked on
  chain, is what authenticates a public issuer.  The paper claims only the latter.
- =Issuer.revoke='s docstring still describes epoch-based renewal, superseded by accumulator
  spec 8 (validity is live-set membership).
- test_receipt_e2e's live test skips A1/A2 receipt checks with a stale comment; the offline
  test and test_privacy_world verify all three flavours, both roles.

* Built (P1, P2)

- One unified world, not three: one chain, one identity tree (Bob's receiving and naming
  leaves; Carol's receiving, mailbox and naming leaves, each its own salt), one note tree with
  Aspen Mutual's batches of four (leaves 0-3, 4-7; the story's notes at 2 and 6) and Bob's A2
  cheque (leaf 8).  Carol cashes B1 and A1 into her everyday account and A2 into savings.
- =scripts/snark/gen_privacy_world.py= + =gen_privacy_fixtures.sh= (=make
  nix-snark-privacy-fixtures=, ~70 s); =prove_spend.js= takes an optional leaves file.
- =alberta_buck/sim/cast.py=, =privacy_world.py= (PrivacyWorld, PrivacyChain, fixture()
  adapter for receipts), =observer.py=; NotesStack takes an external issuer (default path's
  draws unchanged).  The whole story runs on anvil in ~2 s.
