They are not yet the only path a deployment can take, and they are not yet what the canonical receipt or the delivered A2 payload checks.                                                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
The three prototype scripts pass: 11, 9, and 3. I re-ran the flow document's wallet session against the current API. Mint, decryption, both folded witnesses, the theft refusal, the A1 receipt, and the B1 issuer receipt all succeed. A fresh timing of the local proving keys, on this machine, is about 16 seconds and a 1.9 GB key for an addressed spend, and about 21 seconds and 287 MB for a B1 spend.         
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
What the branch actually enforces                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
Addressed notes are keyed to a receiving key derived from the wallet seed, not from the identity scalar. That substitution is the same in Python, Rust, and both bindings, and the committed wallet vectors match. A1 encrypts the recipient's identity under that key. A2 encrypts the issuer's identity under it, and the anti-framing proof uses the same key. Encrypting the identity under itself still identifies 
the recipient with one multiplication. Under the receiving key that scan finds nothing, and the identity scalar does not open the ciphertext.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
The spend gate matches the reason the key had to move. A1 and A2 are one Groth16 proof each, deposit_fold_a1 (3,306,390 constraints, 43 public inputs) and deposit_fold_a2 (3,341,261 constraints, 42 public inputs), both at aggregator depth 20. The leaf is Poseidon(m, k, salt). A2's fifth relation is a second association of the issuer, an ordinary salted identity leaf, so the salt that names the issuer is  
not the salt of the issuer's mailbox. When depositFoldVerifier is set, DepositFoldVerifierAdapter derives every public input and reads the depositor's registered account from the registry. test/NotesE2E.t.sol spends real proofs that way. A stolen receiving key is refused on the leaf relation in the Python reference.                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
B1 did not fold, and should not have. It still encrypts the depositor's identity under the issuer's registered account key. The depositor is not given the issuer's secret, so that product stays variable-base. The membership circuit is identity_membership_b1 (491,764 constraints, 9 public inputs). Its blind is a multiple of H_PEDERSEN, hashed to the curve. The same point is what the sigma commits and what 
the contract passes into the verifier. A certified identity record does not decrypt a B1 ciphertext.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
Where writing the papers against the code failed                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
These are the gaps. The papers now say them, instead of describing the old coupling sigma as the shipped gate.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
• The sound gate is optional. spendCoupledA1 and spendCoupledA2 use the fold only when depositFoldVerifier is set. The constructor leaves it unset, and governance can clear it. The fallback is verifyDepositCoupling, which still decrypts under the identity scalar. NotesCoupledA1 and NotesCoupledA2 test that fallback with stub verifiers. B1 always uses whatever contract sits in the membership slot. The     
repaired adapter and the old depth-10 G1-tie adapter implement the same interface.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
• The canonical receipt still uses the identity scalar. make_receipt and make_receipt_a1 decrypt under the receiving key. wallet/verify_receipt.py and the Rust twin still require decryption under m_rec for note-a1 and note-a2. test_receipt_e2e.py skips both flavours and says that rework is pending. The unilateral receipt also checks unsalted point membership, not the receiving leaf, and it does not carry 
a proof that the receiving key belongs to the named identity.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           
• The payer's binding evidence hands over the mailbox. prove_receiving_binding returns k in the clear. There is no hiding circuit for it. A payer who checks that evidence can read later notes addressed to the same key.                                                                                                                                                                                              
• An A2 recipient cannot build the fold from the serialized payload. The mint object carries r' and the issuer's naming salt. notePayload in the end-to-end fixture is only the two ciphertexts. The generator keeps the other two values in memory while it proves.                                                                                                                                                    
• The addressed spend event logs an unchecked point. SpentCoupledA1 and SpentCoupledA2 still emit dc.P_I. The fold does not read dc.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
• register does not admit a receiving leaf. It still refuses a caller-supplied leaf. The circuit checks a path to whatever root governance posted.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
• The legacy A2 note-binding witness builder does not match the renamed circuit. make_note_binding_witness still multiplies by the identity scalar and emits the signal name m_rec. The circuit input is k_recv. The folded spend does not call that builder. The fallback path's separate note-binding proof does.                                                                                                     
• Proofs Part IV does not cover this. Theorems 7 and 8 still state encryption under the identity point and soundness of the coupling sigma.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
scripts/gen_unilateral_a1_vectors.py and scripts/gen_unilateral_a2_vectors.py called insert_receiving with points. The leaf function requires scalars, so those scripts could not run. Both calls now pass m and k. I did not regenerate their JSON. Those scripts still build depth-10 trees, and the circuits are depth 20.

Decisions the architecture still leaves open                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
1. One receiving key, or one per payer? The code has one key per wallet, rotated with the salt counter. Because the only payer-assurance helper discloses k, every payer who checks it learns the mailbox. A hiding proof would keep one key. A key per payer would bound a leak. Which of those is the requirement?                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
2. Should an addressed spend with no fold verifier revert? The architecture treats the fold as the gate. The contract treats it as an optional upgrade and keeps the old sigma as the default.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
3. Guardian recovery. Is a wrapped copy part of the note payload, or a wallet convention outside it, and who holds the shares?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
4. The A1 value ciphertext. It is still encrypted under the receiving key. The spend publishes the face. Should that ciphertext stay?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
5. The security argument. Theorems 7 and 8 are now the wrong statement. Should Part IV of alberta-buck-proofs.org be rewritten next, or is the gap recorded in the notes papers enough until that pass?
