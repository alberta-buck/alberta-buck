* Accounting to transplant, and what can disappear

Let =Q= be =1e36= fractional units per six-decimal raw BUCK, =Y= be 365.25 days,
=T= be 50 years, =S= be outstanding issued principal, and =D(dt)= be the tested
=NetDemurrageMath.decay= factor: continuously applied nominal 2% per year.
Do not change to a different annual-compounding convention during this port.

** Wallets: replace exposure history with unsigned net value

An ordinary unsponsored account needs one current net balance and a checkpoint,
not a signed balance or the integral of its holding history:

#+begin_example
N(now) = decay(N(checkpoint), elapsed)
balanceOf(ordinary) = floor(N(now) / Q)
#+end_example

An incoming BUCK is cash even if its recipient owes principal on an NFT.
Transfers never issue credit or automatically repay that NFT. Mint explicitly
issues BUCK, including unspent BUCK, and burn explicitly extinguishes it.
Unissued capacity is a distinct credit view and is excluded from balanceOf.
A K change affects capacity and settlement eligibility, not existing cash.

The BuckNet ledger's separate received =held= and issued =unspent= components
can be combined for token accounting: the financial checks in the quoter,
operations and swap guard use their sum. NFTs already retain all debt and
age information. Preserve origin-specific reporting, if useful, in events or
client bookkeeping rather than assigning different economic rights to
fungible cash. Adapt readers to an exact scaled net-balance view; do not
return invented held/unspent labels. Combining two separately rounded decays
can differ by one internal scaled unit per checkpoint; specify and test that
bound instead of promising identical rounding.

The old hot-path packing need not be abandoned. A candidate representation
is =uint200 netScaled=, =uint40 timestamp=, =uint16 flags= in one slot:
the existing account cap times Q fits in 199 bits. Carrying nominal value
needs additional storage, and the fund needs wider aggregate accounting.
Retain existing amount caps initially; never rely on a narrowing cast to check
them. This is a representation proposal, not a tested storage migration.
The signed BuckQty wrapper still used by credit/legacy code can be cleaned up
separately; the corrected token must not use a signed wallet balance.
Sponsorship requires additional funding state: do not count its protected
balance independently from the same money in its payer's funding pool.

For a Carrying service, retain nominal gross =G= alongside net =N=. Time
reduces N without reducing G. Transferring nominal =x= moves the proportional
net value, using one explicitly specified rounding rule; the recipient's mode
controls whether its nominal or net value is exposed by ERC-20 balanceOf.
Keep full-precision receipt evidence as well as nominal Transfer events.
Ordinary cash, Carrying nominal balances and NFT debt must never be collapsed
into one number. Synchronize registry mode changes before changing their
interpretation, and implement the selected neutral-service authorization.

** Jubilee cash and relief are separate computations

For an event-free interval with constant issued supply, reuse the tested fund
checkpoint, in scaled units:

#+begin_example
J_net(now) = S*Q - decay(S*Q - J_net(checkpoint), elapsed)
J_gross(now) = J_gross(checkpoint) + floor(S*Q * elapsed / T)
#+end_example

Checkpoint before changing S or transferring value into/out of fund cash.
Gross accrual includes self-demurrage; it is not another source of spendable
net BUCK. Deployed BUCK still pay demurrage in their actual holding accounts;
a basket position's mark is an asset claim, not additional token supply.
Reuse the full-width fund-flow counters and distinguish current cash from
lifetime incoming fees, investments, proceeds and paid relief.

Per-NFT issuance-seconds *remain necessary*. Each facility records principal
=P_i= and capped age =A_i <= P_i*T=. New issuance changes its weighted age;
partial retirement removes proportional age; ordinary transfers change
neither. Relief targets 2% of the *full issued principal* per year, even if
the issued BUCK have been spent or are left unspent in the wallet.

Reuse =JubileeClaims=/=JubileeClaimBook= for the exact aggregate capped age,
including deferred matured debt. A single integral of total supply cannot
substitute for the sum of separately capped ages after facilities mature.
Keep bounded maturity processing and explicit backlog refusal; do not
simplify the denominator by silently counting excess age.

For a selected reduction of principal p, use the selected capped age's share
of net fund cash plus the guarded wheel mark, capped at p. Baseline 2% is a
target; marked returns and losses determine actual relief. Normal reduction
and renewal use already-liquid full-value BUCK, with the existing operation
limit and shared block budget. A successful ordinary burn roughly costs the
owner =p - actual relief - net insurer refund=; exact rounding, coverage/K
constraints and the requested balance change determine the actual allocation.

At average issuance age 50 years, lien release remains available even if
cash is insufficient: Jubilee assumes the unburned principal, which stays in
supply until actually burned. The original insurer's nominal refund remains
an independently payable claim. Retain atomic in-place renewal, explicit
owner/insurer consent, premium-pool isolation and no worse K shortfall.

** Responsibilities and transition invariants

| Block | Preconditions | Postconditions |
|-------+---------------+----------------|
| Checkpoint / transfer | Correct verified identities and receipts; authorized Carrying mode; sufficient sender net or nominal balance; safe time/amount range. | Money moves with its correct net/nominal value; cash never goes negative; neither NFT principal nor total issued supply changes; demurrage is counted once. |
| Mint / per-facility issue | NFT ownership and insurer consent; present-value/K capacity; exact premium terms and recorded pool; one pre-mint funding reserve for the whole batch. | Issued principal, supply, cash and premium funding increase consistently; each NFT and aggregate claim book synchronize; mint(N) increases balanceOf by at least N or all effects revert. |
| Burn / reduction | Owner-selected or documented default NFT order; exact current claim, pool refund, K and guarded mark; prepared insurer liquidity; current and shared Jubilee cash limits. | Owner net plus fund net extinguish exact principal; nominal refund comes from the original pool; coverage/age/supply decrease consistently; burn(N) achieves at least N of actual balance decrease or everything reverts. |
| Renewal / maturity | Correct facility and consent snapshot; normal renewal funding or the independent 50-year release eligibility. | Renewal crystallizes actual relief and updates one facility atomically; maturity releases the lien, retains unburned supply as fund debt, and preserves unpaid premium refund rights. |
| Sponsored fees | Two-party consent; verified ordinary accounts; no self-sponsorship/chains; actual payer funding, with projected spendability specified. | Sponsorship changes who bears the fee without creating net BUCK, double-counting payer money or changing NFT relief. Exhaustion, cancellation, fan-out and settlement order have explicit tested behavior. |
| Wheel investment / redemption | Existing wheel authority; one receipt; bounded work, guarded prices, nonzero meaningful execution bounds and actual liquidity. | Position, cash, shares and basis reconcile; actual proceeds determine liquidity; no position sale, swap or keeper reward executes under Buck's monetary lock. |

The conservation target is net BUCK across actual holding accounts equaling
issued supply within a proved rounding bound, and
=S = sum(open NFT principal) + deferred Jubilee principal=. A basket receipt
is not counted as BUCK a second time. Nominal Carrying balances must not be
summed as economic supply. New fund accrual must not be reconciled using the
old positive/negative balance and feesRealized/reliefRealized identities.

** Sponsorship clarification and smallest accounting candidate

The user confirmed that sponsorship offloads fees *completely* while funded;
a declining balance with occasional reimbursement is not the requested
behavior. At insufficient funding, settleDemurrage may break the connection,
clear the flag/link and return the account to normal fee-bearing operation.
The proposed shortfall policy is to use the sponsor's available contribution,
charge uncovered fees against the sponsored balance and detach. Confirmation
of that final shortfall policy is pending; detaching must not forgive fees or
create unbacked cash.

For a funded group, a useful candidate is one payer funding balance A and a
sum B of protected sponsored balances. Between group mutations, A compounds
down; sponsored balances stay fixed, and the payer's uncommitted spendable
cash is A minus B. This accounts for pending sponsored costs without reading
every member or promising the same free payer cash to several accounts.
The group must account for *both* payer and protected cash, counted once in
system net holdings. It adds shared sponsorship state, not signed NFT debt
or wallet holding-seconds.

When A falls below B, full protection is no longer funded. Specify how the
remaining assets and uncovered fees are allocated across members before
unlinking; a funded-case formula alone does not solve that failure. Prove
membership/transfer updates, multiple payers' independence, payer withdrawals,
accrued-cost views, cancellation, inherited Carrying fees and same-block
settlement order. A transfer that discovers underfunding must never spend
the unfunded promise. If an oversized transfer reverts, its attempted
detachment also reverts; an independent settleDemurrage call must allow the
account to resolve the linkage before retrying.

retaining the source foundation does not solve the allocation problem.
The prototype's sampled maximum 1,275,392 burn gas is not a worst-case bound.
