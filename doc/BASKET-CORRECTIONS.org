* Baseline and experiment order

=818db7d= preserves the pair observer, basket policy, market comparisons and
exact opportunity audit before these corrections. Historical cached runs
retain their original flags and interpretation.

The new progression enables the basket immediately after linking the pools.
Only then does K move, followed by the desk and persistent buyer/seller arms.
The fundamental basket arm has fixed K and no desk or private flow. Later
arms retain the basket. The full selector matrix remains 891 visible cases,
now sharing 279 distinct active configurations instead of 351.

The [[file:control-lab/basket-first/00-isolated/results.org][isolated baseline]] compares the old EMA response and the original pair
response for 120 clean days. Assertions verify fixed K, zero desk actions,
zero private trades, identical initial books/feeds, and agreement between
proposed pair legs and receipts. Removing the other actors does not cure
the defects: the pair response makes 46 transfers and 11 wrong-side pair
legs, and fails the repeat-cycle check.

* Conditions to verify

| Condition | Discretionary paired action |
|-----------+-----------------------------|
| Missing/warming route or reference | No trade |
| Seller relatively cheap, ratio already crossed its reference | No reversion sale |
| Significant separation, steady/increasing outward motion | Quench |
| Significant separation, departure braking | Small bounded release |
| Significant separation, reliable closure | Stronger release |
| At least one material allocation gap, both legs improve toward targets | Consider transfer; counter-leg need not also cross its band |
| Both allocations inside the trigger band | No discretionary transfer |
| Recipient would worsen its allocation | Choose another recipient or explicit cash/risk work |
| Shared donor or recipient capacity exceeded | Cap jointly |
| Executable amount below minimum | Wait; do not manufacture repeated budgets |
| Same-time repeat, stale evidence, or reversal | No fresh turnover from call count |
| Planned recipient refuses | Roll back every leg |
| Hard liquidity/credit constraint | Separate risk override, visible in trace |

Relative prices must be normalized to accepted historical references, never
the first observed price. A trace is not a promise of mean reversion after
permanent news. These tests check the stated trading behavior, accounting
and matched outcomes; terminal equity is not liquidation ROI.

* Reproduction

=make control-lab-basket-first= runs the clean case with a common 120-day
mean-price LP prelude followed by 120 live days. Set
=CONTROL_LAB_BASKET_FIRST_WARMUP_DAYS=0= to retain the cold-start experiment.
The allocation band remains 200 bp; override
=CONTROL_LAB_BASKET_FIRST_BAND_BP= for explicit sensitivity experiments.
Select noise profiles
with =CONTROL_LAB_BASKET_FIRST_PROFILES= and keep each confirmed revision in
its own =CONTROL_LAB_BASKET_FIRST_OUT= directory. The =-render= target uses
cached JSON only. Each correction is committed after its tests and runs.

* Correction 1: orient reversion around accepted relative history

The policy now receives explicit historical whole-TOKEN log-price means at
deployment. The lab derives them from the configured means, excluding live
perturbations. Filtered displacement and both live quote routes must place
the donor above its counter-TOKEN's relative reference. Live pool checks
also catch a crossing earlier in the same block while the rate still lags.

An explicit 100-tick spread floor (about 1.005%) excludes equality and very
small relative movements. This is an experimental margin above the two
0.3% pool fees in this lab, not a claim of positive realized profit or a
universal setting for other venues. Direction/sizing risk overrides remain
separate from discretionary reversion transfers.

Six policy tests plus inherited observer tests pass (16 tests in that suite),
including accelerating departure, braking, stronger closure, wrong-side
history, sub-minimum separation, same-block live crossing, own impact and
elapsed-budget authority. The [[file:control-lab/basket-first/01-relative-direction/results.org][120-day clean confirmation]] eliminates all
11 wrong-side pair legs. Transfers repeat: 41, 40, 38, 41 across four cycles.
However, the mature allocation remains asymmetric: A is only bought, B is
only sold. Mean allocation error rises to 4.224 points. This confirms the
direction correction, not a completed rebalancing strategy. Next test the
independent two-sided band and band-edge sizing defect.

* Correction 2: separate the trigger band from capacity to target

One material allocation error now opens consideration of a paired transfer.
Both legs must improve their own allocations, but the counterpart need not
also exceed its band. Size uses the actual distances to target, subject to
the same time, depth, step, shared-budget and minimum-size limits. Two
inside-band allocations still cannot authorize a discretionary swap.

All nine response tests pass, including the two one-sided material-gap
cases, refusal rollback, no fresh draw, no overweight recipient, quenched
intent and preserved elapsed allowance. The [[file:control-lab/basket-first/02-allocation-capacity/results.org][120-day clean confirmation]] has
200 transfers (45, 60, 48, 47), zero wrong-side legs, and 481 of 481 response
traces. Every proposed leg appears in the swap receipts. Mean allocation
error improves from 4.224 to 4.020 points, but A remains persistently
underweight. This is confirmation of usable counterpart capacity, not
confirmation of symmetric cycle harvesting. Inventory and independent LP
placement need the next isolated examination.

* Correction 3: place owned inventory by allocation shortage

The opening trace shows B receiving LP funding repeatedly before pair
signals are ready: its weight rises above 42% while C stays near 27%.
The legacy LP choice compares capped step sizes and can veto an existing
TOKEN's LP placement with its falling-price EMA signal. This concentrates
the new book in the larger eligible leg.

Independent LP placement now chooses the largest shortage against the
funded target allocation. It retains inventory, credit, liquidity, step and
depth limits, makes no TOKEN swap and does not consume the pair execution
clock. A TOKEN turn is no longer required to pair inventory already owned.
The legacy risk response remains a separate override.

All 12 focused response tests pass, including 40 balanced bootstrap steps
and a price-turn veto that cannot block LP placement. In the [[file:control-lab/basket-first/03-balanced-placement/results.org][clean 120-day
confirmation]], mean allocation error falls from 4.020 to 1.718 points and
wrong-side paired legs remain zero. It is still not the final cycle result.
The trace coverage check also exposes three missing responses (days 28,
34.75 and 111.25); read-only replay reports a revert. The report lists them
explicitly instead of counting them as absent opportunities. Investigate
the minimum-size boundary next, then test the allocation-band compatibility
with the smaller price sensitivity of funded LP positions.

* Correction 4: minimum requests must leave executable proceeds

A deterministic response test reproduces =pair cash unavailable=: a nominal
request only 0.1% above the minimum cannot survive the venue's 0.3% sale fee.
The planner now reserves room for allowed venue-price deviation on input
and output before accepting each requested leg. Execution retains its actual
proceeds, minimum-size and credit checks; nothing is forced through a failed
bound. Small opportunities wait with their elapsed allowance intact.

All 13 focused response tests pass. The [[file:control-lab/basket-first/04-executable-minimum/results.org][120-day confirmation]] has all 481
response traces and zero wrong-side legs. The three missing responses
disappear. Note a diagnostic limitation in correction 3: pyrevm rejects an
=eth_call= sender with contract code, so its saved empty revert messages
cannot establish the individual failures' reasons. The focused failing test
and the complete corrected run establish the minimum-budget defect and its
correction; future missing-response probes must avoid that RPC limitation.

* Confirmation 5: separate bootstrap from recurring cycle behavior

The [[file:control-lab/basket-first/05-one-point-band/results.org][one-point-band sensitivity run]] does not repair the cold-start asymmetry.
It raises transfer count but does not make every TOKEN trade both ways in
each cycle. The production default therefore remains two points. Merely
lowering a threshold is not the answer to that startup result.

The [[file:control-lab/basket-first/06-settled-book/results.org][settled-book comparison]] instead prepares both arms identically through
the corrected production LP placement at known mean prices for 120 days.
This prelude excludes all selected live perturbations, keeps K and private
actors idle, and allows the initial deposit-flow reserve estimate to settle.
Both arms then run 120 live days with identical feeds and opening books.
Opening weights are 33.36%, 33.33%, 33.31%; equity is 1,002,936.95 BUCK and
lien 712,549.39 BUCK. Prelude costs/flows are part of that disclosed opening
book, not credited to the live strategy's return.

With the original two-point band, the pair response executes 26, 31, 35, 35
transfers across four cycles. Every TOKEN is bought and sold in every cycle,
including all three mature cycles. There are 133 paired legs, zero wrong-side
legs and all 481 response traces. Every planned leg improves both weights,
has at least one material gap, stays within its shared capacities and appears
in the actual receipts. Twenty legs have a closing-side error envelope; 113
use the smaller cessation/braking release. This is primarily bounded
participation in slowing departure, consistent with the stated objective.

Mean allocation error is 1.436 points versus 1.448 for the matched legacy
response. Terminal marked equity is 1,013,727.68 USD versus 1,009,772.74 USD;
the pair arm has slightly less lien. These marks do not establish net ROI
after liquidation or caller gas. The cold-start runs remain important:
their first cycles are dominated by changing LP composition, and they do
not pass the same repeat-cycle criterion. The settled baseline isolates
recurring rebalancing; it does not certify arbitrary startup conditions.

The lab now exposes both the band and common prelude explicitly. Missing
response diagnostics replay through a temporary EOA wheel inside a discarded
EVM snapshot, rather than using a contract-code transaction sender. Python
causal checks were updated for the new stage order: the K-without-actuators
comparison is monitor versus monitor-K, since the later K stage includes
the basket. All 24 control-lab Python tests and 397 basket Solidity tests pass.

* Confirmation 6: noisy cycles and noisy flat prices

The [[file:control-lab/basket-first/current/index.html][combined cached presentation]] selects the clean case and three
noise cases, all with the same two-point band and settled opening book.
The [[file:control-lab/basket-first/07-noise/results.org][first noise campaign]] uses seed 41404; the [[file:control-lab/basket-first/08-noise-second-seed/results.org][second-seed check]] uses 41405.
Noise is independent per-TOKEN feed noise at each six-hour sample, with
standard deviations 20 or 80 bp of baseline price. Random walk remains
zero in these experiments. These are not historical commodity traces.

| Case | Seed | Paired transfers | Every TOKEN both ways each mature cycle | Wrong-side legs | Missing response traces |
|------+------+------------------+----------------------------------------+-----------------+-------------------------|
| Clean | 41404 | 127 | Yes | 0 | 0 |
| Cycle + 20 bp noise | 41404 | 121 | Yes | 0 | 0 |
| Cycle + 80 bp noise | 41404 | 93 | Yes | 0 | 0 |
| Cycle + 80 bp noise | 41405 | 89 | Yes | 0 | 0 |
| Flat + 80 bp noise | 41404 | 0 | Not applicable | 0 | 0 |
| Flat + 80 bp noise | 41405 | 0 | Not applicable | 0 | 0 |

For seed 41404, closing-side error envelopes back 20 clean legs, 16 with
20 bp noise, and three with 80 bp noise. Remaining executed legs are the
smaller cessation/braking releases. Stronger noise reduces that stronger
evidence rather than manufacturing confident closure. Both flat runs stay
inside the allocation trigger band; their zero paired turnover verifies
the combined gate, not the price estimator's accuracy by itself.

The second noisy-cycle seed has mean allocation error 1.452 points versus
1.446 for legacy. The first seed is slightly better than legacy. These
small differences do not justify tuning to the seed or claiming uniform
allocation superiority. There is no clear tested trading defect in these
noise cases, so no additional policy change is made in response to them.

All six pair runs have 481/481 response traces. Planned capacities, readiness,
intent, both allocation directions and actual swap receipts are checked.
The diagnostic EOA-wheel probe was also exercised separately and restored
the wheel address, observation publication, execution clock, account and
inventory when its EVM snapshot was discarded.

* Correction: restore the opening phase after LP bootstrap

The mean-price LP prelude left all three TOKEN/BUCK pools at their means.
At live day zero only the external TOKEN/USDC route was driven to the
selected phases. Arbitrage therefore spent the opening days repairing an
artificial discrepancy. That is a simulation initialization defect, not
evidence of slow signal detection or an intended arbitrage opportunity.

After the prelude, the driver now sets both routes to the first selected
live feed using funded swaps, and lets their TWAP windows settle before
activating observers or actors. Clean opening prices are 100, 110.392305
and 89.607695. Noise or an explicit day-zero disturbance also belongs in
the opening quote; it remains excluded from historical EMA/reference means.
The bootstrap JSON records the opening target, actual prices and settled
window separately from the original mean-price LP prelude.

The prelude now has its own observer. The live pair estimator starts with
an endpoint at the correct opening phase and warms up from live intervals;
120 days at an artificial constant price cannot seed its rate estimate.
Regression checks cover both routes, no spurious day-zero arbitrage in
the isolated basket, unchanged historical anchors, and two-day readiness.

The original interactive walkthrough now uses the same production pair
response from stage 5 onward. All arms share the LP prelude, preserving
the comparison when an actuator is enabled. Pair signals are available
inside “Inspect sensor quality…”, beside the original EMA diagnostics.
The 891 selector combinations resolve to 279 actual 120-day EVM runs.

Earlier tables above retain their historical results; the regenerated
=current= report records the corrected opening conditions.

The corrected 120-day pair runs make 133 clean transfers, 127 at 20 bp
noise, and 94 at 80 bp noise. Every TOKEN still trades both directions in
each mature cycle; all three runs have zero wrong-side paired legs and
481/481 response traces. Flat prices with 80 bp noise produce zero paired
transfers. All eight comparison runs start with exact selected prices on
both routes and zero day-zero arbitrage cycles. The clean transfer counts
per 30-day cycle are now 32, 31, 35 and 35.

In the integrated mixed profile with no random walk, the desk remains idle:
it begins with TOKEN inventory but no BUCK cash, and the expensive-BUCK
excursion lasts at most 19 consecutive days outside the entry band. Fresh
issuance requires 30 days under the existing policy. Browser marker checks
therefore use actual recorded desk fills from the selector matrix, rather
than requiring activity in a formerly active legacy case. This integration
does not retune the desk's bands, persistence or funding.

The full interactive campaign completed all 279 distinct 120-day EVM runs
(891 selector combinations) in 46.3 minutes with six workers. The saved-data
audit verifies 134,199 observations, opening prices on both routes, actor
flags, accounting and execution receipts. All nine fixed-K/moving-K replay
comparisons preserve identical market prices. The isolated production
basket agrees with the diagnostic basket on all 481 clean observations:
prices, allocation book, pair signals and proposed transfers match exactly.
The nine basket-only profile/walk combinations all make paired transfers.
Across the entire selector matrix, 14 desk operations occur in the stronger
mixed and shock cases: all are issuance, comprising 42 TOKEN purchases.
No desk absorption is recorded in this grid. The browser checks both actual
fills and the absence of markers for quiet directions; the simulation does
not manufacture the missing side of the response.
