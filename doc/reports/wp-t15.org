* The role, and what it replaces

=doc/ORGANIC-SCALE.org= "The stand before the controller" names the
external-debt retiree first, beside the income channel, because it sets
g: a real borrower refinances a USD mortgage into BUCK credit when the
premium, the funding factor and the discount make it pay, and that
decision over a population IS the supply flow per unit K.  The thin cast
replaced the decision with a formula -- the =endog= debtor's arrival
clock advancing at (K / 0.75) x (1 + 5 (1 - bvib)), capped at 3x -- and
WP-16's g (~50k BUCK/day per unit K) was that formula's.

=ExternalDebtRetireeAgent= (=alberta_buck/sim/household_agents.py=) is
the =BuckCreditDebtorAgent='s household -- the same insured home, USD
mortgage, income, fair ledgers (master fe3083f) and market legs, audited
to the ledger -- with the arrival replaced by a monthly decision taken by
the carry kernel (=alberta_buck/sim/carry.py=):

- *A household from day 0.*  No arrival clock.  Until it joins it lives
  the external life: income, the mortgage payment, and the insurance
  premium paid as a COST, in the real ledger as in the counterfactual.
- *The decision.*  Each month: the most it can retire (K-scaled credit
  capacity, net of the deposit; at most half the pool's USDC), five sizes
  valued, the best taken if its carry repays its one-time costs within
  theta years.
- *Partial refinancing.*  The atomic debtor converts the whole mortgage
  or nothing, so K acted on it as a threshold (LTV <= K).  The retiree
  retires what K lets it draw, keeps the rest external, and tops up when
  K rises: the supply answers K as a flow.
- *The funding gate is real.*  Short of poolPrincipal x fundingFactor it
  saves (buys BUCK) and waits a month.

* Insurance as an outlay (the design owner's instruction)

Master fe3083f made the debtor ledger compare the two paths fairly: both
insure the same asset at the same rate; the external path pays the
premium as it falls due, a cost; the BUCK path deposits POOL_ROI_INV (10)
years' premium on what it draws, an asset the pool returns when the
insurance is dropped, charged only the opportunity of the capital.  The
kernel carries that into the DECISION, so a household refinances on the
same accounting the audit judges it by:

| carry term (/yr)  | value                                        | source                                                       |
|-------------------+----------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------|
| interest          | + usd x rate                                 | the external interest no longer paid                         |
| relief            | + coverage x 2%                               | =BuckCredit.jubileeRelief= accrues on the activated coverage |
| premium           | + face x premium, until it joins              | the external premium stops once BuckCredit insures           |
| deposit_opp       | - deposit x opportunity (the external rate)   | fe3083f: the deposit's only cost is the opportunity          |
| deposit_age       | - deposit x 2%                                | bd792e7: a Carrying pool's refund carries its accrued age back |

The deposit is Buck.sol's: e = POOL_ROI_INV x premium per unit of
coverage, and the limit is K x the activated coverage, so a unit of
coverage adds K - e of spendable.  Drawing B BUCK beyond the headroom
already activated needs coverage C = (B - headroom) / (K - e) and
deposits e C; a top-up inside existing headroom deposits nothing.  K
therefore enters the decision twice: the capacity (what can be drawn at
all) and the coverage per BUCK drawn (a higher K, a smaller deposit).
| risk              | - buck x risk                                 | owing a basket-indexed unit against USD income (assumed)     |

| one-time          | value                                        |
|-------------------+----------------------------------------------|
| execution         | buck - usd: the liability at par beyond the USD it retired |
| fixed             | switching cost (legal, appraisal, discharge), on joining only |
| penalty           | usd x rate x penalty_months / 12, off the renewal date |

Go iff carry > 0 and theta x carry > one-time.  With every term but the
interest zeroed this is the octl debtors' theta law.  With the whole face
activated the deposit is ten years' premium on the face, so the BUCK
path's insurance is the cheaper one below an opportunity rate of
1 / POOL_ROI_INV = 10%, whatever the premium; the relief the coverage
earns (2%/yr) offsets the carried age of the deposit many times over.
=test_carry.py= pins the terms, the theta-law reduction, and a household
that refinances under this accounting and would not under the old one
(the deposit written off as a loss).
