* Findings

1. =uptake_shock= is inert on the catalogue cast.  It adds to
   =FatCreditBorrowerAgent.extra_cap= (experiment.py:492,
   equilibrium_agents.py:1589 / 1682, the only readers), and every
   catalogue arm sets =FatCreditBorrowerAgent = 0=.  Class 6 is therefore
   realized as a population staircase of honest debtors (one every 10
   days for 180 days), each made to arrive at once.  Command: =grep -n
   extra_cap alberta_buck/sim/*.py=; ramp60's =octl= growth.
2. The guard trip is visible only inside the tick of the push.  The
   basket's TWAP over the last 600 s still reads the old price until
   time advances (the swap's oracle observation carries the pre-swap
   tick), so spot != TWAP for readers in the same tick and not after;
   the pusher therefore pushes at the day's LAST tick (the end-of-day
   frame and the loop's cycle see it) and pokes the observer's
   permissionless =refresh()= so the trip is registered whether or not
   a keeper's cycle runs after the push.  The cast can still arb the
