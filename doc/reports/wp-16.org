self-regulating, position integrating, their integrals, with the
controller's own current-sample integral convention at the tick cadence),
LQR by the structure-preserving doubling Riccati iteration in numpy, Bryson
weights on the deploy's own constants (=dk_rail=, =e_max=, =tau_i_days=)
with the price weight calibrated to the sim's Kp, SIMC and margin checks,
the S / V fill-unit mapping, the step-test identification (differential
fits of a stepped cell against a control cell that shares its keyed
history), ten step-test experiments (=kdesign-step-*.toml=), a CLI that
writes the TOMLs, launches the cells detached, fits and solves, and 16
unit tests on a synthetic plant.  Showed: (1) the joint 4-state plant is
NOT stabilizable -- one lever drives both integrals through one flow, so
=k_s (a Ie + e) + k_e s= is conserved and a joint LQR that costs both
integrals has no finite solution (the linear shadow of D7's lever count);
the derivation is therefore regime-wise, the mid-ranging design, with the
joint loop checked for eigenvalues and margins; (2) in the real sim K is a
WEAK lever: a unit of K commands ~50-60k BUCK/day of supply flow (linear
in the step, R2 0.81 / 0.94), the price barely moves (a full-authority 0.5
K shifts bvib by ~0.2%, the cast holds it within the day), and the
undertakings' book on the calm arm is transient (unwound at par within
days), so the position loop's gains that put tau_s inside the stated
range [tau_I, T_p] spend the full rail authority at a 21-24% fill of the
real reserve (~$3.2M of $15.3M) and the S0-equivalent lambda is 21-24
against the 0.5-1 the D4 cells ran.  Next: the loaded-book confirmation
cells (a 60-day grind, K steps at day 150; in flight at hand-off), cycle 1
on these gains with the two-point tau_s axis, and the three decisions
below (the book-based persistence horizon, the pseudo-stabilizer's cap,
the position loop's authority).

* Findings (numbered; each with the command that produced it)

1. *K is a weak, slow, asymmetric lever on the flow.*  A K0 step held
   open-loop from day 150 changes the supply by a RAMP of 49,595 BUCK/day
   per unit K at depth 10 (six steps -0.20 .. +0.20, R2 0.81 through the
   origin) and 59,618 at depth 40 (R2 0.94); cuts and rises differ (55k vs
   44k; 44k vs 75k), and only the -0.20 step (K 0.55) adds an IMMEDIATE
   deleveraging of -6.3M BUCK (the debtors' limits bind; -0.10 and -0.05
   add ~100).  The K channel is the debtors' arrival hazard (k / k_ref,
   =equilibrium_agents.py:2516=) and their K-scaled limits: at +0.20 the
   supply difference is +8k BUCK at day 240 and +2.15M at day 364 (the
   arrivals compound late).  =python -m alberta_buck.sim.kdesign fit=.
2. *The undertakings' book on the calm arm is transient, not integrating.*
   At eps 0.5% the desk chases the daily noise: it absorbs and unwinds at
   par within days (|q| > 1000 BUCK on 12-20% of days; max 50k BUCK; 38
   trades in the control), and the K step leaves it unchanged (Dq at the
   end 0 / -50 / -13 / -29 BUCK; the ramp F_K 0 with R2 0.00); the only
   K-dependence is a STANDING book of +126k BUCK per unit K (R2 0.38) --
   proportional to the flow, self-regulating, 0.8% of the reserve.  So
   the integrating regime D7 defines k_s in ("the active facility absorbs
   F while it holds price, ds/dt ~ F / cap") is not exercised on the none
   arm, and k_s of record is the SUPPLY ramp per unit K over the real
   reserve -- the flow a facility holding price would absorb.  The
   confirmation on a loaded book (a 60-day $8-12M grind ending at the step
   day, no reacquisition: =python -m alberta_buck.sim.kdesign launch
   --grind=, three cells under =build/sim/kdesign/grind/=) was launched at
   hand-off; =python -m alberta_buck.sim.kdesign fit --grind= fits it once
   the =.done= markers exist.
3. *The price reverts within the day.*  In the open-loop control (gains
   off from day 120) the daily error has std 0.70% and lag-1
   autocorrelation -0.53 (lag 2: +0.18): there is no daily-scale reversion
   constant, the cast (the whale's truth-pool re-pins, the arbs, the DM

* Open issues and proposed decisions

1. The book-based persistence horizon.  The level-2 dwell is price-based
   and silenced under D7; a BOOK dwell (|s| above a threshold for N days
   -> outright) is undefined, and it is the horizon the position loop's
   integral weight is scaled to.  Proposed: T_p = 180 d as taken here,
   recorded as a decision, and WP-15's class-5 cells to report the
   book's dwell so the number can be revisited.
2. The pseudo-stabilizer's cap.  =cap_off = target_buck= is not the
   undertakings' reserve (reserve_frac x NAV, ~$15.3M at either depth);
   at "depth 40" it overstates it 4x and V's fill is diluted accordingly.
   Proposed (with decisions 17 / 18): book the cap from the agent's own
   reserve (the UndertakingAgent knows reserve_frac x NAV), one
   stabilizer per class, so sigma_V is the same at both depths and the
   depth axis means D.
3. The position loop's authority.  At this cast's credit elasticity K
   commands ~50k BUCK/day per unit; the derived gains rail at a 21% fill
   and the authority-limited alternative has tau_s ~400 d.  Proposed:
   cycle 1 runs the derived gains AND the two-point axis (x0.5 rails at
   a 10% fill, x2 at 41%), reports rail days and the book's dwell, and
   the design owner decides between (a) more authority for the position
   loop (a wider rail, or =dk_rail= per loop), (b) accepting that K
   cannot mid-range books of the undertakings' size and leaving the
   drain to the facilities' own unwind rules and the local skews (WP-14),
   or (c) a facility mix whose flow per unit K is larger.
4. The loaded-book confirmation.  The three grind cells (control, K
   -0.20, +0.20 at depth 10; a $8-12M grind days 90-150, no
   reacquisition) confirm or replace the supply-ramp k_s with the book's
   own drain rate per unit K.  In flight at hand-off (=build/sim/kdesign/grind/*.done=);
   =python -m alberta_buck.sim.kdesign fit --grind= then =solve --grind=.
   Proposed: the integrator runs them before cycle 1 and, if the book's
   drain per K differs from the supply ramp by more than 2x, regenerates
   the table from the grind fit.
5. The two-integrator redundancy (finding 4).  Proposed: WP-15 adds
   =pid_i= / =pid_qi= drift (their difference's total variation) to the
   K-economy metrics, and the rails' anti-windup is reviewed for the
   case where the two integrators wind in opposite directions with K
   interior.
6. The lambda knee.  The derived S0-equivalent lambda is 21-24; the D4
   cells and the WP-13 smokes ran 0.5-1.  Proposed: cycle 1's S0 design
   tag runs lambda = 1 (as built) AND lambda = 24 (the derived knee) so
   the structural comparison S0 vs S is not a comparison of tunings.
7. The joint LQR as specified is ill-posed (finding 4); the derivation
   of record is regime-wise.  Proposed: CARRY-CONVEXITY.org's "optimal
   control explicitly in the design" paragraph records that the LQR is
   loop-at-a-time (the mid-ranging design), and that a joint law needs
   the band (a hybrid / piecewise design), which is what the cascade with
   anti-windup already is.
